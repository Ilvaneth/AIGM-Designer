## The two corrections

1. **`secret_death_refused` conflicts with `break_dead_rise` and with `contest_living_dead`.** Its first clue is "someone who was seen to die and is seen again". Where the dead rise as a matter of course (the trope break) or a realm of the risen stands next door (the contest), that sight is ordinary and the trail has no first step: the same ground on which the coding tab made `secret_curse_fell_due` conflict with `break_oath_curse` and `secret_dreamed_into_being` with `break_dreams_are_a_place`. `ruin_kingdom_of_the_dead` needs no conflict: its undying kings are the past, and the archetype hides a present person.
2. **`origin_changed_from_outside` states no choice.** The origin table's rule is that a living person's choice stands behind every origin. The row says something changed them; its hook names only the place. Add to the hook who chose: they went to it, or someone brought them to it, a dated act by a person in the history or the roster.
