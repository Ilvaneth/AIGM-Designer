He personally warned them that a client recently asked whether he'd heard descriptions of "an elf and a dragonborn" — confirming in-fiction (via Ilvaneth's Insight, 21) that Corvin is being honest about the deal, but his interest in the relics themselves is personal/collector-obsessive, not purely mercenary — a future risk if trust is overextended. **Session 8, day 51**: the Verrick collection job closed — 265 gp paid out, based on a false account of costs (Deception 17) that hides the party killed the rival dealer who'd originally lowballed the tiara. Pleased with the result but voiced a real warning ("if this comes back on me, it changes things") after catching Kriv dodge a direct question — he doesn't fully trust the account, even though he accepted it. |
