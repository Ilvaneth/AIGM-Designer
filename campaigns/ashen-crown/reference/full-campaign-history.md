Session 19 is not covered here — see `state.md → Continuity Archive` for its summary, already captured during migration.*

## Session 1 (day ~14-18)
Mother Vey hired the party to break into Gallowmere's ossuary before a Concordat convoy arrived. Fennick Orle warned them of a rival fixer (later revealed as Sefwyn Marrow/"the Grey Tally") working the same lead for a Concordat buyer, and passed an ash-king legend. Inside, Ilvaneth correctly read the ward as wanting an offering (grave-ash), not force — the guardian never woke. Found **fragment #1** plus grave goods, and fresh unexplained footprints just outside (never investigated). Told Vey a cover story; she didn't fully believe it but didn't push. Open from here: the fragment's identity, who left the footprints, Vey's quiet suspicion, who betrayed Kriv's house, Ilvaneth's mother's fate.

## Session 2 (day 18-19)
Cleared Sorrel's Hollow (deserter toll gang) in three fights. In the camp assault, **Sergeant Roskel** (Ironclad Compact mercenary) was fought to near-death, dropped his weapon, recognized Kriv's fighting style as Shestendeliath/Ash-Warden, and confirmed **it was the Ironclad Compact's hired swords that brought down House Shestendeliath** — recruited on the spot as the party's first companion. In Thornwick, Hesta Bram confirmed the Concordat had returned to the Vaelthorn crypt weeks after the first dig and left something at the empty socket "with visible reverence" — a hidden Concordat ward stone was found and left untouched. Mother Vey privately confirmed the Concordat has been asking about "an elf and a dragonborn" since the ossuary job — the recipe's origin. Both PCs reached level 3. Roskel sent ahead to Harrowgate to find a corruptible Compact contact (failed, session 3).

## Session 3 (day 19-25)
Ambushed and wiped out a 12-strong Concordat honor-guard convoy (9 skeletons, 3 priests) escorting a warded coffin to Ashvale Necropolis — inside: **fragment #2**, plus a sealed transport manifest (Order No. 447-C) confirming Sarelle Duskbourne's own authority over the shipment (this manifest is the item Ilvaneth later used as a forgery reference, session 21 continuation). First Harrowgate visit: sold loot, laundered stolen horses, Roskel's Compact-contact task failed (DC15, rolled 4). A Draconic treasure map led to the Harpy Crags (cleared). First real contact with **Sefwyn Marrow**, confirmed as the Grey Tally who cased the ossuary — offered a lead on **Magus Orell Tain** (senior Concordat archivist opposing Sarelle's Crown project from within, and — critically — already aware Sarelle began excavating months before the Emperor's death) in exchange for a look at the party's fragment.

## Session 4 (day 25-33)
Cleared Hollowmoor Barrows in full. The outer mounds: 2 skeletons. The deep barrow's inscription (read via Comprehend Languages): the last ash-king assembled the Crown, returned as something wearing his identity but not himself, and was put down by his own household guard, who broke the Crown and stayed to warn others — the campaign's founding lore reveal. **The Warden** (a fallen Ash-Warden guard, reflavored Wight) confronted them; Ilvaneth's honest confession of her real ambition (filling her own inner void) earned genuine alarm from the Warden and Inspiration for honest Neutral Evil play. The Warden fought and died (Kriv's Action Surge finish); its last line: *"...someone else will have to watch, then."* Took **the Barrow-King's Blade** from the sarcophagus; a crest confirmed House Shestendeliath belongs to a wider tradition of Crown-guardian houses. Both PCs reached level 4 (new standing rule set here: level-up mechanics apply at the next long rest, not instantly).

In Harrowgate: met **Corvin Thale** (gnome), who revealed a client had recently asked about "an elf and a dragonborn" — struck a standing deal (first look at future ash-king finds, his silence, his rumor network). Kriv's own Scriptorium research found House Shestendeliath's official attainder record deliberately vague, plus a marginal note about an unnamed "contracted company" payment around the same time (the first paper echo of the Compact's role). Met **Prior Aldric Venn** (named the Ash-Kilns Cinder Choir cell as the region's real threat) and **Renata Kroll** (revealed the Harrowgate Garrison House quietly answers to Ironclad Compact retainer money, same pattern as Gallowmere's Kell; offered to fund a standing force under Kriv's command — deferred, later formally declined session 8).

At **the Weeping Tower**: Harn's bound spirit-guardian challenged them; Ilvaneth's honest "I don't know his specific work, but I know there's something here worth learning" was accepted as more truthful than any fabricated answer. Took Harn's spellbook and a Pearl of Power (his one partial self-preservation success in a lifetime of failure). Session paused at a riverside camp near Gallowmere, day 33, having spotted a Concordat scout patrol searching for something (evidently the destroyed convoy) without being seen.

## Session 5 (day 33-35)
Fixed a settlement-scaling gap in Gallowmere (added an inn and smith). Scouted the Ash-Kilns; Ilvaneth (invisible) spotted **Odric Fenn** performing ritual ash-handling. Kriv confronted him openly; a negotiation (intimidation, then Ilvaneth's honest account of the real Hollowmoor Warden encounter, then the concrete leverage of "Prior Venn wants your cell exposed") secured a deal: Fenn's silence and the Choir's own correspondence, in exchange for never revealing what's sealed in the fourth kiln — a caged ghast, a failed Choir attempt to manufacture a "returner." (Fenn was later killed for becoming a liability, session 17.)

Reading the Choir correspondence (nat 20 Investigation): confirmed Sarelle holds 3 fragments at Ashvale Necropolis (a 4th being the party's own interception), a cryptic reference to "the Prophet's house," and Choir interest in "Ash-Warden bloodlines" tying to Kriv's own house.

Snuck into Gallowmere: confronted **Alis Wend** (still angry about the session-1 con, talked down, given nothing); took a job from Mother Vey (find who talked to the Concordat); confronted **Fennick Orle** directly about selling their description — he admitted it (sold to Sefwyn session 1, to the Concordat ~day 5); found **Sergeant Dallin Marsh** near the river, forgave his debt to Fennick and converted it into personal loyalty — Dallin became a garrison-side asset, instructed to watch Kell. Reported to Vey a version withholding operational details (the forged document, the Fennick deal never mentioned to her). Sent a letter recalling Roskel to Gallowmere.

A goblin band and a toll gang were dispatched/talked past on the road. In Thornwick: **Corran** recognized Ilvaneth's "Lady Serah Ashworth" alias on sight; his private game confirmed ~day 40. **Sister Mave** confirmed Shestendeliath Hold's general location (eastern Thornlands, a day's ride from Harrowgate) when Kriv asked "under cover of research" — she pressed once, he deflected, she let it go. Harrowgate temple archive research: Ash-Warden houses confirmed as a real order sworn to the Ember Crown faith; House Shestendeliath's official attainder record found (no traitor named, the house itself blamed).

Built and cleared **The Widow's Hollow** (a deliberately faction-independent, "no strings" dungeon) — 16 rooms, secured a Mithral Chain Mail (Kriv), 400+ gp, and a real bargain with **the Widow** (green hag): Ilvaneth's genuinely vulnerable confession of her loneliness earned a one-time saving-throw advantage and her hoard opened freely. Found the map to **Kargrim's Reach** in the process, and cleared most of it same session (kobold-vs-bugbear war site) — Groth Half-Ear's warband killed, the vault motto found ("Stone holds; iron answers"), but the vault itself (Room 11), Skree's kobold negotiation, and the basilisk boss were left for session 6.

## Session 6 (day 35-42)
Finished Kargrim's Reach entirely: the Sealed Vault (worked silver, a +1 Dagger, proof Kargrim the dwarven captain deliberately abandoned it), the basilisk (killed without it ever acting), Skree's Warren (negotiation bypassed — Skree and 4 kobolds killed outright). Both resident factions eliminated; the site now sits empty.

In Harrowgate: sold loot, copied Identify (Cole), fully transcribed Harn's spellbook. **Sefwyn Marrow's long-overdue Orell Tain introduction finally fired** — a meeting arranged day 42 at Millward Crossing. The 5-night road there included a second Concordat convoy wipeout (**fragment #3** recovered, forged evidence planted implicating the Ironclad Compact — this forged evidence is what later (session 21 continuation) turned out to have driven Concordat-Compact open conflict). Both PCs reached level 5 (Extra Attack for Kriv; Fireball/Haste for Ilvaneth). Arrived at Millward Crossing disguised, watched Tain and an aide approach at sundown — **the actual conversation had not begun when the session paused.**

## Session 7 (day 47-48)
*Recovered 2026-09-08 — not present in `session-7-conversation.md` (header only) but corroborated by `info/campaign-clock.md`, `info/consequences.md`, `party/state.md`, `info/npcs/vesa-ilkraven.md`, `info/npcs/orell-tain.md`.*

Opened exactly where session 6 paused — Tain and an aide approaching at sundown, Millward Crossing, both PCs disguised. **The Tain meeting resolved as a real trade**: the party gave up the torn Vaelthorn delivery order (carried since session 2), and Tain reciprocated with intelligence on Sarelle Duskbourne's paranoia/operational hardening, the Ash-Warden archive mechanism, and — once Kriv named House Shestendeliath directly — **confirmation the house's attainder had been irregularly fast-tracked**. Tain committed to researching who signed off on it (this research is what eventually produces the session-18 House Velkarune find).

The party then traveled back to Harrowgate in time for Corran's private card game. Ilvaneth played under her "Lady Serah Ashworth" alias; **Vesa Ilkraven** (a new Karsgate socialite attendee) caught the alias as false via Deception but didn't expose it — left an open invitation to find her in Karsgate. No combat this session. Both PCs level 5, full resources.

## Session 8 (day 51)
Resolved Corvin Thale's Verrick collection job (found the Verrick family at the Corley farmstead, killed a rival dealer "Marsh" in an Ostwick alley ambush, delivered goods to Corvin under a successful false accounting). Bought a Wand of Magic Missiles and Ring of Protection. Discovered **Dunnel Ashe** — "the Ostwick wereboar" — is actually a man cursed with lycanthropy 8 years ago defending the village; commissioned a real Remove Curse from Yveth Corrane (~day 58 delivery, later cast by Ilvaneth session 14). Renata Kroll's standing-force offer formally declined. Bought an Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location (500 gp) — directly relevant to the active Concordat search.

## Session 9 (day 51)
**Kriv's first visit to Shestendeliath Hold.** Met the bound garrison — four retainer wights led by **Ser Kethrax**, captain of the gate guard — who recognized Kriv as the returning heir via blood/oath resonance and knelt. The gate ledger named **Vharkoss** (Kriv's uncle) as missing from the watch the night the house fell, and the watch-night page had been physically tampered with afterward. Family records showed every death formally closed except one: an older sister, **Sethra**, whose fate was never recorded. The **Charge-Roll** (read in Draconic) confirmed House Shestendeliath's own charge was the Gallowmere ossuary fragment (the party's first) and named **House Doskarn**, charged with a fragment beneath Karsgate's old ash-mint (later: The Debased Mint).

Read the Warden's Charge's inner band ("what sleeps in the blood wakes only for true cause") — resonated with Kriv's ascension symptoms, unexplained at the time. Took Wardensteel (unattuned). Vault yielded the Warden's Oathplate, ~800 gp, and Lady Isvara's wedding ring (given to Ilvaneth). The great hall held three wraiths (a real fight, no damage taken in the final tuning). A three-phase restoration plan was worked out with Kethrax: physical repairs, living staff via a nearby hamlet named **Greyholt**, legal recognition via Tain's ongoing attainder research.

## Session 10 (day 51-56)
Kriv set the bound garrison to clearing debris; scouted Greyholt (too early to approach). En route to Harrowgate, found a dead Concordat courier's warning about duergar activity near Kesh Deeps (delivered to smith Borgha Kresk, who now gives real prices). Liquidated old gear (including deliberately melting the Shestendeliath family treasury pieces to remove the identifying house sigil, rather than selling intact). Sent a courier recalling Roskel to Harrowgate.

The 4-night road to Kesh Deeps: a harpy fight; first real contact with **Kessra Vane**, a freelance relic-hunter who recognized the party on sight against a description she's held since day 28 — became an accepted fencing/business partner (and, unknowingly, a second carrier for the party's own forged Karsgate rumor). Interrogated and killed a lone Concordat archivist, **Nessa Wren** — her body preserved with Gentle Repose for later Animate Dead use. Cleared Kesh Deeps entirely (stealth kill, animated duergar zombie, a Chief + guards); Kriv personally broke through a sealed dig face by hand, revealing an untouched ash-king era memorial chamber naming "Kaelth Ashborn, Record-Keeper, Last of the Third Dynasty" — a real find with no Crown fragment.

## Session 11 — Design/tooling only, no play
No in-fiction events. The table paused to build the dungeon-design taxonomy system and expand several sites — narratively irrelevant except: this is when The Debased Mint (18 rooms, House Doskarn's fragment site) and Ashvale Necropolis (22 rooms, Sarelle survives by design) were first fully built as complete sites, well before the party ever found them in play.

## Session 12 (day 56-57)
En route from Kesh Deeps toward a newly-discovered sealed door lead, killed a second Concordat archivist, **Perrine Oskold** — her notebook showed she was overdue to compare notes with Nessa Wren, the first hard link between the two disappearances (Concordat's "Missing Archivists" thread, 2/3). An unnamed half-orc relic-hunter (later named Orna Threk, session 14) chasing the same lead was talked down and let go, but mentioned someone in Karsgate has been asking pointed questions of local merchants. Entered **The Maker's Undoing** (Cindermoor vault, built by artificer **Marek Stillwright**) — cleared Wing 1 Rooms 1-6, recovering Resonance Keys 1-2 and Journal Fragments 1-2 naming the vault's builder. Paused at Room 7 (2 hidden Mimics), undecided how to approach.

## Session 13 (day 57)
Cleared the rest of The Maker's Undoing. Room 7's two Mimics tested and killed cleanly (Mage Hand probing avoided all risk). A genuine two-Manticore lair (Room 9): one was reduced to 8 HP and successfully **escaped, wounded, into the dungeon — the party chose not to pursue.** Its blood trail led to Room 10's alert alpha pair (a direct, earned consequence of not finishing the wounded survivor) — Fireball plus a nova killed both. Crossed into Wing 2 (Marek's actual workshop). A Gargoyle in Room 11 was ruled to have already noticed the party (loud searching, audible prior combat) — the party was surprised this time, an honestly-ruled self-inflicted consequence. Found Resonance Key 3 + Journal Fragment 3 (all three keys now in hand — the maker's own name, unspoken until then). Long-rested at Room 13's Sanctuary; both PCs reached level 6 (Ilvaneth: Undead Thralls, Misty Step + Tiny Hut added free; Kriv: Great Weapon Master feat). During the rest, Ilvaneth deduced Room 18's puzzle solution from the three journal fragments, and cast Animate Dead on Nessa Wren's and Perrine Oskold's preserved bodies — two zombie thralls raised.

Room 15's second Gargoyle pair: full surprise, zero damage. Room 17's trap was triggered by a sacrificed zombie thrall instead of a PC. Room 18's puzzle solved cleanly (having actually read Marek's story first, exactly as designed). Room 19: found linked communication stones addressed to "Karsgate — the Ledger Trading House, attn: V. Marrow" (an unresolved future echo of Sefwyn Marrow's surname) and a dangerous life-draining prototype shortsword Marek himself refused to deliver. Room 20 (the vault, boss): a reinforced Clay Golem (190 HP) went down in 3 rounds with zero PC damage — but its Berserk trigger turned on the party's own zombie thralls: Zombie A (Nessa Wren) destroyed, Zombie B (Perrine Oskold) survived at a permanently reduced 8 max HP. Loot: a Ring of Mind Shielding (Marek's own masterwork) and gold. **The Maker's Undoing fully cleared, 20 rooms**, except the escaped Manticore A — a standing loose end.

## Session 14 (day 57-65)
Tracked and killed the wounded Manticore A via a side-den ambush (established the standing rule that player-controlled summons share the controller's initiative and roll their own dice). Established a new standing travel routine: Ilvaneth ritual-casts Tiny Hut every night on the road from here on.

3-night road to Harrowgate: a Will-o'-Wisp pair (one killed, one fled); **Orna Threk** (the half-orc relic-hunter, named for the first time) confronted the party directly with 2 hired scouts over the Maker's Undoing — killed outright (Fireball + Kriv's finish), body preserved with Gentle Repose, her own hand-drawn territorial map looted (this map is what later — session 15 — named the Drowned Cathedral and Unquiet Barrow as unclaimed sites); a third, unconnected thug crew tried to shake the party down — 2 killed by Fireball, the third **surrendered and was executed anyway** by Kriv (325 roleplay XP for the honest Neutral Evil cruelty — an early, clean data point for how this table plays these PCs). Arrived Harrowgate day 60.

Downtime: recorded the previously-unwritten Ring of Mind Shielding and a missed Ring of Protection bonus. Collected the commissioned Remove Curse scroll from Yveth Corrane; identified Room 19's items (the V. Marrow-addressed Sending Stones, the dangerous prototype shortsword, a +1 Quarterstaff Ilvaneth took as her primary focus). Reported the cleared Kesh Deeps duergar threat to Borgha Kresk (received a small gift, thread closed). Hired **Ostrig Kelmar** (dwarf mason) for Hold restoration Phase 1 (300 gp, 150 gp deposit) — this is what activated the Hold's restoration as a real tracked project.

Party split, alternating turns: **Kriv's thread** — took Kelmar's crew and food stockpile to the Hold; caught and fixed a real gap live (had Kethrax's undead garrison withdraw to the lower cistern before the mundane work crew arrived, so they wouldn't be terrified); found real evidence en route of a Concordat/Compact skirmish (burned Concordat banners, a Compact-style buckle, no bodies — confirmed the session-6 forged evidence had actually ignited open conflict between the two factions). Reunited with **Roskel** (given a race for the first time in play — half-orc) and **formally named him Steward of Shestendeliath Hold, the authority in Kriv's absence** — Roskel's own words: *"Yirmi yıl bir bayrağın altında hizmet ettim, sonra hiçbir şeyin altında sürüklendim. Şimdi bana bir kale ve bir yetki mi veriyorsunuz? Hayal kırıklığına uğratmam."*

**Ilvaneth's thread** — solo to Ostwick, avoided a deadly 2-Wight encounter via Stealth, left her old quarterstaff as a nature-shrine offering (24-hour boon). Confronted **Maren Ashe** directly about her cursed brother; struck an explicit bargain — "you and your brother will be my subjects, follow my orders" — Maren, uncertain but desperate, agreed for herself and undertook to convince Dunnel. Found Dunnel Ashe, cast Remove Curse on him directly (cured after 8 years); he agreed readily once told his sister was safe. Before leaving Ostwick, robbed the dead courier Marsh's forgotten hidden stash at the Hollow Sheaf inn (230 gp) — resolving a genuinely forgotten planted hook from day 52.

Both threads converged at Shestendeliath Hold, day 65 — the full party, Roskel, Maren Ashe, and Dunnel Ashe reunited, alongside Kelmar's mid-Phase-1 crew and Kethrax's still-hidden garrison. Session ended here at the DM's explicit request (checkpoint only, no full end-session narrative).

## Session 15 (day 65-67)
Ilvaneth deattuned her Sending Stone (no longer needed with Kriv close), re-attuned her Pearl of Power. **Kriv publicly declared himself Lord of Shestendeliath Hold** in front of Roskel, Maren, and Dunnel Ashe, naming Roskel the highest authority in his own absence (a household-only declaration, not yet Phase 3's actual legal recognition). The freed Sending Stone was repurposed into a Kriv↔Roskel pair. That evening, at the household's first dinner, Kriv introduced Ser Kethrax and the bound garrison to Roskel and the Ashes for the first time, then ordered them to stay out of sight until Phase 1 restoration finishes.

Ilvaneth read Orna Threk's territorial map: named **The Drowned Cathedral** (Cindermoor) and **The Unquiet Barrow** (Thornlands) as real, unclaimed sites. Set out for the Barrow — Night 1's travel roll produced a genuinely dangerous fight (1 Ghast + 2 Wight, both PCs caught fully surprised mid-camp), Ilvaneth dropping to 10/45 HP at the low point before it was won.

## Session 16 (day 67-69)
The Unquiet Barrow pursued to near-completion: the outer maze and inner ring explored via landmark navigation, two wandering Xorn killed (one a genuine surprise ambush that nearly dropped Kriv), the four-room puzzle chain solved via accumulated in-fiction clues. At Room 18, sighted the true guardian — a **reinforced Elder Xorn (HP 240, Legendary Resistance/Actions)** — and chose to retreat while under-resourced, resealing the barrow with its real prize still unclaimed. (This is the Elder Xorn later referenced as "seen but not fought" in the current campaign's state.md.)

Return route: a status check-in with Roskel at the Hold (Phase 1 on track); an overnight at Harrowgate's Hanged Man; then a deliberate detour into the Weeping Wood out of curiosity. First contact with **Sylvenna**, the wood's ancient dryad — a real, fulfilled bargain: cleared three fights (a Giant Spider pair, an Ettercap nest that dropped Ilvaneth to 1 HP, and the actual bargain target) in exchange for genuine, corrective lore contradicting all three factions' official founding histories — a real, still-unused leverage asset. Session paused mid-fight in Area 5 (Spider Hollow).

## Session 17 (day 69-81) — a campaign-defining session
Finished the Spider Hollow fight; found real loot (Boots of Elvenkind) in the burned webbing after direct feedback that the Wood's payoff had been lore-only. Traveled to Gallowmere, ambushing and killing **Ysolde Marrenth** — Sarelle's own long-referenced dedicated investigator — with her entire escort, no survivors. Both PCs reached level 7 and activated **Ascension Stage 1** (Kriv: Draconic Awakening; Ilvaneth: The Dead Notice, first two "dreams" triggered here — Ysolde's, then Odric Fenn's).

**Odric Fenn** — the Choir ally since day 33 — warned the party his own knowledge of them was now a liability, and was killed for it on the spot along with his remaining cultists. The fourth kiln's cage was found already broken (confirming the day-60 ghast-escape rumor firsthand). **Alis Wend** was recruited into Kriv's service with several other refugee families, still without learning his real identity.

**The Gallowmere garrison coup**: Sergeant Dallin Marsh turned 20 of Captain Kell's 30 soldiers for 330 gp; the party personally cornered Kell, let him buy his life with his hidden stash, his real Compact contact (**Renwick Tale**), and his own bribery ledger, in exchange for permanently disappearing. The garrison (27 soldiers total, after 6 remaining loyalists were intimidated into swearing loyalty) came directly under Kriv's control. The same night, **Oswin Drask** (a moneylender harassing Dallin) was killed at his warehouse.

**The entire garrison and Alis Wend's civilian recruits were relocated to Shestendeliath Hold** across a 5-night march, arriving day 81. At a great feast, **Ilvaneth publicly named Kriv "Lord Kriv Shestendeliath" before the entire household** — his identity is no longer secret to any of them. Phase 2 of the Hold's restoration restarted with real manpower; Greyholt's approach was deliberately deferred to Kriv's own choosing.

## Session 18 (day 81-87)
**Sefwyn Marrow personally diverted to Harrowgate** to deliver Tain's long-overdue attainder-research letter (fired day 63, sat undelivered 18-20 days — Sefwyn's tracking of the party had gone dormant). The letter: a second Ash-Warden house shares Shestendeliath's exact fast-tracked-attainder irregularity, both processed by the same unidentified Concordat clerk — Tain wants to continue only in person, in Karsgate.

Harrowgate temple research named the second house outright: **House Velkarune** — noted as "closed childless." (This is already reflected in the current campaign's `world.md` as Sarelle's known secret, but had not been connected to the "four houses" pattern surfaced this session — see the correction made 2026-09-08.) A search for Vharkoss's own recorded fate found the relevant archive page torn.

In Thornwick, delivering Corran's long-overdue escort crates led him to organically offer his collateral room — where the party found **the Shestendeliath Signet**, Kriv's first physical proof of identity (a hook resolved after 47 days).

**The Drowned Cathedral** — the campaign's single highest lore-value Chapter 1 site — was fully cleared, all 10 rooms. The Drowned Forge Chamber delivered a key insight: the Crown's reassembly ritual requires a living vessel — both PCs' own ascension stages are a controlled preview of what full assembly would ultimately do, permanently. A Ring of Free Action was recovered (still unattuned as of the current campaign — all 3 of Ilvaneth's slots are full).

## Standing facts this history confirms (cross-checked against the current campaign's hot-path files, 2026-09-08)

- **Roskel**: half-orc, former Ironclad Compact mercenary sergeant, defeated by Kriv at Sorrel's Hollow (session 2) and recruited as the party's first companion. Formally named **Steward of Shestendeliath Hold** by Kriv (session 14, day 63) — "the authority in my absence." The current campaign additionally treats him as "Kalenin Yüzbaşısı" (Castle Captain) — not a contradiction so much as an evolution/refinement not captured in this log (sessions 15-19 aren't fully detailed here); treat "Steward" as his origin title and "Yüzbaşı" as his current-campaign standing, per the table's own 2026-09-08 correction.
- **Ser Kethrax**: captain of the Hold's bound garrison (four retainer wights), recognized Kriv via blood/oath resonance at first contact (session 9). His being Vharkoss's brother is new canon established live in the current campaign (2026-09-07/08) — not present anywhere in this history, and not a contradiction of it.
- **Vharkoss**: named in the Hold's own gate ledger as missing from the watch the night the house fell, tampered records (session 9). Living under an assumed identity in Karsgate (established later, session 20 of the current campaign).
- **Sethra**: Kriv's older sister, fate never formally recorded by the house (session 9) — later established (current campaign) as alive, an Ironclad Compact officer, hunting Vharkoss.
- **House Velkarune**: the second Ash-Warden house sharing Shestendeliath's exact fast-tracked-attainder irregularity (session 18) — "closed childless." This is very likely the second/third name in the "four houses fell the same month" pattern Tain's journal surfaced in the current campaign (2026-09-07 continuation), alongside House Doskarn and House Ostrel.
- **The forged Compact evidence** (session 6, day ~37-42): planted by the party itself at a convoy ambush — this is the actual root cause of the Concordat-Compact open conflict the current campaign has been tracking as a mysterious escalation. The party caused it and does not yet fully realize this in-fiction.
- **Renwick Tale**: Coren Ashvale's real Gallowmere Compact contact, first named session 17 (Kell's bribery confession) — never approached in 18 sessions of this history, still unapproached as of the current campaign (now the source of Gallowmere's quiet post-garrison-move economic takeover).
- **The Elder Xorn** at The Unquiet Barrow (HP 240, Legendary Resistance/Actions) was sighted and deliberately avoided at session 16 — matches the current campaign's "seen but not fought" note exactly.
