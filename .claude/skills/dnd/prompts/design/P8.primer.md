The primer (`design/player-primer.md`, assembled by `render_player.py` from `{{template}}`) is the one bible file fully open to the player: **the world as a native of this culture knows it**, nothing more. You write your culture's **section** to `design/_staging/P8/{{entity_id}}.section.md`, in the primer's voice: the regions, polities and who rules them as this culture sees them; the gods as worshipped here; what everyone knows about magic and its regulator; famous places; the **taught history** as this culture tells it (public layer only); the folklore (a song or a saying the history table planted); what this culture says about the dangerous places. The script adds the shared sections from the registry (the gods' public rows, the calendar and festivals, money and prices, languages and peoples, the taught events, the over-tier sites' far telegraphs, the day-0 rumours, the sockets' questions), so do not repeat tables; write what only a native voice can say.

Rules: public tier only (a discoverable or secret sentence here is a leak; the validator and the leak scan run on the primer); a native of this culture knows less about the other polities; every proper noun English, every sentence Turkish; you may write `[[id]]` links for public entities and the script resolves them to names, but a link to a discoverable or secret entity fails the build.

Fragment `{{fragment_path}}`: `registry` is the section stub (`kind: primer_section`, `file` the section path), `refs` the ids the section names, `counts` (`words`, `sayings`). Also write `design/_staging/P8/{{entity_id}}.facts.json` — the `common-knowledge.json` facts of this culture (`keywords`, `text_tr`, `origin` = this polity's id or `all`, `refs`) — and `design/_staging/P8/{{entity_id}}.news.json` — the day-0 rumours this culture hears (`region`, `settlement`, `visibility` public or rumored, `kind` rumour, `line_tr`, `refs`, `reach`), for the script to merge into `common-knowledge.json` and `news.json`.
