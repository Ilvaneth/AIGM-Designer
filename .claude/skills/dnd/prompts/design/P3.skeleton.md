### What to decide and write
1. **Polities** (`polity_` stubs with `government`, `capital`, `law_level`, a `ruler_at_birth` npc stub reserved with an opaque-free slug, and the `faction` id P4 will create), assigned a naming language in `design/naming.json` `assignments`.

Insets for unreachable regions.
3. **Settlements** by the rolled kinds: ids and names, region, polity, wealth and law from `settlements.yaml` ladders, the rolled fear, problem (a `seed_` stub) and goods, districts (`district_` stubs of the rolled types), the six anchors as `place_` stubs with `kind` and an owner npc stub each, the small-point budget. Villages: name, region, one problem, one npc stub.
4. **`design/map.json`** per `docs/schemas/map.md`: nodes for every settlement, site placeholder you already know (touched planes' sites, the over-tier Act 1 sites' ids reserved with kind and tier so P6 fills them), landmarks and waypoints on the 100×100 grid inside each region's bbox; edges with days on horseback, terrain (Turkish, from the biome's `terrain_tr`), road quality, region; exactly one hub per region; a place reached through a hub is never closer than the hub. The script validates drawn length against days.
5. **Fragments**: one per entity under `design/_staging/P3/` (stubs carry `file: null` and `status: pending` where a later phase writes the prose), plus `design/_staging/P3/skeleton.json` with `roster`, `assignments`, `map` seeds (`graph` nodes and edges for `campaign_graph.py`).
