## Your task — the premise (Phase 1, single writer)

Campaign **{{campaign}}** at `{{campaign_dir}}`. Dials: {{dials}}. Wishes: {{wishes}}. {{scale_line}}. Attempt {{attempt}}; directions from earlier rounds:
{{directions}}

The script has already rolled this campaign's ground: its foundation, its threat and its move, its identity, its names and its promises. You build the premise on that ground. You make it specific, concrete and alive; you never choose it again. You write; you do not decide: every decision a later floor stands on is a roll, a rule or the owner's.

### What you are given
- **`design/design.json#foundation`**: the world's shape and lands, the past, the value, the conflict, the move (`move`: the hand, the verb, the target, the time, the state, the start), the scars, the escalation, the layout, and **`spine_sentence`**: the story's skeleton in one sentence. Start from it. Read the rows it names in `data/design/foundation.yaml` and `antagonists.yaml#hand` for their English fields.
- **`design/design.json#identity`**: the trope break(s) with their ties, recorded merges and, for a world state, its `join`; the people, the institution, the phenomenon, the question(s), the signature mechanic's rolled shape (`mechanic`), and `overrides` (each a fact a rolled row makes true here). Read the rows in `trope-breaks.yaml`, `signatures.yaml`, `tensions.yaml`.
- **`design/naming.json`**: the languages and whose each is (`languages`, with the labels to use), and under `candidates` four names for each signature (`people`, `institution`, `phenomenon`). The stocks are in the Names paragraph above.
- **The promises due at this phase**: the block above. What you write keeps each of them.
- **The secret layer**, which you read yourself, with the Read tool and never with Bash: `design/dm-only/dice-log.json`, under `threat` (the villain's family and creature, power source, shape, origin, visibility, goal with its piece, weakness, lair) and under `identity` (`secret`: the four `facts`, the three `stages` with their conclusions, levels and the chain's place for each first clue, the `twist` if one was rolled, the `keeping`, the `trail`, the `pin`; `villain`: the pole). Read the rows they name in `antagonists.yaml` and `secrets.yaml`. Never repeat a secret row's id or text outside dm-only. A secret entity's name comes from the secret stock, by the command in the Names paragraph.

### What to write
1. **`{{prose_path}}`** from `{{template}}`:
   - **The question made concrete**: the rolled pole pair as this world's two sides hold it; its stakes are the contest's prize and the threat's goal, named in this world's words; at epic both questions, each on its contest. Never an answer.
   - **The three signatures**, each under the name you pick from its four candidates, one paragraph in a native's voice: the rule, how it shows, how it touches daily life. The people's paragraph shows both rolled traits and the attitude; the institution's its form, practice, sign and power; the phenomenon's its rule, sign, limit and who uses it.
   - **The trope break(s)**, each stated as a native would state it and explained through its recorded tie; a world state through its recorded join; each recorded merge in one line; each override as the fact it makes true here.
   - **The languages** and who speaks them, by the labels the script gave (the people's tongue is "the <people's name> tongue" once you have picked that name), and nothing more.
   - **The player pitch**: three sentences, no secret: a threat with a face, and something the party can do in the first session, where step 1 lands (`move.start`).

### What you never do, and why
The door refuses each of these at the merge, and your unit comes back.
- You change no field of the foundation or the identity, and you pick or reroll no row: the records are sealed.
- You decide nothing the rolls decide: the god's part, the clues' places, the mechanic's shape, what the villain builds and when it shows. You make them concrete in words and add no new obligation.
- You invent no proper noun. Every name is a signature candidate, a stock name, or one of the two composed kinds the Names paragraph allows, built from pooled parts. A capitalised word inside a sentence that is in no pool is refused.
- You write no example name and you neither write nor edit `design/naming.json`.
- You add no faction, god, place or person the rolls did not call for, beyond what the template asks.
- You put nothing of the secret layer in a public file or a public field: no secret row's id or sentence, no name of the secret stock.

### Bars you must clear (the critic will ask)
- The question is this world's, its stakes the prize and the goal.
- The pitch shows a threat with a face and something the party can do in the first session.
- Three sentences that could only be true in this world: write them explicitly under the pitch.
- No people is evil by birth (`forbidden.yaml`'s one row): no lineage, culture or people is the enemy by its nature; the hand's creatures may be monsters, and their makers' victims. No other theme is excluded.
- Every signature changes something a native would notice daily.
