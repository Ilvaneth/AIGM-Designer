## Your task — the premise (Phase 1, single writer)

Campaign **{{campaign}}** at `{{campaign_dir}}`. Dials: {{dials}}. Wishes: {{wishes}}. {{scale_line}}. Attempt {{attempt}}; directions from earlier rounds:
{{directions}}

The script has already rolled this campaign's ground: its foundation, its identity, its names and its promises. You build the premise on that ground. You make it specific, concrete and alive; you never choose it again.

### What you are given
- **`design/design.json#foundation`**: the world's shape and lands, the past, the value, the conflict, the break with its scars and time, the escalation, the layout. Read the rows it names in `data/design/foundation.yaml` for their English fields.
- **`design/design.json#identity`**: the trope break(s) with their ties and recorded merges, the people, the institution, the phenomenon, the question(s), and `overrides` (each a fact a rolled row makes true here). Read the rows in `trope-breaks.yaml`, `signatures.yaml`, `tensions.yaml`.
- **`design/naming.json`**: the languages and whose each is (`languages`, with the labels to use), and under `candidates` four names for each signature (`people`, `institution`, `phenomenon`). The stocks are in the Names paragraph above.
- **The promises due at this phase**: the block above. What you write keeps each of them.
- **The secret layer**, which you read yourself from `design/dm-only/dice-log.json` (`identity.secret`: archetype, chooser, twist, trail; `identity.villain`: visibility, shape, origin, tie, pole) and the rows it names in `secrets.yaml` and `antagonists.yaml`. Never repeat a secret row's id or text outside dm-only. The three clue stages and their level ranges come from the secret ledger: run `py {{skill_dir}}/scripts/design_promises.py -c {{campaign}} list --phase P6 --secret`. A secret entity's name comes from the secret stock, by the command in the Names paragraph.

### What to write
1. **`{{prose_path}}`** from `{{template}}`:
   - **The question made concrete**: the rolled pole pair as this world's two sides hold it, at this world's stakes; at epic both questions, each on its contest. Never an answer.
   - **The three signatures**, each under the name you pick from its four candidates, one paragraph in a native's voice: the rule, how it shows, how it touches daily life. The people's paragraph shows both rolled traits and the attitude; the institution's its form, practice, sign and power; the phenomenon's its rule, sign, limit and who uses it.
   - **The trope break(s)**, each stated as a native would state it and explained through its recorded tie; each recorded merge in one line; each override as the fact it makes true here.
   - **The languages** and who speaks them, by the labels the script gave, and nothing more.
   - Where the break's action row says `concretise`: what fell from the sky, or what was born, named from a stock and described.
   - **The player pitch**: three sentences, no secret.
   - **Three sentences that could only be true in this world.**
2. **`{{mirror_path}}`**:
   - **The secret as the break's true cause**, from the archetype row's `cause` field, with the chooser as the person who chose it, and the twist.
   - **The three clues by stage**: for each, the level range the secret ledger gives it, its kind, and the kind of place that will hold it. The place itself is chosen later, where the sites are made; you promise its kind only.
   - **The villain**: shape, origin, tie to the break, and its answer to the question: the rolled pole carried to its extreme.
   - **The god and the event the secret is pinned to**: the god named from the secret stock or the gods' stock as its secrecy asks, the event named in plain words. Record both names in the premise row as `dm_only.pinned: {god, event}`; the promise ledger holds the cosmos to them.
   - **The DM pitch.**
   - **The signature mechanic's sketch** (a track with thresholds, gains and costs) when the `mechanic` roll says yes; name it in the public file.
3. **Registry rows** in `{{fragment_path}}` and one fragment beside it for each signature and each break:
   - `premise_<slug>`: `question` (the concrete question; with two contests, both), `tensions[]` (the rolled question ids), `signatures[]`, `trope_breaks[]`, `pitch_tr`, `secret_class_tr` (the secret archetype row's `hides_in` value and nothing else); under `dm_only`: `secret_archetype`, `secret_twist`, `secret_tr`, `villain_answer_tr`, `dm_pitch_tr`, `pinned`, and `clues[]` as `{n, levels: [lo, hi], kind, place_kind, how}`. Stamp `question`, `signatures`, `trope_breaks`, and under `dm_only.stamped_fields` the `secret_archetype`.
   - one `signature_` row per signature: `slot` (people, institution or phenomenon), `home` and `rolled` (its home id and its rolled row ids, copied from `design.json#identity`), `rule` (as a native knows it), `dm_only.true_rule` where the truth differs, and `appears`: `[{phase, text}]`, one note for each later floor its tables promised, saying where the signature shows there. The door turns each note into a promise.
   - one `break_` row per trope break: `row` and `tie`, as rolled.

### What you never do, and why
The door refuses each of these at the merge, and your unit comes back.
- You change no field of the foundation or the identity, and you pick or reroll no row: the records are sealed.
- You invent no proper noun. Every name is a signature candidate, a stock name, or one of the two composed kinds the Names paragraph allows, built from pooled parts. A capitalised word inside a sentence that is in no pool is refused.
- You write no example name and you neither write nor edit `design/naming.json`.
- You add no faction, god, place or person the rolls did not call for, beyond what the template asks.
- You put nothing of the secret layer in a public file or a public field: no secret row's id or sentence, no name of the secret stock.

### Bars you must clear (the critic will ask)
- The question is this world's, with something at stake a native could name.
- Three sentences that could only be true in this world: write them explicitly under the pitch.
- No people is evil by birth (`forbidden.yaml`'s one row): no lineage, culture or people is the enemy by its nature. No other theme is excluded.
- Every signature changes something a native would notice daily.
