# Ascension Tracks — what the fragments are doing to them

Both PCs are being transformed by the same object, in different directions, at the same rate. **This file holds both tracks together on purpose** — they must never drift apart in power or pacing.

---

## The mechanism

The Ashen Crown is a **container that holds selves**. The Ash-Warden houses were chosen for their dragon blood deliberately — if you need something guarded for four hundred years, you pick a bloodline with long memory, hoarding instinct, and territorial permanence.

So the fragment **recognizes the blood it was made to be guarded by**, and stirs it.

- **Kriv**: the dormant draconic blood of House Shestendeliath wakes. One-way. It does not stop, and it cannot be reversed.
- **Ilvaneth**: the Crown's preservation-of-self pattern begins working on her the way it worked on the ash-kings. Also one-way.

**Both advance on fragment acquisition**, gated by level so plot luck can't outrun the campaign.

## ★ They both know

Neither hides this. They watch each other change and **neither one asks the other to stop.**

They swore in an orphanage never to lie to each other, and they never have. So Kriv tells her his hands don't feel cold anymore and that he can't make himself sell the horses. She tells him she woke up remembering someone else's death, and that she didn't eat yesterday and didn't notice.

Different blood, same thing. That is the horror of this campaign — not a secret, but **two people who love each other, each watching the other become something that will end what they have, and each of them approving.**

# KRIV — Draconic Awakening

Red-line Shestendeliath. Everything below **amplifies what is already on his character sheet** — breath weapon, fire resistance, draconic ancestry. Nothing is added from nowhere.

Size stays **Medium** throughout. Flight arrives at stage 4 and not before.

### Stage 0 — now
Nothing visible. He runs warm. Fires draw his eye. His breath weapon feels *closer* than it used to.

> **Breath weapon note.** Dragonborn breath already scales on its own: 2d6, rising to 3d6 at 6th level, 4d6 at 11th, 5d6 at 16th. Ascension bonuses below are therefore written as **additions to that natural progression**, never as absolute dice — an absolute number would silently grant nothing once his level caught up. (Caught in testing: the original stages 1 and 2 listed 3d6 and 4d6, which he already had at those levels.)

### Stage 1 — The scales *(2 fragments, level 7+)*
- **+1 AC** — scales thicken beneath whatever he's wearing. A flat bonus, stacking with armour; it does not replace his armour calculation
- **Darkvision 60 ft** (new — dragonborn don't have this)
- **Breath weapon +1d6** over natural progression (4d6 at level 7), still 1/rest
- **Cost — hoard-sense.** He cannot make himself sell, gift, or abandon anything he considers his. DC 12 WIS save to voluntarily part with something of value. Play it as roleplay first, dice only if he pushes.

### Stage 2 — The breath deepens *(3 fragments, level 11+)*
- **Fire immunity** (was resistance)
- **Breath weapon +2d6** over natural (6d6 at level 11), **2/rest, 20 ft cone**
- **Darkvision 120 ft**
- **Claws** — unarmed strikes deal 1d6 slashing and count as magical
- **Cost — territory.** He needs a place that is *his*. More than a week away from his holding: disadvantage on WIS saves and mounting agitation. This ties directly into `power-base.md` — his holdings stop being ambition and become need.

### Stage 3 — The presence *(4 fragments, level 15+)*
- **Frightful Presence** — 30 ft, WIS save DC 8 + prof + CHA, frightened 1 min, 1/rest
- **+2 AC** total (replaces the stage 1 bonus, not cumulative with it)
- **Breath weapon +3d6** over natural (7d6 at level 15), **30 ft cone, recharge 5-6 in combat** — this is the real power spike
- **Truesight 30 ft**
- **Cost — scale.** Mortal timeframes stop feeling urgent. He begins making choices that are correct in fifty years and disastrous next month. And the hard one: **Ilvaneth's remaining lifespan begins to feel short to him.** She is an elf. She is actively becoming immortal. It feels short anyway.

### Stage 4 — Wings *(5+ fragments, level 17+)*
- **Flight 60 ft**
- **Breath weapon +4d6** over natural (9d6 at level 17+), **40 ft cone, recharge 5-6** — roughly half an adult dragon's breath, which is the intended feel
- **Truesight 60 ft**
- **Legendary Resistance 1/day**
- **Cost — the hoard doesn't sort.** He is no longer entirely a person, and the hoard instinct has stopped distinguishing between a treasure he will not surrender and **a person he will not lose.** Ilvaneth is in the hoard. He knows it. He tells her.

### Beyond
Full transformation is available as a variant of ending 4 (`chapter-2.md`) — rule by being the largest thing in the world, no crown, no decree, no permission. Not playable. That's the point.

# ILVANETH — The Pattern Holds

Every stage gives her more of exactly what she asked for, and takes a payment she chose to make.

### Stage 0 — now
Advantage on saves against being frightened by undead. Undead hesitate before attacking her. Her dreams have become specific and unwelcome.

### Stage 1 — The dead notice *(2 fragments, level 7+)*
- **Speak with Dead 1/long rest**, no material components — and sometimes it happens unbidden
- **Necrotic resistance**
- Undead of CR ½ or lower will not attack her unless commanded
- **Cost — the dreams.** She dreams as people who died. On a long rest she may wake knowing something she shouldn't. **The DM chooses what she learns; she never does.** Sometimes it is useful. Sometimes it is just a memory of dying.

### Stage 2 — She stops being fully alive *(3 fragments, level 11+)*
- **No need to eat, drink, or breathe** (Trance already covers sleep)
- **Immunity to disease and poison**
- Undead CR 2 or lower won't attack unless commanded; she can **command undead** 1/rest
- **Cost — she stops maintaining.** She **cannot regain hit points from hit dice or short rests.** Only magic or a long rest. She isn't decaying. She just isn't repairing either. She is becoming a preserved thing.

### Stage 3 — The pattern holds *(4 fragments, level 15+)*
- **She stops aging**
- **At 0 HP she does not die.** She falls inert and reanimates after 1 hour at 1 HP. 1/long rest
- **Sees the recently dead and lingering spirits at will**, 30 ft
- **Resistance to cold** (necrotic resistance she already holds from stage 1 — it becomes full immunity at stage 4)
- **Cost — she loses something every time she returns.** A memory, and she doesn't choose which. **Keep a written list at the table.** Start small. Let her watch it grow.

### Stage 4 — Nearly *(5+ fragments, level 17+)*
- **Immunity to necrotic, poison, disease, exhaustion**
- **She cannot be permanently killed by ordinary means** — she reforms in 24 hours unless destroyed by specific measures
- **Command undead at will**, as a 5th-level effect
- **Cost — she is a container now**, like the Crown. Things want to live in her. And the memories she is losing have started coming from one place: **the orphanage.** The thing she and Kriv share is what her ascension is eating.

### Beyond
Endings 1 and 2 (`chapter-2.md`). She gets exactly what she asked for.

---

## The shape of it

Read the two costs side by side:

> **Kriv** is becoming something that will never let her go.
> **Ilvaneth** is losing the memory of why she'd want to stay.

Neither is betraying the other. Neither is lying. Neither will stop.

**Do not force this to a head.** Apply the costs consistently, let both players see the other's list growing, and let them decide what to do about it — including nothing.
