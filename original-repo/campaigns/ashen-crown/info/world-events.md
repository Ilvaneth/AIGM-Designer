# World Events — things that happen with zero party involvement

**Why this exists**: `travel-encounters.md` reacts to where the party *is*. `campaign-clock.md` reacts to *time passing on threads the party already touched*. Neither one generates world/political-scale change independent of the party entirely — this table is that layer. Full design discussion: `world-reactivity-design.md`.

**Cadence — locked, every 7 in-fiction days**: at session start, check `py .claude/scripts/world_events_check.py ashen-crown --check`. If it reports rolls owed, roll that many times and resolve each one before continuing — the marker only advances by `7 × rolls`, so leftover days always carry into the next check (see the script's own docstring).

**Campaign-wide, not regional** — unlike `travel-encounters.md`, this table isn't tied to a specific road or route. These are succession-war/faction-scale events; they land wherever they land, and delivery to the table follows `campaign-clock.md`'s same three-tier rule (walk into it / rumor / a person follows them) — **never announce "while you were away, X happened."**

**Roll it for real**: `py .claude/scripts/roll.py 1d12 --owner dm-table`, then look up the result below. (Not yet wired into `roll.py`'s `--travel` table parser — a plain d12 plus manual lookup is enough for now; extend the script only if this table's use in play shows the manual lookup is actually a friction point.)

---

| d12 | Result |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | **Succession war — territory shifts.** A border skirmish, a bribed garrison, or an outright battle changes who holds a contested region. Pick (or roll) one `Contested` row from `gazetteer.md`'s Region Control table and move it toward one side. Update the table — this must be a real, integrated change (prices, which faction has visible presence, which NPCs are reachable), not a line of flavor text. |
| 2 | **Succession war — an uneasy region flares.** A region already held by one faction has an internal incident (a mutiny, a tax revolt, a raid) that doesn't change control but adds friction — note it in `gazetteer.md`'s Region Control table as a flagged instability, relevant the next time control there is contested. |
| 3 | **Faction-internal — Sarelle vs. Tain advances.** Something moves the Concordat's internal fracture a step (Tain gains an ally, finds a thread of evidence, or Sarelle tightens control) — advance the fracture stage noted in `faction-subplots.md` and update Tain's or Sarelle's "Current knowledge & stance." |
| 4 | **Faction-internal — Coren Ashvale's patience shortens.** Something at Ashvale Necropolis (an accident, a provocation, a cost overrun) makes Coren's grievance sharper — pull the "Compact patience" clock in `campaign-clock.md` earlier, or add a concrete new grievance to `npcs/coren-ashvale.md`. |
| 5 | **Rumor.** Roll directly on `rumors.md`'s d12 pool and deliver it through the next social scene, tavern, or NPC — this entry is a trigger, not separate content. |
| 6 | **Prophecy thread.** A sign, a candidate, or a whispered claim tied to the Cinder Choir's "died and returned" doctrine surfaces somewhere the party could plausibly hear about it. Log it as a Prophecy Thread (see `world-reactivity-design.md`'s original discussion) — a specific claim with a specific, trackable next step, not vague foreboding. |
| 7 | **A regional threat escalates.** One of `regional-threats.md`'s five apex bounties (the Drowned Reeve, the Hollow Reaper, the Moor-Ghost, the White Reaver, the Undercity Cloaker) has been active off-screen long enough to produce a real consequence — a caravan goes missing, a patrol doesn't come back, the bounty-giver raises the reward out of real urgency. Pick the one nearest the party's current activity and make it concrete, not abstract. |
| 8 | **A built site develops past what's written.** Any already-`[full]` location (`gazetteer.md` has no `[sketch]` sites left in Chapter 1) gets a genuine new development beyond its existing content — Kesh Deeps' duergar break through to whatever they were digging toward, Sella Dray moves on Coalback, Kessic Ambrey's unauthorized vault search turns something up. This is a seed for a future session, not necessarily an immediate encounter. |
| 9 | **Other actors — a rival surfaces.** A rival fragment-seeker, bounty hunter, or competing adventuring party appears somewhere relevant. **Build the full NPC template the moment this fires** — Surface, what they want, Current knowledge & stance, Decision logic — never a throwaway sentence with no file behind it, per `.claude/rules/npc-consistency.md`. |
| 10 | **Other actors — someone is asking questions.** Not a confrontation yet — an operative (Grey Tally-adjacent, Concordat, or independent) has been asking around about "an elf and a dragonborn" in a town the party has visited. This is a delivery mechanism for `info/consequences.md`'s Sarelle "Convergence Risk" track specifically, if it's the Concordat asking — check that entry before deciding who's asking. |
| 11 | **Reputation feedback.** The party's growing power base (`power-base.md`) draws real attention — a rival crew eyeing the same territory, a faction quietly reassessing them as more than hired help, an approach from someone who wants what they're building. This is the party's *growth* being noticed, distinct from a specific action they took. |
| 12 | **Convergence.** Roll again on this table. Additionally, check every `pending` or `crossed-unapplied` entry in `info/consequences.md` — if any is closer to firing than it was last check, this is the beat that pushes it, even by a small amount. If nothing applies, this is a quiet stretch: say so, log nothing, move on — not every roll needs to produce a visible event. |
