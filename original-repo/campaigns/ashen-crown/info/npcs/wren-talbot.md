# Wren Talbot

**Location**: Ostwick — The Hollow Sheaf (the village's one tavern)
**Role**: Innkeeper; the villagers' practiced voice for the "boar in the woods" cover story
**Disposition to party**: Neutral — friendly on the surface, first meeting, day 49
**Status in play**: Introduced Session 8, day 49
**Tier**: Minor — runs the tavern, no independent agency over any tracked campaign state; her only real function is gatekeeping Ostwick's shared secret
**Purpose**: Built as the mouthpiece for `locations/ostwick.md`'s cover story — the party's first real test of whether they investigate the wereboar/Dunnel Ashe hook or take the story at face value.

## Surface

A halfling woman in her fifties, sun-cracked hands, hair pulled back tight. Practiced, ready warmth — the kind that comes from years of greeting strangers. Wipes glasses whether they need it or not.

## What she actually wants

To protect Dunnel Ashe and keep Ostwick's business Ostwick's own. She's not lying maliciously — from her side, the "old boar" story is simply true enough for anyone who doesn't need the real truth, and giving strangers the real truth costs a man his safety for nothing she'd get in return.

## What she's hiding

The real nature of "the boar" — Dunnel Ashe, cursed eight years ago defending the village, living in the woods, quietly supported by everyone here. See `npcs/dunnel-ashe.md`, `locations/ostwick.md`.

## Current knowledge & stance

- **As of day 49, updated same day**: told the party the standard cover story; Kriv pressed on the tracks' size, and both PCs read (Insight 17/18) that she was covering for someone, not lying about an animal. Kriv then backed off gracefully rather than pushing further, tipped generously — Wren relaxed, disposition moved to Warming. Freely gave unrelated info since it isn't sensitive to her own secret: the Verrick family's location (the old Corley farmstead) and details on the rival antiques dealer (stayed at her tavern repeatedly, impatient, last seen 2 days ago saying he'd return). Does NOT know the party went on to press a villager and learn Dunnel's identity/family name directly — that happened after they left her tavern.

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect her

- If pressed with genuine care (not threats) and convinced the party means Dunnel no harm → could soften, admit there's more to it, without necessarily naming him yet.
- If threatened or the party makes clear they intend to hunt "the boar" as a monster → hardens, sticks to the story completely, may quietly warn Dunnel's sister at the Ashe Farmstead.
- If the party demonstrates real competence/discretion (e.g., mentions they've dealt with curses or the supernatural before) → more likely to trust them with a partial truth.

## Connections

- `locations/ostwick.md` — the whole village shares this secret; she's just its most practiced voice.
- **Dunnel Ashe** — protects him the same as everyone else, per the village's standing debt to him.
- **The Ashe Farmstead** — Dunnel's sister keeps his old home; Wren would likely send a concerned party there before naming Dunnel directly herself.
