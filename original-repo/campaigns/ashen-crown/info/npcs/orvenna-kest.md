# Orvenna Kest

**Location**: Karsgate — The Wagered Crown (the Ledger)
**Role**: Owner and host of Karsgate's largest casino
**Disposition to party**: Unaware of them specifically
**Status in play**: Not yet introduced
**Tier**: Important — owns Karsgate's largest casino, deliberately positioned as neutral ground where every faction's money and secrets cross her tables; her private ledger is real, growing leverage.
**Class & Level**: Bard 5 (College of Lore) — sharp, theatrical when it serves her, unsentimental in private. AC 13, HP ~38.
**Purpose**: Built as Karsgate's neutral-ground casino owner — tasked with being a cross-faction information/laundering resource the party can use, and a potential power broker if her private ledger ever proves decisive.

## Surface

A dwarf, dressed in more gold thread than is strictly tasteful and entirely aware of it — the opulence is deliberate advertising, not vanity. Booming, theatrical warmth on the floor among her patrons; flat, precise, and entirely unsentimental the moment a door closes behind her. Remembers every regular's face, favorite table, and outstanding balance without ever consulting a ledger in front of them.

## What she actually wants

**A house that never needs to cheat, because the house always wins anyway — and to be the one person in Karsgate every faction owes a favor to.** Orvenna built the Wagered Crown as neutral ground on purpose: nobility, merchants, mercenaries, and criminals all gamble under the same roof, and she extends credit to all of them equally, which means she quietly knows more about who owes what to whom, across every faction in the city, than almost anyone else alive.

## What she's hiding

- **She launders coin for anyone who tips well enough**, no questions asked, through the casino's own cash flow — Ironclad Compact contract fees, Concordat "donations," proceeds from Corla Vint's contracts at the Broken Wheel, all pass through her tables at some point if the right people want it clean.
- **She keeps a private ledger of debts** far more detailed than the official house accounts — genuine leverage on nobles, factors, and officers alike, kept purely as insurance, rarely used and never forgotten.
- **She has no loyalty to any faction** and considers that her actual product — a gambling house that picked a side would stop being neutral ground, and neutral ground is worth more to her than any single faction's favor.

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect her

- If the party gambles honestly and pays their debts → treated exactly like any other paying regular, no special interest either way.
- If the party needs coin laundered or a debt quietly recorded/erased → she'll do it, for a fee proportional to the risk, the same terms anyone else gets.
- If asked directly what she knows about a faction's finances or a specific figure's debts → she trades information the same way Sefwyn Marrow does, for something of comparable value, never for free.
- If the party threatens her neutrality (demanding she take a side, or exposing her laundering to one faction to spite another) → this is the one thing that turns her genuinely hostile; she has real leverage on enough people in the city to make retaliation expensive.
