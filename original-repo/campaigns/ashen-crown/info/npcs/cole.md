# Cole — Vestry & Cole

**Location**: Vestry & Cole, Harrowgate (apothecary and alchemist)
**Role**: Apothecary, alchemist, the only reliable magic-item identifier in the Thornlands
**Disposition to party**: Neutral, returning customers — prior business relationship established before session 6 (exact prior visit not logged in detail)
**Tier**: Minor — confirmed by the DM, 2026-08-29 NPC-tier sweep. A service provider; no independent scheme.
**Purpose**: Built to give the Thornlands region a reliable magic-item identifier (a mechanical gap — someone has to run Identify checks for a fee) — a service provider.

## Surface

Half-elf, looks perpetually somewhere in her thirties (the usual half-elf ambiguity), sharp-eyed and quick-handed. Sleeves rolled to the elbow, fingers stained in a rainbow of dried reagents, a pair of thin wire spectacles pushed up into her hair more often than on her nose. Runs the shop alone now — "Vestry" isn't mentioned unless asked, and she changes the subject when it comes up.

## What she wants

To keep the shop stocked and her reputation as the region's one dependable identifier intact. She's businesslike but not cold — genuinely enjoys a strange find, the rarer the better, and will talk shop at length if someone lets her.

## What she knows / can offer

- **Identification.** The only person in the Thornlands who can reliably identify a magic item — for a fee, scaled to rarity.
- **Potions and components** for sale, standard prices.
- **Monster trophies and curios** — the obvious buyer per Harrowgate's own market notes, values them against CR and actual alchemical use, 50% RAW anchor before haggling.
