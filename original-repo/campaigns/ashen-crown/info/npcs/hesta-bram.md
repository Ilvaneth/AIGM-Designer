# Hesta Bram

**Location**: Thornwick — keeper of "The Log" tavern/inn
**Role**: Innkeeper, unofficial spine of the village
**Disposition to party**: Wary neutral
**Status in play**: Introduced session 2 (unnamed at the table so far)
**Tier**: Minor — confirmed by the DM, 2026-08-29 NPC-tier sweep. Everything she does keeps Thornwick functional day to day; no independent scheme with a completion point that would move tracked campaign state.
**Purpose**: Built as Thornwick's innkeeper and the village's emotional anchor — tasked with gatekeeping information behind the Sorrel's Hollow job and giving the village a human face. Reactive, not a schemer.

## Surface

A dragonborn, heavyset, tattooed forearms (the ink following her scale pattern), sizes up every arrival before they reach the bar. Not hostile, not welcoming. Runs a dice game most evenings and keeps the stakes deliberately low.

## What she actually wants

**Thornwick to still exist next year.** Hesta served eleven years as a soldier and came home to a village that the war hasn't reached yet and probably will. Everything she does is keeping this place functional a little longer: the dice game exists to keep coin circulating inside the village instead of draining out to traders; the inn's prices for locals are a fiction; she talks people out of leaving when leaving would break the village, and talks them into leaving when staying would kill them.

She has no interest in thrones. She has a very great interest in whether armed strangers are about to make her village into a battlefield.

## What she's hiding

- **She watched the excavation.** When Concordat agents cordoned off the Vaelthorn crypt, Hesta went up the hill at night, more than once, and observed. She saw how many there were, roughly what they carried out, and — importantly — that they came back a second time, weeks later, and left something behind rather than taking it. She has told no one, because telling people invites exactly the attention she's trying to avoid.
- She recognized the party as dangerous immediately and is deciding whether they are the kind of dangerous that passes through or the kind that stays.

## What she can offer

- An eyewitness account of the Concordat's crypt operation — the single best source on it, if she's won over
- A read on the surrounding region: roads, who's been through, what's gone quiet
- Rooms, a dice game, and if she decides she likes them, genuine local goodwill

## What changes her attitude

- **Toward the party**: doing something that visibly protects Thornwick. The bandit camp at Sorrel's Hollow is preying on the refugee road and on Thornwick's trade — clearing it would buy her loyalty outright.
- **Against the party**: any hint they intend to bring more armed interest to the village. Threatening a local. She won't fight them — she'll make sure the village closes to them completely.
