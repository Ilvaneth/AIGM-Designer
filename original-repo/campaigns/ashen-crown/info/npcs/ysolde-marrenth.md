# Ysolde Marrenth

**Location**: Mobile — the Thornwick-Gallowmere road specifically, and the Millward Crossing road, working both known ambush sites
**Role**: Ashlord Concordat Inquisitor — Sarelle Duskbourne's dedicated investigator, assigned day 42 to trace the two missing fragment convoys
**Disposition to party**: Hostile once she identifies them — currently doesn't know their names or faces, only "an elf and a dragonborn" (the description Sarelle has held since day ~5)
**Status in play**: **DEAD, day 73 (session 17).** First and only encounter — the party ambushed her convoy on the Thornwick-Gallowmere road (partial surprise, one alert priest). Killed round 1 by Ilvaneth's full Wand of Magic Missiles volley (33 force) followed by Kriv's melee follow-up, before she ever got to act. Her entire escort (2 priests, 6 skeletons) was also destroyed — no witnesses survive. See `party/session-17/session-17-combat-hardened-convoy.md`.
**Tier**: Important — she is Sarelle's own instrument for closing "Convergence Risk" (`consequences.md`); if she survives to report a matching description, that threshold moves directly.
**Purpose**: Built to give Sarelle's previously-unnamed investigator (referenced since day 42, deliberately left undetailed per `missing-systems.md` until actually encountered) a real face — the mechanism by which the party's two prior convoy ambushes could catch up to them.
**Class & Level**: Cleric 8 (War Domain, reflavored Ashlord ash-magic — not in the free SRD, applied from standard published rules). Built on the SRD Priest block (CR 2, 5th-level caster) as a base, scaled up: HP raised to a real margin against this level-6 party's burst, caster level raised to 8th (per `solo_burst_check.py`'s "size HP against the party's actual burst" principle — not a lone boss, but the dangerous individual within a hardened escort, per `two-player-scaling.md`'s "tougher leader/sergeant" group technique).

## Surface

A tiefling, mid-30s, iron-grey hair cropped short, ash-colored horns filed to blunt points (a Concordat mourning custom, not injury). Moves like someone used to being underestimated and correcting that impression quickly. Wears practical half-plate under a plain traveling cloak, not ceremonial robes — she dresses for fieldwork, not doctrine.

## What she actually wants

To close her assignment. Two convoys vanished without a trace on her watch, and Sarelle is not a patient employer — Ysolde's own standing at the Concordat depends on finding who's responsible, or at minimum proving it wasn't incompetence on her end. She is methodical, not zealous — she wants an answer, not a crusade.

## What she's hiding

Nothing from the party (she doesn't know them yet) — but she privately suspects the Ironclad Compact frame (the forged evidence from day 39) is too convenient, too clean. She hasn't voiced this doubt to Sarelle yet; reopening a "closed" line of inquiry costs her credibility if she's wrong.

## Current knowledge & stance

Knows: two convoys lost on this road and the Millward Crossing road, both fragment shipments, both with zero survivors and no alarm triggered — professional work, not banditry. Holds the "elf and dragonborn" description from day ~5 but has never connected it to either ambush site personally. Privately doubts the Compact frame but has no alternative lead yet.

## Decision logic

- If she encounters the party and they fight competently, efficiently, with no wasted motion → this alone won't identify them (professional skill isn't a physical description), but if she survives and later hears *any* description matching "elf and dragonborn" in connection with this fight, the pieces click immediately.
- If captured/interrogated rather than killed → she knows real operational detail (Sarelle's escort patterns, the investigation's actual scope) but nothing about the party's identity or motives — she has no idea who she's hunting.
- If she dies here with no witnesses → the investigation stalls again, a third loss reported to Sarelle, escalating Sarelle's own urgency (see `npcs/sarelle-duskbourne.md`) without ever producing a face.

## What she can offer

Nothing transactional — she's not a merchant or a broker. A captured Ysolde is a real intelligence source on Concordat operations, if the party chooses that route over killing her.
