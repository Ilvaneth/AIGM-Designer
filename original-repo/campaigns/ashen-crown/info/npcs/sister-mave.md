# Sister Mave

**Location**: Thornwick — the old shrine, at the village edge
**Role**: Shrine keeper; holds the village's burial and genealogical records
**Disposition to party**: Warm, open — talks to strangers unprompted
**Status in play**: Introduced session 5
**Tier**: Minor — confirmed by the DM, 2026-08-29 NPC-tier sweep. No agenda, per her own file; a records-keeper, not an independent actor.
**Purpose**: Built to give Thornwick a records-keeper who can hand Kriv his family's general location — a one-time information source with no ongoing agenda of her own.

## Surface

An elf, genuinely old even by elven standards — centuries, not decades. Silver hair kept in a simple braid, hands spotted and steady, weathered from turning brittle pages far more than from any physical labor. Plain grey robes, no faction marking. Nearly the only person in Thornwick who'll speak freely to an outsider without being asked twice.

## What she actually has

The shrine's real value isn't devotional — it's archival. Sister Mave keeps burial records going back generations, including Vaelthorn-era entries: who died, who was buried where, which families held which names before the succession war scattered half of them. She has no idea what most of it is worth to the right person.

She has not yet been asked about Ash-Warden bloodlines specifically — a live thread the party is bringing to her for the first time (session 5), following a lead from Choir correspondence describing the Choir's long interest in "ash-warden soyu" (the ancient dragon-blooded guardian houses, including Kriv's own House Shestendeliath).

## What she can offer

- Genuine research on any Crown-era or pre-war lineage, given time
- Burial and family records for Thornwick and (via older transferred registries) parts of the wider Thornlands
- No hidden agenda, no price beyond genuine interest and patience — she likes being asked

## What changes her attitude

- **Toward the party**: patience with her pace (she's old, thorough, not fast) and genuine curiosity in what she's found — she lights up when someone actually wants to know the history, not just extract a fact.
- **Against the party**: nothing established yet; she has no reason to distrust anyone who asks kindly.

## Connections

- **Vaelthorn crypt** — her records already touched this in session 2's research thread.
- **House Shestendeliath / Ash-Wardens** — a new thread as of session 5; whether her records reach back far enough to help is not yet established, worth a real research scene rather than a single roll.
