# Oswin Drask

**Location**: Gallowmere — the docks, a converted warehouse near the river
**Role**: Dockside moneylender and smuggler-adjacent enforcer
**Disposition to party**: N/A — dead
**Status in play**: Referenced session 5 (Dallin Marsh's file) as an unresolved threat. **First and only encounter, night of day 75/76, session 17** — the party found him counting coin in his warehouse with 3 enforcers, stealthed in undetected (total surprise), and killed all four in 2 rounds with zero damage taken. Dallin's old debt is permanently settled by removing the creditor entirely.
**Tier**: Important — controls a real piece of Gallowmere's dockside underworld (debts, smuggling muscle), a genuine power-base target per his own connections to the docks.
**Purpose**: Built as the rougher, violent counterpart to Fennick Orle's patient debt-collection — enforces with beatings, not patience, and it was his men who bruised Dallin's wrist. Now a real target as the party moves to consolidate dockside power.
**Class & Level**: Thug-tier enforcer, built on the SRD "Bandit Captain" reflavored — CR 2, AC 15 (studded leather + parrying dagger), HP 65. Not a spellcaster; pure muscle and blade.

## Surface

A human man, early fifties, thick-built going to fat, knuckles scarred from a lifetime of collecting the hard way. Missing his left earlobe (an old debt dispute that didn't go his way once). Speaks quietly, which is somehow worse than shouting — people who've heard him raise his voice usually didn't survive the conversation.

## What he actually wants

Money and control of the dockside debt trade — territory Fennick Orle's quieter methods have been slowly encroaching on for years. Drask doesn't do patience; he does leverage, violence, and fear, and he's built a real crew of dockside muscle (3-4 enforcers, always around) doing it.

## What he can offer

Nothing the party particularly needs — he's a target, not a contact, per the party's own decision this session.

## Connections

- **Sergeant Dallin Marsh** — a past debtor, enforced with violence (the bruise Kriv promised to handle). See `npcs/dallin-marsh.md`.
- **The docks** — his own real territory, a `power-base.md` target the party could inherit once he's removed.
