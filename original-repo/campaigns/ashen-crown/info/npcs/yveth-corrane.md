# Magus Yveth Corrane

**Location**: Harrowgate — The Ashgate Conservatory
**Role**: One of three hedge mages (plus a retired battle-caster) running the Conservatory; sells spell components, scrolls, and the occasional spellbook page
**Disposition to party**: Friendly — established customer relationship with Ilvaneth since session 4
**Status in play**: First appeared session 4 (unnamed in the moment, named/raced in the session log as "gnome, per a later race-diversity fix"); properly filed session 8 after being caught with no NPC file despite recurring
**Tier**: Minor — runs a shop, no independent agency over tracked campaign state
**Purpose**: Built as Ilvaneth's recurring arcane-goods contact in Harrowgate — a place to buy scrolls/components and hear arcane gossip, per `locations/harrowgate.md`'s own framing of the Conservatory.

## Surface

A gnome woman, sharp-eyed, ink-stained fingers from constant work with scrolls and components. Practical robes, not ceremonial — she's a working mage, not a showpiece. Direct, unhurried manner.

## What she actually wants

To keep the Conservatory's small, precarious arcane infrastructure funded and running — three hedge mages and a retired battle-caster pooling resources is not a wealthy operation. Reliable, well-paying customers like Ilvaneth matter to her directly.

## Current knowledge & stance

- **As of day 51**: sold Ilvaneth a Web scroll (session 4, 85 gp haggled). No other significant exchanges yet. Views Ilvaneth as a good customer — competent, pays fairly, worth doing business with.

## Decision logic

- If Ilvaneth becomes a regular, reliable customer → more willing to set aside good finds for her specifically, mention rumors from the wider arcane trade.
- If asked about arcane gossip/rumors → shares what a small, resource-strapped magical circle would plausibly hear, not deep faction secrets.

- `locations/harrowgate.md` — the Conservatory itself, "the closest thing to arcane infrastructure in the Thornlands."
