# Corran

**Location**: Thornwick — the trading post on the village square
**Role**: Trader, moneylender, land speculator
**Disposition to party**: Commercially friendly
**Status in play**: Referenced session 2, met in person session 5. Immediately recognized Ilvaneth as "Lady Serah Ashworth" from his game list. Confirmed his private game is in ~6 days (day ~40), location TBD, a new Karsgate attendee expected.
**Tier**: Important — actively buying up Thornwick's land at panic prices; his campaign directly threatens Hesta Bram's fight to keep the village independent and could redraw who effectively owns it.
**Purpose**: Built as Thornwick's trader/land-speculator whose quiet buyout of the village creates a slow-burn conflict with Hesta Bram — tasked with being a fence for loot and eventually forcing a village-ownership confrontation.
**Class & Level**: Not built for combat — "he doesn't fight," per his own file; if robbed or threatened he hires the Sorrel's Hollow deserters (now eliminated by the party) or equivalent muscle rather than defend himself. No meaningful class levels.

## Surface

A gnome, neat, affable, genuinely good at his job. Gives fair prices and says so, and it's true — he doesn't need to cheat on a sale. Everyone in Thornwick does business with him and most of them dislike him.

## What he actually wants

**To own Thornwick when the war ends.** Corran's bet is that the succession will resolve within a few years and land will be worth many times what panic-selling refugees are accepting for it right now. He has been buying farms, houses, and plots from families fleeing north for a fraction of their value, entirely legally, with signed papers. He considers this shrewd. Hesta Bram considers it vulture-work. Both are correct.

## What he's hiding

- **The collateral room.** A locked back room holding goods taken as security against loans that were never going to be repaid: heirlooms, jewelry, a few weapons, a handful of genuinely valuable pieces, and at least one item he doesn't understand and hasn't been able to sell. It is a treasure cache guarded by nothing but a lock and a man who isn't a fighter — and robbing it would be easy, profitable, and would land squarely on people who are already ruined.
- Among the collateral: **a signet or seal from a destroyed noble house**, taken from a refugee family who fled south. A live thread for Kriv — either his own house's, or one destroyed in the same wave of betrayals.

## What he can offer

- Fair prices, buying and selling (the honest option for offloading the ossuary grave goods)
- Small loans, at real interest
- Regional trade news: what roads are safe, what's moving, where the money is going

## What changes his attitude

- **Toward the party**: money and repeat business. He's transactional to the bone.
- **Against the party**: he doesn't fight. If robbed or threatened, he takes losses quietly and then hires someone — and the people available for hire in this region are the Sorrel's Hollow deserters, which would be an ugly and very deserved consequence.

## Move List

Tied to `info/consequences.md`'s "Corran — Buying Up Thornwick" threshold (currently 1/3).

- **At Weight 1 (current)**: ongoing land-buying, legal, quiet.
- **At Weight 2**: he acquires something visibly significant — a building the village actually uses, not just an empty farm. Playable now: Hesta mentions, bitterly, that Corran just bought the old mill or a family's home outright, and there's nothing anyone can do about it.
- **At Weight 3 (crossed)**: he owns enough that his and Hesta's opposing visions for Thornwick's future come to a real head — a confrontation, a village-level decision point the party could plausibly get pulled into.
- **If it stalls**: the slow, legal buyout continues exactly as before.
