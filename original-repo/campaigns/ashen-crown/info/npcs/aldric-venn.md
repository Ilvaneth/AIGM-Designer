# Prior Aldric Venn — The Temple of the Ember Crown

**Location**: The Temple of the Ember Crown, Harrowgate
**Role**: Prior of the orthodox imperial cult (the faith the Cinder Choir splintered from)
**Disposition to party**: Neutral — first meeting session 4
**Status in play**: First face-to-face session 4
**Tier**: Important — his growing frustration at having "no real power to act" could push him toward a real, campaign-affecting move against the Ash-Kilns cell, independent of the party.
**Purpose**: Built to give the orthodox faith's Harrowgate branch a frightened, sympathetic ally-not-obstacle who names the Ash-Kilns threat and offers the temple archive as a research lead — tasked with pointing the party toward the Choir cell and eventually needing their help against it.
**Class & Level**: Cleric 6 (Life Domain) — a devout, protective priest, not a combatant by inclination. AC 13, HP ~44, spell save DC 13.

## Surface

Half-elf, sixties (which reads as vigorous middle age on him), thin in the way of someone who forgets to eat rather than fasts deliberately. Plain vestments, no jewelry beyond a worn ember-crown pendant. Voice is calm by discipline, not by nature — a man holding a lid on something.

## What he actually wants

To keep his faith's actual meaning intact against two threats at once: the Concordat's bureaucratic version of the Crown (ritual legitimacy, archive-managed doctrine) and the Cinder Choir's violent heresy (death-and-return theology, splintered from his own church generations ago and now actively dangerous in his region). He is genuinely devout — this isn't politics to him — and genuinely frightened, because he has no real power to act on what he knows.

## What he knows / can offer

- **The temple archive** holds imperial cult records back to the dynasty's founding, including the orthodox account of what the Ashen Crown actually does — which contradicts the Concordat's official doctrine. Real research value for Ilvaneth's question specifically (what happens to whoever completes it).
- **He knows the Cinder Choir has active cells in the region** (the Ash-Kilns cell among them) and has no means to move against them himself — no soldiers, no authority beyond his own walls.
- **He would be deeply grateful** — archive access, clerical legitimacy, a genuine ally — to anyone who dealt with a Choir cell for him.

## First contact — how to play it

Calm, measured, but the fear underneath is real and shows in small ways (a held breath before certain words, a glance toward the door). He doesn't trust easily, but he's also not naturally suspicious of strangers — he's more likely to be quietly relieved that someone capable walked in. Won't hand over full archive access on a first meeting without some reason to trust them, but will engage honestly about what he knows and wants.

## Move List

Tied to `info/consequences.md`'s "Aldric Venn — Losing Patience with the Choir Threat" threshold (currently 1/3).

- **At Weight 1 (current)**: genuinely frightened, no means to act, has named the threat clearly to the party.
- **At Weight 2**: his fear sharpens into something more active — he starts writing to a higher orthodox authority, or asks the party directly (again, more urgently) to deal with the cell. Playable now: a follow-up message or visit from Venn, less measured than their first meeting.
- **At Weight 3 (crossed)**: he takes a real, self-endangering step on his own — reports what he suspects to someone with actual power (possibly Varic Sennett, possibly someone less careful), setting events in motion the party doesn't control.
- **If it stalls**: he continues privately fearful and publicly calm, exactly as he's been.
