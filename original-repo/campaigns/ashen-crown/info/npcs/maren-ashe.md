# Maren Ashe

**Location**: Ostwick — the Ashe Farmstead
**Role**: Dunnel Ashe's sister; keeps his old home, leaves food at the woods' edge for him
**Disposition to party**: Bound, grateful — agreed to real terms
**Status in play**: Introduced Session 8, day 49. Session 14, day 62: Ilvaneth returned alone, confirmed the promised cure was real, and named her price — Maren and Dunnel leave Ostwick, relocate to Shestendeliath Hold, and serve her. Maren agreed for herself, then convinced Dunnel once the cure was proven. Both departing day 63.
**Tier**: Minor — no independent agency over tracked campaign state; her brother's fate is a personal/tragic thread, not a faction or clock.
**Purpose**: Built as the last real gatekeeper of Ostwick's shared secret — the person with the most personal stake, and the hardest conversation of the three (Wren, the frightened villager, now her).

## Surface

A human woman, looks older than her years, work-worn hands, sharp watchful eyes. Dressed plainly. Carries herself like someone permanently braced for bad news.

## What she actually wants

To protect her brother, full stop — from hunters, from the village's own fear if it ever turned, from anyone who might see him as a monster instead of a person. Eight years of this has made her tired but not soft; she's had this exact conversation in her head a hundred times, dreading the day someone actually came asking.

## What she's hiding

The same truth as everyone else in Ostwick, but she's the one with the most to lose if it comes out wrong. She does NOT know that Dunnel has told no one (not even her) that the curse is getting harder for him to control — see `npcs/dunnel-ashe.md`.

## Current knowledge & stance

- **As of day 49 (first meeting)**: has almost certainly already heard, by the time the party arrives, that strangers have been asking pointed questions about "the boar" and pressured a villager into confirming it's not really an animal — Ostwick is small enough that this news travels fast. Meeting the party already braced, not caught off guard.
- **As of day 62 (session 14)**: Ilvaneth returned alone, reminded her of an earlier promise to look into a cure, and delivered on it — real, undeniable proof (Dunnel, cured, in front of her). The price: both Ashes leave Ostwick and serve Ilvaneth at Shestendeliath Hold. She agreed for herself before Dunnel was even found, then helped bring him around once he saw the proof too. Packing to leave everything she's known for eight years, day 63.

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect her

- If the party is genuinely respectful and makes clear they don't intend to hurt Dunnel → could open up, possibly even arrange a supervised meeting, cautiously.
- If threatened, or if the party frames this as monster-hunting → hardens completely, may try to warn Dunnel to flee deeper into the woods before anyone can find him.
- If offered a real, credible way to help (not just reassurance) → this would matter more to her than anything else, mirroring Dunnel's own Decision logic.

## Connections

- **Dunnel Ashe** — her brother. See `npcs/dunnel-ashe.md`.
- **The village of Ostwick** — shares the secret, has supported the arrangement for eight years.
