# Magus Orell Tain

**Location**: **The Grey Hall, Karsgate** (Concordat chapterhouse, the Kingsward district) — per `locations/karsgate.md`, which already placed him there before this file existed. Sefwyn Marrow's lead (session 3) points toward this same person; the party would need to reach Karsgate to make contact in person, unless Sefwyn arranges something remote first.
**Role**: Senior Concordat archivist, internal dissenter against Sarelle's Crown project
**Disposition to party**: Neutral, first face-to-face meeting in progress (session 6, day 42, Millward Crossing).
**Status in play**: Meeting arranged by Sefwyn Marrow, confirmed session 6. Approaching the party now at the ruined chapel, sundown, at most one aide, unarmed.
**Tier**: Important — a senior Concordat archivist actively losing an internal political fight; his outcome (survives, marginalized, or defects openly) is tracked as its own threshold.
**Class & Level**: Wizard 9 (no combat subclass — a career archivist, not built for a fight). AC 12 (unarmored, elderly), HP ~40, spell save DC 15. Combat with Tain should be avoidable; he's a potential ally, not an obstacle — this stat block exists only so a scene never has to improvise numbers for him.
**Purpose**: Built as the Concordat's internal dissenter — tasked with being a potential ally against Sarelle and the deliverer of the campaign's central irony (he's losing an argument he's right about); the day-42 meeting is his first real scene.

## Surface

A tiefling, elderly, precise, a lifetime spent in archives rather than in the field. Dresses in Concordat formal grey but wears it like a scholar wears a uniform he didn't choose — correctly, without pride in it. Speaks like someone used to being right and increasingly unused to being listened to.

## What he actually wants

**The fragments catalogued and kept apart, permanently.** Not on moral grounds — on scholarly ones. He has read the same historical sources Sarelle has, and his reading is unambiguous: the last ash-king who assembled the Crown did not survive the assembling as himself. What walked away afterward wore his face and answered to his name, and that is not the same thing as saying the ash-king survived.

He is losing this argument inside the Concordat. He is also, quietly, correct. **Tracked as a real threshold now**: `info/consequences.md`'s "Orell Tain — Losing the Internal Argument" (1/3, pending) — and note that file's warning that the party's own anti-Concordat campaign may be accelerating his downfall, not helping him.

## What he doesn't know

**Sarelle began excavating months before the Emperor's death.** The Concordat engineered the succession crisis it now claims to be responding to. Proof exists in the Ashvale archives. If Tain learned this, he would move against Sarelle openly — this is the single most valuable piece of leverage the party can hand him, per Sefwyn Marrow (session 3).

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect him

- **He does not do favors for free.** Any request outside his own stated interest (fragments kept apart, the Ashvale proof, a foothold against Sarelle) needs something concrete in return — access to a fragment for examination, information he doesn't have, or a real gesture of shared alarm about the Crown project. He is losing a political fight and cannot afford charity.
- If asked for help unrelated to fragments/Sarelle (e.g., Compact records, House Shestendeliath) → he has no direct access, but Ash-Warden house collapses touch his own scholarly interest (fragment-guardian lineages) — he might trade a lead for something he wants, not do it gratis.
- If given the Ashvale premeditation proof → moves against Sarelle politically, becomes a much deeper ally.
- If the party betrays his trust or wastes his time → he doesn't retaliate violently (not his style) but simply stops being useful; Sefwyn's bridge doesn't get rebuilt easily twice.
- If he learns the party holds more fragments than he assumed → reassesses their value/danger; may raise what he asks for future cooperation.

## First contact — how to play it

Tain doesn't need to be bribed or threatened — he needs to be given a reason to believe the party is worth listening to, and then given the buried-excavation proof. He will:
- Talk to almost anyone who shares his alarm about the Crown project, including the party, once introduced (Sefwyn Marrow is the current bridge)
- Prefer the party keep their fragment(s) and stay away from Sarelle rather than surrender anything to her
- Not act rashly — he's a scholar losing an internal argument, not a conspirator with an army. Any move he makes against Sarelle will be political/evidentiary, not violent
- Become a real ally if the party brings him the Ashvale proof, or a source of deep Crown lore even if they don't

He should not be run as an obstacle. If a scene threatens to turn into a fight with Tain specifically, that's a sign the scene has gone off the rails from his intended role — de-escalate in the fiction rather than resolve it with initiative.
