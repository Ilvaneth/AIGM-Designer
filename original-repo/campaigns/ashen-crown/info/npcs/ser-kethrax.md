# Ser Kethrax

**Location**: Shestendeliath Hold — the gate hall, though he'll follow Kriv anywhere in the Hold, and possibly beyond it
**Role**: Captain of the gate guard, House Shestendeliath — now bound as a wight
**Disposition to party**: Allied — recognized Kriv as the returning heir, knelt, offered his service
**Status in play**: Introduced session 9, day 51
**Tier**: Important — commands the Hold's other three bound retainers, is the party's only living (unliving) witness to the night the house fell, and is a real companion/garrison-asset candidate per `two-player-scaling.md`'s standing need for this party to get help
**Purpose**: Built as the emotional anchor for Kriv's first visit to the Hold — the one person who can answer "what happened to my parents" from direct memory, and the seed of a garrison companion asset
**Class & Level**: SRD **Wight** (CR 3), captain-tier — HP raised to 60 (from base 45, matching the "tougher sergeant" precedent already used for Roskel/gnoll pack leaders), otherwise standard Wight stats (AC 14, Multiattack longsword/Life Drain, Sunlight Sensitivity)

## Who he is

Dragonborn, was in life — scaled half red-bronze, half now bone where death has taken him. Died defending the gate the night the house fell, still in his captain's harness. Doesn't remember dying, only remembers still being on duty afterward, same as before.

Formal, correct, a soldier's cadence in every sentence. Fifteen years of guarding an empty house didn't make him bitter — it made him patient.

## Current knowledge & stance

- **Day 65, session 15**: formally introduced to Roskel (steward) and Maren and Dunnel Ashe at the household's first dinner in the great hall. Addressed Roskel directly, soldier to soldier — "we do the same work in this house now." Ordered by Kriv to withdraw with the other three retainers and stay out of sight (barring real danger) until the restoration is finished, now that the living household knows of them. Obeyed without hesitation, per his own nature — a direct order from the Lord needs no oath invoked to be followed.
- **Day 81, session 17**: with 27 soldiers and 6 civilians (Alis Wend's group) newly arrived, Kriv narrowed the standing order — Kethrax and the other three retainers now guard only Kriv's own upper-floor chambers, staying completely unseen by the new arrivals. Obeyed immediately.
- Recognized Kriv immediately via blood/oath resonance, not sight — knelt without hesitation, offered service.
- Was fighting elsewhere in the Hold when the gate was actually opened; did not personally see who did it.
- Knows Vharkoss was missing from the second watch shift that night, from the ledger he filled out himself as his last act.
- Saw Lord Threxis die fighting, from a distance (heard him, not witnessed directly). Saw Lady Isvara trying to reach the library, could not save her.
- Did not see Sethra die or escape — genuinely doesn't know her fate, has "always had hope."
- Does not know about the records-tampering visit specifically (that happened after his own death, when the garrison had already settled into its bound state) — if asked, he'd be troubled by it, not have an answer.
- Does not know about the Ash-Warden dragon-blood secret beyond what the oath itself says — he's a retainer, not blood of the house.

## Decision logic

- If Kriv invokes the house oath on him for any reason → obeys without resistance; he's already sworn, this changes nothing for him.
- If Kriv asks him to command the other three retainers on some task → he will, competently; he outranked them in life and still does.
- If the party is ever in real danger at the Hold (the wraith, e.g.) → he will not intervene uninvited (see `encounters/shestendeliath-wraith.md` — bound to the gate, not the great hall) unless Kriv specifically orders him to.
- If Kriv asks him to leave the Hold and follow as a companion → genuinely uncertain how his own binding works away from the site; a real open question, not a guaranteed "no." Worth a scene if it comes up, not an instant yes/no.

## What he offers

- The clearest firsthand account of the night the house fell that exists.
- Command over the other three gate retainers.
- Potential companion/garrison asset — `shestendeliath-hold.md`'s standing note that "the bound retainers will follow the heir, and a two-person party badly needs bodies."
