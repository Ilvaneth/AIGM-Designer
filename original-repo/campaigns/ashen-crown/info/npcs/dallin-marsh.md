# Dallin Marsh

**Location**: Gallowmere — the garrison house, by the north gate
**Role**: Garrison sergeant; now a personal asset of Kriv's
**Disposition to party**: Loyal — genuinely, out of relief and fear both
**Status in play**: Introduced session 5
**Tier**: Important — an embedded intelligence asset inside the Gallowmere garrison; his discovery risk is a real, tracked threat to the party's own information pipeline on Captain Kell.
**Class & Level**: Fighter 3 (no subclass — a career sergeant who never made it further, per his own file). AC 14 (studded leather), HP ~28.
**Purpose**: Built to give the party a real embedded intelligence asset inside Gallowmere's garrison — tasked with feeding Kell's movements and confirming the Compact-retainer secret, at real risk to himself if discovered.

## Surface

A human man in his early forties, greying stubble, dark circles under his eyes from bad sleep and worse debts. Tired rather than hard — a career soldier who never made it past sergeant and knows why. Left wrist carries a fresh bruise from a moneylender's collector.

## What happened

Fennick Orle sent the party to collect an old debt from him. He had nothing to pay with — garrison wages have been late for two months — so he offered information instead. Under Kriv's questioning he revealed:

- **Captain Doreth Kell has outside money.** Private late-night meetings with merchant-looking men, and once, a glimpse of a sealed letter carrying the Ironclad Compact's hammer-and-chain mark, hidden the moment he noticed it. No soldier asks who Kell answers to. This matches the DM-only "discoverable truth" that Kell is on Compact retainer — **now confirmed in play for the first time**, though Dallin has no hard proof, only a consistent pattern.
- **He also owes Oswin Drask**, a rougher dockside moneylender — roughly 8-9 gp, growing with "late fees," enforced with violence (the bruise). Unresolved.

Kriv forgave the Fennick debt in full and told him he'd handle Drask too — converting the debt into personal loyalty rather than closing it as a transaction. Dallin accepted plainly, understanding exactly what it means: he now owes Kriv, not coin, and offered his eyes and ears inside the garrison in return.

**Day 74 (session 17)**: Kriv, still not revealing his real name, offered Dallin 330 gp to personally recruit up to 20 of the garrison's 30 disgruntled soldiers (three months unpaid, morale in the ground) into his own service — regular pay, housing, security. Dallin took the money (30 gp for himself, 300 gp for the men), confirmed persuasion won't be the hard part given how bad morale already is, but flagged the real risk honestly: a same-night mass departure is impossible for Kell to miss. He'll have everyone convinced by tomorrow evening (day 75); the actual departure timing is Kriv's call to make.

**Night of day 75 — the coup.** Gave the order that turned the garrison: 21 of 30 soldiers (himself included) against the 9 still loyal to Kell, while Kriv and Ilvaneth cornered Kell personally. Kell fled rather than dying. Dallin is now the senior loyal figure inside a garrison with no captain — the de facto commander in practice, though nothing has been formalized yet. His own discovery-risk thread (`consequences.md`, "Dallin Marsh — Under Kell's Suspicion") is moot now that Kell is gone — there's no one left to discover the leak.

## Move List

Tied to `info/consequences.md`'s "Dallin Marsh — Under Kell's Suspicion" threshold (currently **2/3**, after the day-74 mass-recruitment plan).

- **At Weight 1**: actively feeding intelligence, undetected so far.
- **At Weight 2 (current)**: the mass-recruitment plan is live — 20 soldiers being quietly persuaded, timing not yet fixed. Playable now: any delay in the actual departure raises real risk of loose talk reaching Kell before the party controls when it happens.
- **At Weight 3 (crossed)**: Kell notices or traces the leak/the recruitment before the party executes it. Real, immediate danger to Dallin and the 20 soldiers, and a real cost to the party if they don't act to protect their asset — this should land as a consequence with teeth, not a shrug.
- **If it stalls**: the 20 stay convinced but undeparted, a standing risk that grows the longer it sits unexecuted.

## What he can offer

- Garrison schedules, patrols, who's on duty when
- Kell's comings and goings, insofar as a sergeant would notice them
- A foothold inside the garrison that didn't exist before — the first real asset toward `power-base.md`'s docks/garrison angle

## What changes his attitude

- **Toward the party**: making good on the promise to deal with Drask. If that promise is dropped, his loyalty likely curdles back into fear and self-preservation.
- **Against the party**: exposing him to Kell, or letting Drask's people hurt him again after being told not to worry.
