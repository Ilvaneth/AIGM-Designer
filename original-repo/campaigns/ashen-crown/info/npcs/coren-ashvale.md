# Warmarshal Coren Ashvale

**Location**: Mobile — wherever the Ironclad Compact's largest current contract is
**Role**: Warmarshal of the Ironclad Compact; effectively its chief executive
**Disposition to party**: Unaware of them
**Status in play**: Not yet introduced
**Tier**: Important — Warmarshal and effective chief executive of the Ironclad Compact; his search for a deniable instrument against the Concordat is the campaign's cleanest bridge from "two thieves with a rock" to a funded, backed party.
**Purpose**: Built as the Ironclad Compact's leader — tasked with eventually offering the party real support against the Concordat at Ashvale Necropolis, and with holding the answer to who destroyed Kriv's house.
**Class & Level**: Fighter 13 (Battle Master) — a Warmarshal who commands through tactics, not just rank. AC 18 (plate), HP ~135, Maneuver DC 17.

## Surface

A dwarf, blunt, professional, refreshingly honest about being a mercenary. Does not pretend the Compact serves anything but its own ledger. Keeps his word to the letter of the contract and not one inch past it — a reputation he has spent decades building because it is worth more than any single betrayal could pay.

## What he actually wants

**The war to continue, profitably, without ever being won.** A resolved succession means standing armies and no more contracts. Coren's strategy is to sell to all three claimant blocs simultaneously, at scale, and ensure no one achieves a decisive advantage. He is, functionally, the reason the Hollow Kingdoms are still bleeding.

## The Ashvale problem

**The necropolis where the Concordat is assembling the Crown fragments carries his family's name.** It is his ancestral burial ground — the Ashvales were minor nobility three generations before they became sellswords, and their dead are still in those vaults.

Coren learned of the Concordat's occupation recently and it has done something to him that money never has: it has made this personal. He has not moved on it yet, because moving on it means open war with the Concordat and that is bad for business. He is looking for a **deniable instrument** — someone unaffiliated who could go into Ashvale Necropolis and cost the Concordat their work, without a Compact banner anywhere near it.

The party is, from his perspective, an extremely attractive candidate. This is the campaign's cleanest bridge between the party's Crown hunt and a faction that can actually arm and fund them.

## What he's hiding

- **The Compact brokered the betrayal of at least one noble house during the early succession maneuvering** — sold the intelligence that got a family destroyed. Which family is not yet fixed. This is the strongest available candidate for **who destroyed Kriv's house**, and Coren would know, and would trade the name for something he wants. (DM: keep this option open; if the table would rather the Choir or the Concordat be responsible, adjust — but the Compact fits the theme best: Kriv's family died as a line item.)
- He is holding Doreth Kell's supply-skimming as leverage rather than punishing it. See `info/npcs/doreth-kell.md`.

## Current knowledge & stance

*Living record — update whenever he learns something new. See `.claude/rules/npc-consistency.md`.*

- **As of session 6**: not yet introduced in play. Doesn't know the party exists. Still looking for his deniable instrument against the Concordat at Ashvale.

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect him

- If approached by an unaffiliated party willing to hit the Concordat at Ashvale → sees them as exactly the deniable instrument he's been looking for; offers real funding/backing, no Compact banner attached.
- If asked directly about who brokered the betrayal of a noble house (Shestendeliath specifically) → he'd know, and would trade the name — but only for something he actually wants, not as a favor. He trades in answers; he doesn't give them away.
- If he learns Kriv is a surviving Shestendeliath heir → this could cut either way: a live heir is either a liability (a name someone might still want dead) or leverage he could use — decide based on how the scene actually plays out, but he wouldn't act against his own profit motive without a reason.

## Move List

Tied to `info/consequences.md`'s "Coren Ashvale — The Ashvale Grievance" threshold (currently 1/3).

- **At Weight 1 (current)**: has made it personal, hasn't moved yet — actively looking for a deniable instrument.
- **At Weight 2**: he starts quietly vetting candidates through intermediaries (never approaching directly himself) — playable now if the party has any Compact-adjacent reputation: a Compact contact makes careful, indirect inquiries about whether the party would take unaffiliated work against "a target near Ashvale," without naming the Concordat outright.
- **At Weight 3 (crossed)**: he makes a direct offer — real funding, real backing, no Compact banner attached, exactly as his own file describes. This is the campaign's clean bridge into Chapter 1's climax if the party wants it.
- **If it stalls**: he keeps looking, increasingly frustrated, more receptive to any credible approach the party makes first.
