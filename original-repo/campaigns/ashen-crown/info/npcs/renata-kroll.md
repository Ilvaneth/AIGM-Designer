# Renata Kroll — Merchants' Assembly Hall

**Location**: Harrowgate, Merchants' Assembly Hall
**Role**: De facto spokesperson for Harrowgate's self-organizing merchant council
**Disposition to party**: Neutral, evaluating — first meeting session 4
**Status in play**: First face-to-face session 4
**Tier**: Important — de facto leader of Harrowgate's merchant self-governance; her standing-force offer directly checks the Compact's quiet control of the Garrison House, a real parallel power-base thread to Gallowmere's.
**Class & Level**: Fighter 5 (no subclass) — carries herself like a soldier, an old unexplained injury, per her own file: a hidden martial past, not just a merchant's bearing. AC 15, HP ~48.
**Purpose**: Built as Harrowgate's merchant spokesperson — tasked with offering Kriv a parallel power-base thread (a standing force) that directly checks the Compact's quiet control of the Garrison House.

## Surface

Dwarf, early fifties, sharp-eyed, dressed as a cloth merchant but carries herself like a soldier — spine straight, weight balanced, always aware of the room. Missing a finger on her left hand, an old injury or punishment she's never explained. Doesn't waste words.

## What she actually wants

To keep Harrowgate functional and prosperous while Karsgate ignores it, and to be the one holding real influence when — not if — the town's informal arrangement becomes something more permanent. She's not power-hungry in a dramatic sense; she's a pragmatist who stepped into a vacuum because someone had to and she was capable. She'll recognize the same instinct in someone else immediately.

## What she can offer

- **A read on the region's actual power structure** — who's really in charge of what, which merchants matter, where the empty magistracy's authority has quietly been absorbed.
- **Legitimacy, eventually** — if the party proves useful and trustworthy, the merchant council could become a real avenue toward formal influence in Harrowgate, parallel to whatever the party builds in Gallowmere.
- **A test, not a handout.** She doesn't give trust for free. Expect her to propose something concrete before offering real access.

## First contact — how to play it

Direct, evaluating, not hostile. She's trying to figure out what the party actually wants and whether they're worth investing in. A show of genuine capability or ambition (not empty noble posturing) will land better with her than titles alone — she respects competence over birth, which makes Kriv's Position of Privilege only a door-opener, not a closer.

- **As of day 51**: the standing-force offer is closed. Kriv declined directly rather than deferring again; she's moving on to find another candidate rather than hold the opportunity open. ## Decision logic

- If the party approaches her again with a genuinely different, concrete offer (not just "we're still deciding") → she'd hear it out; she's pragmatic, not vindictive.
- If she finds and funds another candidate → that person becomes a real fact on the ground in Harrowgate, a new power broker the party didn't create and doesn't control.
- If the Compact ends up formalizing control of the Garrison House before she finds anyone → she'd view that as the worst outcome, and might reconsider approaching the party again out of genuine need, not preference.
