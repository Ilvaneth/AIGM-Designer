# Dunnel Ashe

**Location**: The woods surrounding Ostwick
**Role**: Cursed wereboar, formerly an Ostwick farmhand
**Disposition to party**: Grateful, bound — cured and indentured in the same encounter
**Status in play**: Introduced session 14, day 62 — cured of his lycanthropy by Ilvaneth (Remove Curse) at his sister's insistence, on the condition that he and Maren relocate to Shestendeliath Hold and serve her
**Tier**: Minor — confirmed by the DM, 2026-08-29 NPC-tier sweep. Deliberately self-isolating; his own want is to never act on anyone again, not to move anything in the world.
**Purpose**: Built to give Ostwick a tragic, self-isolating cursed figure for texture and a possible future thread — not an active schemer.

## Surface

A human man, mid-thirties in his own memory, though he hasn't seen himself in a mirror in eight years. Withdrawn, gaunt from a diet of whatever the woods provide, speaks rarely and carefully when he does — every conversation is a risk he's calculating in real time. In boar form: large, scarred, deliberately avoids populated trails.

## What he actually wants

**To never hurt anyone he knows, ever again.** Dunnel took the curse defending Ostwick from a bandit raid he never asked to be a hero for, and withdrew into the woods the moment he understood what he'd become — not out of self-pity, but out of a clear-eyed fear of what an uncontrolled transformation could do to people he'd known his whole life. He has made peace with a smaller, lonelier existence in exchange for certainty that he won't be the thing that hurts Ostwick next.

## What he's hiding

- **He can feel the curse getting harder to control**, slowly, over the years — he hasn't told his sister, because there's nothing she could do except worry, and worry is the one thing he still has the power to spare her.
- **He knows exactly how he could be cured** (a rumor, unconfirmed even to him, of a ritual or a specific rare remedy) but has never pursued it, partly from fear of the cost and partly from a quiet belief he doesn't deserve to ask anyone to risk anything on his behalf.

## Current knowledge & stance

*Living record — update whenever he learns something new. See `.claude/rules/npc-consistency.md`.*

- **As of day 62 (session 14)**: cured, human again for the first time in eight years. Knows Ilvaneth by sight only, not well — she appeared invisible, touched him, and the curse was simply gone. Committed (via Maren, without being consulted on terms beforehand) to relocating to Shestendeliath Hold and serving her — accepted it immediately, given the magnitude of what she'd just done and his sister's own agreement. Resting one night at the farmstead before departing, day 63.

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect him

- If confronted as a monster, with no attempt at conversation → fights defensively, tries to flee rather than press an advantage, per his own fear of what he could do if he actually let go.
- If approached carefully, with genuine curiosity rather than a weapon drawn → willing to talk, cautiously, especially with someone who doesn't flinch at what he is.
- If offered a genuine, credible path to being cured → this is the single fastest way to his trust and gratitude, more than any amount of coin or threat could achieve.
- If his sister or Ostwick itself is ever threatened in front of him → the curse becomes far harder for him to control, not easier — treat this as a real risk, not a convenient plot lever.

## Connections

- **Ostwick** — the village that's protected his secret for eight years out of a debt they consider still open. See `locations/ostwick.md`.
- **His sister** — runs the Ashe Farmstead, leaves food at the woods' edge, has never told an outsider the truth.
