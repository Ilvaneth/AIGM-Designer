# Mother Vey

**Location**: Gallowmere — a cluttered junk shop on the lower street, back room bolted
**Role**: Fence, information broker, the closest thing Gallowmere has to a power that isn't a garrison
**Disposition to party**: Friendly, mildly suspicious since session 1
**Status in play**: Introduced session 1
**Tier**: Important — the closest thing Gallowmere has to a power that isn't the garrison; her read on the party directly determines whether she becomes a real ally or a quiet liability.
**Class & Level**: Rogue 6 (Thief) — a lifetime fence and information broker who could handle herself if genuinely cornered, though she'd rather never need to. AC 14, HP ~40.
**Purpose**: Built as the party's original patron and Gallowmere's shadow power broker — tasked with being the ongoing job-giver and the one NPC whose trust the party has to actively maintain, or lose to a quiet betrayal.

## Surface

A halfling, old, thin, grey hair pinned tight, eyes that price everything including people. Speaks in short sentences. Doesn't waste warmth, doesn't waste threats either. Runs a junk shop nobody browses and a back room everybody knows about.

## What she actually wants

**Gallowmere survives the succession war by belonging to nobody.** Vey has watched three factions circle this town for two years. She takes Concordat coin and Compact coin and plays each one's interest against the other so neither ever gets a foothold. Every job she brokers is, at some level, a move in that game. She sent the party into the ossuary partly for profit and partly because a Concordat convoy arriving to claim something officially would have meant Concordat presence in Gallowmere permanently.

She is not a good person. She will sell out anyone who threatens the balance. But she is genuinely, stubbornly loyal to this ugly little border town in a way that surprises people.

## What she's hiding

- **A daughter.** Years ago Vey sent her daughter away to the capital, into service with a minor noble house, to get her out of a smuggler's life. That house is now aligned with the Ashlord Concordat. Vey has not spoken to her in nine years and has no idea whether she's alive. Anyone who learns this has real leverage on her — and the Concordat may already know.
- **She suspects the party lied** about the ossuary (session 1). She hasn't acted on it. She's waiting to see what they do with whatever they took, because that tells her more than a confession would.

## What she can offer

- Fencing (fair-ish rates, no questions), forged documents at a price, and jobs — see `info/jobs-and-opportunities.md`
- Access to the Gallowmere underworld: names, doors, who's bribable
- Protection, if the party becomes worth protecting

## What changes her attitude

- **Toward the party**: proving they're an asset to Gallowmere's independence, not a lever for a faction. Bringing her something that hurts the Concordat's position specifically.
- **Against the party**: evidence they're working *for* a faction, or that they brought faction attention down on the town. If she decides they're a liability, she doesn't threaten — she just quietly sells them.

## Move List

Tied to `info/consequences.md`'s "Mother Vey — Reading the Party" threshold (currently 2/3).

- **At Weight 2 (current)**: unresolved suspicion about the ossuary cover story, sharpened by the deflected follow-up question. She's watching, not acting.
- **At Weight 3 (crossed)**: she reaches a real verdict — either the party proves themselves an asset to Gallowmere's independence (bringing her something that genuinely hurts a faction's position in town) and she becomes a real, invested ally, or she decides they're a liability and quietly sells them, per her own Decision logic — no threats, no confrontation, just a transaction against them.
- **The party's own choices are the lever**: one more deflected question or one more sign of outside-faction entanglement pushes toward the liability outcome; a genuine gift to her actual interest (hurting the Concordat's foothold specifically) pushes the other way.
