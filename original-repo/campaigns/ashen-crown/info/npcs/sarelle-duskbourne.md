# Archivist-Magus Sarelle Duskbourne

**Location**: Ashvale Necropolis (current seat of operations); formerly the capital archives
**Role**: Effective leader of the Ashlord Concordat's Crown project
**Disposition to party**: Unaware of them specifically — for now
**Status in play**: Named in play session 2 (torn delivery order in the Vaelthorn crypt)
**Tier**: Important — leads the Concordat's entire Crown project; her decisions drive `campaign-clock.md`'s "Sarelle collects" clock and both `consequences.md` thresholds below.
**Class & Level**: Wizard 13 (School of Necromancy) — the same subclass Ilvaneth carries, deliberately: Sarelle is a version of what Ilvaneth's ambition could become. AC 15 (Mage Armor), HP ~78, spell save DC 18.
**Purpose**: Built as the Concordat's de facto leader and the campaign's central antagonist — tasked with eventually offering the party its biggest temptation (recruitment into her Crown project) once she identifies them.

## Surface

An elf, precise, unhurried, entirely without theatrics. Dresses like a records clerk, not a magus. Treats necromancy as archival work: the dead are documents, and documents are to be read, catalogued, and filed. Never raises her voice. Has people killed the way she'd correct a mis-shelved book.

## What she actually wants

**Legitimacy, made permanent.** Sarelle's position is that the succession war is a symptom of a solvable problem: there is no verifiable, incorruptible test of who should rule. The Ashen Crown is that test. She intends to reassemble it — and she does not, in fact, particularly want to wear it. She wants to be the one who *administers* it. The kingmaker outlives the king.

She began excavating Crown sites months **before** Emperor Ashkar Vaelthorn's death. This is the single most damning fact about her, and the party already has physical evidence of it.

## What she's hiding

- **She knows the Crown's price** — that whoever reassembles it either reborns the relic or becomes ash themselves. She has read the same inscription the party has, in three other places. Her solution is not to survive it. Her solution is to have **someone else** complete the final assembly while she controls the outcome. She needs a candidate. A determined, ambitious outsider carrying a fragment would be ideal.
- **She knew the Emperor would die**, because the Concordat arranged the circumstances. Not poison — something slower and more deniable. Proof of this exists somewhere in the necropolis archives and would fracture the Concordat if it surfaced.
- **She is not unopposed inside her own faction** — see `info/faction-subplots.md` and Magus Orell Tain, below.

## How she should enter the story

Not as a boss fight. Sarelle's natural first move toward the party is **recruitment**: she finds out they hold a fragment, and instead of sending soldiers she sends an invitation. She is genuinely willing to make them wealthy, protected, and informed — because a willing candidate who walks into the final ritual is worth more to her than a corpse she has to loot.

This lands directly on Ilvaneth's goal (transcend death) and directly on Kriv's (restore his house). Sarelle can credibly offer both. She can also credibly deliver on them, right up until the moment the bill comes due.
