# High Prior Varic Sennett

**Location**: Karsgate, the Ember Crown Cathedral (Kingsward)
**Role**: Regional High Prior of the orthodox Ember Crown faith — Prior Aldric Venn's actual superior
**Disposition to party**: Unaware of them specifically
**Status in play**: Not yet introduced
**Tier**: Important — regional High Prior, Venn's actual superior; his correct suspicion about a Karsgate Choir cell (Kessic Ambrey's) could move independently of the party and reshape the Choir's Karsgate presence entirely.
**Class & Level**: Cleric 10 (Life Domain) — senior orthodox clergy, patient and formidable rather than aggressive. AC 16, HP ~85, spell save DC 16.
**Purpose**: Built as Karsgate's senior orthodox Prior — tasked with independently closing in on Kessic Ambrey's hidden Choir cell, and as a deeper doctrinal resource than his subordinate Venn.

## Surface

A dragonborn, gold-scaled going grey at the jaw, moves with the unhurried certainty of someone who has never had to raise his voice to be obeyed. Formal vestments in Ember Crown's ash-and-gold, worn like armor rather than costume. Speaks slowly, chooses words the way a mason chooses stones — for load-bearing, not decoration.

## What he actually wants

**To keep Ossara's actual teaching from being buried under two centuries of arguments about her.** Sennett is neither reformer nor reactionary — he genuinely believes death is final and that she keeps the names of the dead, full stop, and considers both the Concordat's procedural indifference and the Choir's death-and-return doctrine to be different flavors of the same error: mortals deciding they've found a shortcut around a truth that was never supposed to have one. He is patient about this in a way that reads as passivity and isn't — the orthodox faith outlasted the schism once already and he intends for it to outlast this one too.

## What he's hiding

- **He suspects the Cinder Choir has a presence inside Karsgate itself**, not just Gallowmere — he has no proof, only a pattern of unusual funerary requests and a missing acolyte three years back who was never satisfactorily explained. He has not raised this with Prior Venn or anyone else, because raising it without proof would look like paranoia from a man his own clergy already considers old-fashioned.
- **He knows more about the schism's history than he's shared with anyone, including Venn** — including a fuller account of the priestess who founded the Choir than the fragmentary record the party has already found. He has never had a reason to volunteer it.

## Current knowledge & stance

*Living record — update whenever he learns something new. See `.claude/rules/npc-consistency.md`.*

- **As of session 7, not yet introduced**: unaware of the party. Unaware his own suspicion about a Karsgate Choir cell is correct.

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect him

- If shown genuine, credible proof of a Choir presence in Karsgate → moves decisively and quietly, using the cathedral's own resources rather than reporting to secular authority first — he doesn't trust the Magistrate's Court to handle this correctly.
- If asked directly about the Choir's founding schism → he'll share what he knows, but only with someone who approaches it as a scholarly or doctrinal question, not a political one. He can tell the difference immediately and doesn't reward the wrong approach.
- If he learns a party member has genuinely died and returned → this is, to him, the single most dangerous possible turn of events — not because he'd disbelieve it, but because he'd believe it completely, and it would validate exactly the doctrine his entire career has stood against.
- If offered proof the Concordat engineered the Emperor's death → he'd treat this as confirmation of what he already suspects about the Concordat's moral hollowness, but wouldn't act on it rashly; he's outlasted worse scandals by being patient.

## Move List

Tied to `info/consequences.md`'s "Varic Sennett — Confirming the Karsgate Cell" threshold (currently 1/3).

- **At Weight 1 (current)**: a years-old pattern and one unexplained missing acolyte — real suspicion, no proof.
- **At Weight 2**: a new, similar incident occurs (another unusual funerary request, another disappearance) — enough that his private suspicion hardens toward certainty. Playable now: Sennett, if the party has any standing with him, mentions the pattern aloud for the first time, testing whether they'd help him look into it.
- **At Weight 3 (crossed)**: he acts — uses the cathedral's own resources to move against Kessic Ambrey's cell directly, without involving secular authority. A real, sudden escalation in Karsgate's Undercity that the party may or may not see coming.
- **If it stalls**: he continues patiently unconvinced-on-paper, correct in private, silent in public.
