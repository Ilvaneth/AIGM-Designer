# Corla Vint

**Location**: Karsgate — The Broken Wheel (the Reeks)
**Role**: Dirty-contract broker — theft, kidnapping, and, for the right price, worse
**Disposition to party**: Unaware of them specifically
**Status in play**: Not yet introduced
**Tier**: Important — Karsgate's dirty-contract broker; her network's growth is a real shift in the Reeks' balance of power, and she could as easily be hired against the party as for them.
**Purpose**: Built to give Karsgate's criminal underworld a dirty-contract broker parallel to Corvin Thale's legitimate information network — tasked with arranging jobs for or against the party, and as a potential rival-network thread against Sella Dray.
**Class & Level**: Rogue 4 (no subclass — deliberately unremarkable, per her own file, not built to be memorable in a fight). AC 14, HP ~27.

## Surface

A gnome, unremarkable in exactly the way she's cultivated — grey clothes, grey hair, the kind of face that's hard to describe five minutes after meeting her. Sits at the same back table at the Broken Wheel most nights, nursing one drink for hours, and somehow every serious inquiry in the room eventually finds its way to her without anyone quite remembering who pointed them there.

## What she actually wants

**To die rich, old, and never once personally connected to anything she's arranged.** Corla doesn't do the work herself — she's a broker, not an operator. She listens to what a client actually needs, prices it honestly against the real risk, and connects them to whoever in the city can do it (the Ninefinger crew for anything requiring real hands, freelancers for the rest). Her entire value is distance: nothing traces to her because she's never in the room when it happens.

## What she's hiding

- **She keeps no records of any kind** — no ledger, no names, nothing written down ever. Everything lives in her memory specifically because memory can't be seized in a raid.
- **She has a real, if narrow, moral floor**: no contracts involving children, and no contracts against anyone who's ever done her a genuine, unpaid kindness. She won't announce this as a principle — clients simply find those requests quietly, permanently unavailable.
- **She reports nothing to any faction** and has turned down approaches from all three at different points — being owned by anyone would make her far less useful to everyone else.

## What she can arrange

Theft (from a lifted purse to a serious burglary), kidnapping, intimidation, and — for a price reflecting the real danger to everyone involved, including her — an actual assassination. She always names the price before the target, and the price is never small.

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect her

- If approached with a legitimate contract and real coin → handled professionally, no judgment, no questions beyond what's needed to price the job accurately.
- If the party tries to identify or threaten her directly → she has no loyalty worth dying for and will give up a client's name before her own life, but genuinely doesn't know much to give up beyond names — she deals in introductions, not operational details.
- If asked to arrange something against her stated moral floor → a flat, final no, with no negotiation and no explanation offered.
- If she learns the party themselves are worth real coin as a target (a bounty, a faction's interest) → purely professional; she'd broker a contract against the party the same as anyone else, with zero personal animosity.
