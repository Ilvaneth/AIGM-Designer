# Captain Doreth Kell

**Location**: Gallowmere — the garrison house by the north gate
**Role**: Garrison commander; officially the crown's authority in Gallowmere
**Disposition to party**: N/A — fled, gone for good
**Status in play**: **FLED, day 75, session 17.** First and only in-person meeting was also his last — Kriv and Ilvaneth staged a coup inside his own garrison (21 of his 30 soldiers turned via Dallin Marsh, 330 gp), cornering him in his office. He offered everything he had to survive: his hidden 3-year skimming stash (~500 gp, a fisherman's hut south of the docks, key surrendered), and his real Compact contact — **Renwick Tale**, a grain merchant who's actually Coren Ashvale's eyes and ears in Gallowmere, monthly meetings at the old dockside depot, next one ~10 days out. Kriv accepted the information, then told him to disappear completely — change his name, never be heard from again, "Captain Kell" ceases to exist, or he dies. Kell agreed and fled into the night with his sister's family. Genuinely relieved to survive, not resentful — got exactly what he wanted (out) at the cost of his identity, not his life.
**Tier**: Important — commands Gallowmere's garrison, is secretly compromised (Compact retainer), and is actively working toward a self-directed exit. Tracked via `campaign-clock.md`'s "Kell's escape fund" clock, not a `consequences.md` block.
**Class & Level**: Fighter 6 (no subclass — a competent, tired career soldier, not a duelist). AC 16 (chain mail), HP ~58.
**Purpose**: Built as Gallowmere's nominal authority who's secretly compromised (Compact retainer, quietly saving to flee) — tasked with being a source of official blindness/intelligence for the right price, and a live clock the party can accelerate or prevent.

## Surface

Human, career soldier, mid-forties, weathered, competent, tired. Keeps the garrison in reasonable order and the town in reasonable peace. Known to be bribable in small ways — a fine reduced, a search not conducted — which everyone treats as normal and nobody treats as corruption.

## What he actually wants

**Out.** Kell has read the war correctly: Gallowmere is a border town on a road three armies will eventually want, and when that happens the garrison's thirty-odd soldiers will be a speed bump. He is not going to die here for a throne with no one on it. He is quietly assembling enough money to buy passage for himself and his sister's family to somewhere far south, and he intends to be gone before the first real column arrives.

Every bribe he takes goes into that fund. He is not saving for greed. He is saving for a specific day he thinks is coming.

## What he's hiding

- **He is on Ironclad Compact retainer.** The garrison answers to the crown on paper and to the Compact in practice — Kell takes a stipend to make sure Compact caravans pass unsearched and Compact interests in Gallowmere stay unbothered.
- **He is skimming garrison supplies** — grain, arrowheads, boots — and selling them through intermediaries. This is the bulk of his escape fund.
- **The Compact knows about the skimming** and says nothing, because a commander they can hang is more useful than one they merely pay. Kell knows they know. It is the single thing that keeps him awake.

## What he can offer

- Official blindness: a gate opened at the wrong hour, a search that doesn't happen, a body that isn't investigated
- Garrison intelligence: troop movements, who's been asking after whom, which convoys are coming
- If genuinely befriended or bought: real protection inside Gallowmere

## What changes his attitude

- **Toward the party**: money, obviously — but more effectively, a credible route out. Anyone who can get his sister's family south has him completely.
- **Against the party**: bringing attention to Gallowmere. He doesn't care what the party does; he cares whether it makes his last months here loud.
- **Leverage**: proof of the skimming would ruin him — but the Compact already holds that card, so a second holder just makes him desperate rather than obedient. Play him as a cornered man, not a pliable one.

## Move List

Tied to `campaign-clock.md`'s "Kell's escape fund — fires ~day 90" clock.

- **At the current pace (day 42)**: skimming garrison supplies and taking Compact bribes steadily, per his own file. No visible change yet.
- **Roughly halfway (before day ~65)**: the fund is visibly closer — a DM-playable beat: he starts quietly asking around about safe southern routes, or turns down a bribe that would draw too much attention, uncharacteristically cautious.
- **At day 90 (fires)**: he leaves. Gallowmere loses its garrison commander with no clean transition — a real, felt change to who holds nominal authority in town, and a real opportunity or crisis depending on who fills the vacuum.
- **Preventable**: per `campaign-clock.md`'s own note, this clock can be stopped — a credible route out delivered by the party (or removing his reason to fear the war reaching Gallowmere) changes his calculus entirely.
