# Borgha Kresk — Ironmonger's Row

**Location**: Ironmonger's Row, Harrowgate
**Role**: Master smith, one of a dozen forges in the quarter
**Disposition to party**: Neutral, first meeting (session 6, day 37)
**Tier**: Minor — confirmed by the DM, 2026-08-29 NPC-tier sweep. A service provider; no independent scheme.
**Purpose**: Built to give Harrowgate's Ironmonger's Row a real named smith (settlement-scaling requirement) who buys loot and sells mundane gear — a service provider, not a plot lever.

## Surface

Half-orc, mid-forties, built like the anvils she works — broad shoulders, forearms scarred pale from old burns. Soot-streaked leather apron over bare arms, hair cropped short and singed at the ends in more than one place. Talks in short, appraising sentences; doesn't waste breath on pleasantries when there's metal on the table.

## What she wants

Honest stock, fair trade, no trouble. She's built a reputation as the one forge on the Row that won't ask where something came from as long as it isn't obviously stolen from someone local — dwarven ruin-salvage and monster parts are ordinary business to her, not a red flag.

## What she knows / can offer

- Buys raw metal, mundane weapons/armor, and scrap at the standard 50% baseline — she doesn't haggle much below that, but a good pitch can move it up.
- Kriv's Smith's Tools proficiency would register with her — a professional courtesy, not a discount, but she'd talk shop if he showed interest.
- Regional smith gossip: unusual metal orders, who's commissioning strange pieces — a minor lead source if the party ever needs one.

## Standing arrangement

**Day 52, session 10**: the party brought her a dead Lonca courier's undelivered warning about unusual duergar activity near Kesh Deeps threatening the Row's iron supply — real news, not something she could have gotten otherwise. In thanks, she now gives them her real price on future trades, no haggling required. The party also verbally committed to looking into Kesh Deeps itself; she's relaying the warning to the Guild in the meantime.
