# Brother Kessic Ambrey

**Location**: Karsgate Undercity — the Choir's cell among the old burial vaults
**Role**: Cinder Choir cell leader, Karsgate
**Disposition to party**: Unaware of them specifically
**Status in play**: Not yet introduced
**Tier**: Important — an unauthorized Choir cell leader independently searching for a fragment; his search's outcome is tracked as its own threshold.
**Class & Level**: Cleric 5 (Death Domain). AC 13, HP ~38, spell save DC 12. Channel Divinity: Touch of Death.
**Purpose**: Built as a second, more anxious Choir cell leader in Karsgate — tasked with independently searching the Undercity vaults for a fragment, creating a discoverable thread and a possible collision with Sennett's suspicion or the party's own investigation.

## Surface

A halfling, older than he looks, missing three fingers on his left hand from an accident he describes differently every time he's asked. Moves through the Undercity's dark with the total confidence of someone who's mapped it by feel over years. Dresses like a gravedigger because he effectively is one — his cover trade is retrieving and properly interring the dead who fall into the Undercity and are never claimed.

## What he actually wants

**Proof, and he's stopped being patient about waiting for it.** Unlike Fenn's calm certainty, Kessic's faith has curdled into something more anxious — he's spent a decade in the dark beneath a city that doesn't know he exists, screening bodies nobody else wants, and has nothing to show for it. He is closer than Fenn to leadership's own desperation, not further from it — if the manufacture program produces anything at all, he's more likely to accept it uncritically than question it, because he needs the doctrine to be true more than he needs it to be honest.

## What he's hiding

- **His cell is smaller than Fenn's** — two members besides himself, both recruited from among the Undercity's own forgotten people (someone has to bury the city's unclaimed dead, and grief finds recruits everywhere).
- **Tracked as a real threshold now**: `info/consequences.md`'s "Kessic Ambrey — The Unauthorized Search" (1/3, pending). Session events involving his search should add weight there, not just stay in prose.
- **He has independently identified a Crown-adjacent site** — the burial vaults his cell already lives among (`locations/karsgate.md`'s Undercity, Vault of the Nameless specifically) are, in his own doctrinal reading, exactly the kind of unmarked grave a fragment could hide in. He hasn't found anything yet and hasn't told Yeva Ashwright he's looking, because if he's wrong he'd rather not have reported it.
- **He knows High Prior Sennett suspects something** — a near-miss three years ago (the missing acolyte Sennett half-remembers) taught him to be far more careful than Fenn has ever had to be, operating this close to the orthodox cathedral's own seat.

## Current knowledge & stance

*Living record — update whenever he learns something new. See `.claude/rules/npc-consistency.md`.*

- **As of session 7, not yet introduced**: unaware of the party, unaware of Fenn's own situation with them. Actively searching the vaults on his own initiative, unauthorized by leadership.

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect him

- If the party is discovered searching the same vaults he's interested in → his first instinct is territorial, not doctrinal — he doesn't want competition for a find he's spent years quietly pursuing, and might treat a Crown-bearing party as a threat to *his* discovery before he ever asks about their doctrine.
- If shown genuine, undeniable proof someone has died and returned → unlike Fenn's measured reverence, Kessic's reaction is closer to relief bordering on obsession — this is the vindication he's been waiting a decade for.
- If Yeva Ashwright ever learns he's been searching the vaults without authorization → he's not disciplined for initiative in a faction this thin on results, but he would resent being made to share credit for anything he actually finds.
- If he ever crosses paths with Odric Fenn (unlikely, different circuits) → their contrast — Fenn's calm certainty versus Kessic's anxious need — would be visible immediately to anyone who met both.

## Move List

Tied to `info/consequences.md`'s "Kessic Ambrey — The Unauthorized Search" threshold (currently 1/3).

- **At Weight 1 (current)**: a decade of quiet searching, no results yet.
