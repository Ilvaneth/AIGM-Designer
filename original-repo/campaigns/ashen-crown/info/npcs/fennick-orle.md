# Fennick Orle

**Location**: Gallowmere — "The Last Coin" tavern, behind the bar
**Role**: Barkeep, gossip broker, quiet debt-holder
**Disposition to party**: Friendly — has an old working relationship with Ilvaneth
**Status in play**: Introduced session 1
**Tier**: Important — quietly accumulating real leverage over roughly a quarter of Gallowmere's households; his debt book is a genuine future power lever, not just gossip-broker flavor.
**Class & Level**: Not built for combat, per his own file ("folds immediately and permanently resents it" if threatened physically) — no meaningful class levels; treat as a non-combatant. His actual power is entirely informational/financial, expressed through the Move List below, not a stat block.
**Purpose**: Built as Gallowmere's gossip broker who's secretly building a town-wide debt ledger — tasked with being the party's go-to rumor source and, later, a real informal power lever as the ledger grows.

## Surface

A half-orc barkeep who never stops polishing something. Cheerful, chatty, remembers everyone's name and everyone's business. Sells information the way other men sell ale — by the measure, priced to the buyer. Likes being the man who knows.

## What he actually wants

**To own the town's debts, not its coin.** Fennick has spent six years quietly buying up small debts across Gallowmere — a fisherman's loan here, a widow's back-rent there, never enough to notice. He isn't collecting. He's accumulating. His logic is simple and cold: when the war finally reaches this town, coin will be worthless and *obligation* will be the only currency left. He intends to be the man everyone owes.

He genuinely likes Ilvaneth. That does not mean he wouldn't include her in the ledger.

## What he's hiding

- **The debt book.** A ledger, kept off-premises (buried under the floor of a rented storeroom two streets over), listing roughly forty households and what they owe him. Nobody knows how far it goes. If this became public, half of Gallowmere would want him dead and the other half would want it burned.
- **He has already sold information about the party.** Not maliciously — someone paid, he answered. He told Sefwyn Marrow that two outsiders were asking about the ossuary. He'll admit this if directly and specifically confronted; to him it was simply business and he'd expect Ilvaneth to understand. **Confirmed session 5**: confronted directly by Ilvaneth at the tavern, he admitted it immediately and without resistance — sold "an elf and a dragonborn" to both Sefwyn (session 1) and a Concordat convoy agent (~day 5), no names given, for pay. No guilt, no defensiveness. Now owes Ilvaneth a favor for not holding it against him.

## What he can offer

- Rumors and leads — see `info/rumors.md`; Fennick is the single best source for the rumor pool
- Names, timings, who's arriving and when
- Cheap goodwill: he'd rather have a favor owed than coin paid

## What changes his attitude

- **Toward the party**: being treated as a peer rather than a vending machine. Bringing him information instead of only buying it.
- **Against the party**: threatening him physically (he folds immediately and permanently resents it), or moving on the debt book.

## Move List

Tied to `info/consequences.md`'s "Fennick Orle — The Debt Book Grows" threshold (currently 1/3).

- **At Weight 1 (current)**: roughly forty households on the ledger, six years of quiet accumulation.
- **At Weight 2**: the book crosses into genuinely town-shaping territory — enough households that a real crisis (the war reaching Gallowmere, coin becoming worthless) would make him the single most powerful private creditor in town. Playable now: Fennick mentions, almost offhand, that "half this town owes somebody something, and more of it's me than you'd think."
- **At Weight 3 (crossed)**: the war-driven crisis actually arrives (or Fennick decides to call in the book preemptively) and he becomes a real, exercisable power in Gallowmere — a major shift in the town's informal power structure, worth a real scene, not a status update.
- **If it stalls**: he keeps accumulating quietly, exactly as before.
