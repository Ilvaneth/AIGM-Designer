# Ostrig Kelmar

**Location**: Harrowgate — works out of a stoneyard near the Garrison House
**Role**: Master mason and foreman; runs a small crew of masons and carpenters, hires out for construction/repair contracts across the Thornlands
**Disposition to party**: Neutral, first meeting (session 14, day 60)
**Tier**: Minor — a hired specialist, no independent scheme, but tied to the ongoing Shestendeliath restoration project and may recur across future sessions
**Purpose**: Built to fill `shestendeliath-restoration.md`'s Phase 1 requirement — "a real mason and carpenter, hired out of Harrowgate" — the first concrete step of Kriv's restoration plan.

## Surface

A dwarf, weathered and stocky, forearms corded with real working muscle rather than a soldier's build. Stone dust permanently ground into the creases of his hands. Direct, unsentimental — sizes up a job by what it'll cost him in time and materials, not by who's asking.

## What he wants

A paying contract with clear terms and a client who won't vanish halfway through. Reputation matters to him — Harrowgate's a small enough town that a bad job or an unpaid bill follows a builder for years.

## Current knowledge & stance

- **As of day 60**: knows "Shestendeliath" only as an old, attainted house name — vaguely recalls stories of a burned-out hold somewhere in the eastern Thornlands, "the one nobody goes near." Doesn't personally believe the ghost stories, but knows his crew might need convincing, and it'll affect what he charges.

## Decision logic

- If offered fair pay and the job is honestly described (fire damage to a roof/upper floor, not a ruin) → takes the contract, no different from any other job.
- If his crew balks at the site's reputation → raises the price to cover hazard pay, or asks for the client's own people (Kethrax's garrison) to be visibly present as reassurance.
- If paid late or lied to about the scope → won't work with the party again, and says why to other Harrowgate contractors.
