# Vesa Ilkraven

**Location**: Karsgate — no fixed shop or seat mentioned; moves in high-stakes gambling and merchant circles across the region. First met at Corran's private game, Harrowgate (session 7, day 47).
**Role**: Wealthy Karsgate socialite/information broker — exact business unstated, deliberately.
**Disposition to party**: Neutral, intrigued. First meeting went well — she respects skill, was charmed rather than offended by being deflected.
**Status in play**: Met session 7 at Corran's game. Watched Ilvaneth (as "Lady Serah Ashworth") play three hands — read through one bluff and let it go, caught the fake accent/identity, called a later bluff and won, lost the final big hand. **Knows "Lady Serah Ashworth" is not Ilvaneth's real identity.** Did not press the point or expose her at the table. Offered an open invitation to find her in Karsgate.
**Tier**: Important — she now holds real leverage (knowledge that the party's cover identity is false) that could plausibly be used, sold, or sat on, the same shape of capability that made Kessra Vane Important before ever appearing in a scene.
**Class & Level**: SRD Spy block (reskinned) — AC 12, HP 27, Insight +4 (already used in play), Deception +5, Investigation +5, Perception +6, Persuasion +5, Stealth +4. Not built as a combat threat; her leverage is informational and social, not physical.
**Purpose**: Built live at the table (session 7) as the "new face from Karsgate, pockets full" thread `jobs-and-opportunities.md`/`state.md` had left open since session 5 — tasked with being an intriguing, ambiguous Karsgate contact who now holds a real card to play against the party's cover identity, whenever she chooses to.

## Surface

A tiefling woman, mid-thirties, short filed horns ringed in thin silver bands, dark red eyes. Dresses expensively but deliberately understated — someone who doesn't want to look like a target, and doesn't quite manage it. Controlled, watches people more than cards, gives away almost nothing unless she chooses to.

## What she actually wants

Unclear even to the party — deliberately. She reads as someone who deals in information and favors as much as coin (the Karsgate references, the careful vetting-by-conversation approach at the table). Whether she's a genuine socialite, a fixer, or something closer to Kessra Vane's line of work is unresolved and should stay that way until she reappears.

## Current knowledge & stance

*Living record — update whenever she learns something new. See `.claude/rules/npc-consistency.md`.*

- **Session 7, day 47, Corran's game**: knows "Lady Serah Ashworth" is a false identity (caught a Deception check on the accent question, DC effectively 17 vs. Ilvaneth's rolled 7). Does not know Ilvaneth's real name, race (saw her disguised, human-presenting), or anything about the Crown/fragments. Left the invitation open — "ask for me in Karsgate" — without pressing further or reacting with hostility. ## Decision logic — how new information could redirect her

- **If the party visits Karsgate and seeks her out** → she engages on her own terms, first — treats it as the party choosing to extend the relationship, a position of some leverage for her.
- **If she learns anything connecting "Lady Serah Ashworth" to the Crown fragments or the Concordat's manhunt** → this is where her Move List below actually moves; what she knows now (just a false name) isn't enough to act on by itself.
- **If the party never follows up** → she doesn't chase them. A curiosity she noted, not an active investigation — she has her own business.
- **If offered a real deal (information, coin, a favor) in exchange for her silence or her network** → transactional at heart; she'd consider it seriously rather than refuse on principle.

## Move List

Tied to `info/consequences.md`'s "Vesa Ilkraven — What She Does With What She Knows" threshold (currently 1/3).

- **At Weight 1 (current)**: holds one fact (a false name) and no motive to act on it yet. No visible move.
- **At Weight 2**: she does some quiet digging on her own initiative — asking around Karsgate's information circuits whether anyone knows an elf wizard using "Serah Ashworth" or matching Ilvaneth's real description. Playable: a rumor reaches Corvin Thale or Sefwyn Marrow that someone in Karsgate has been asking after "an elf who plays cards well."
- **At Weight 3 (crossed)**: she's connected enough dots (or been offered enough money) to act — approaches the party directly with a proposition, sells the lead to someone else, or otherwise turns the leverage into something concrete. Not violent, not an ambush — she deals, per her own established style.
- **If it stalls**: she remains exactly what she was at the table — an open, faintly amused thread in Karsgate, available if the party ever follows up.
