# Alis Wend

**Location**: Gallowmere — the refugee camp outside the walls
**Role**: Refugee mother; the party's victim
**Disposition to party**: Bitter, then re-deceived, now genuinely loyal — believes a second lie still, but the day-74 offer is real and she knows it
**Status in play**: Introduced session 2; first direct confrontation session 5, day 33; **recruited into Kriv's service, day 74, session 17**
**Tier**: Minor — confirmed by the DM, 2026-08-29 NPC-tier sweep. Was a closed victim/consequence thread; **reopened day 74** as a real recruitment thread — she and her two children now serve Kriv, and she's gathering other refugee-camp families/individuals (Bertrand family, a young tiefling named Vesper, an old carpenter named Corrik) on his behalf, not yet told his real name or house. 20 gp given for preparations.
**Purpose**: Built as the live, human consequence of the party's own con — the refugee family they swindled — to give a Neutral Evil party's choices real weight without moralizing. Closed as a thread, kept as a record.

## Who she is

A half-elf woman from Thornwick with two young children, fled south when the Concordat cordoned the crypt and the village started emptying. She is the family Kriv earned the trust of, and the family Ilvaneth then conned out of 18 gp — every coin they had — with a promise of a home, work, and a fresh start at an address that does not exist.

**Session 5 update**: she ran out of patience before the party ever came back. Spotted them sneaking through the camp's edge (day 33) and confronted them directly, openly angry, in front of a few other refugees. Ilvaneth talked her down with a second lie — claiming the party itself had been conned by the men who were supposed to arrange the house/job — and Alis believed it (Deception 18). **The party gave her nothing.** She's calmer now but still poor, still hungry, and her trust in "the elf and the dragonborn" rests entirely on a story that won't survive real scrutiny. If she ever compares notes with someone who knows the truth, or simply keeps asking around about "the men who took our money," the second lie collapses along with the first — and this time she'll know it was deliberate, twice.

## Why she matters

This is the campaign's most immediate **consequence thread**, and it should not be wasted as a guilt-trip — the party are Neutral Evil and chose this deliberately. Play it as *complication*, not punishment.

Three ways it can land, all live:

1. **She finds out.** Within a couple of weeks she has no coin, no home, and children who are hungrier than before. She learns the address was invented. She does not have the means to take revenge — but she has a voice, and a refugee camp full of people who were also told about "the two kind strangers." A soured camp is a soured information source and a soured labor pool. See `info/power-base.md`.
2. **She comes from Thornwick.** She has family and neighbors in the village the party has now visited. If the party is recognized in Thornwick as the pair who took the Wend family's last coin, Hesta Bram closes the village to them permanently.
3. **Someone else uses her.** Sefwyn Marrow collects debts and grudges the way other people collect coins. A wronged, articulate witness who can place the party in Gallowmere on specific days is *useful*, and Sefwyn will pay for that. Fennick would sell it. Vey would file it.

## If the party wants to fix it

Entirely possible and entirely in character for an ambitious evil party: making good on the promise — actually finding the Wends a home and work — costs money and effort but buys something a Neutral Evil party genuinely wants, which is a loyal, grateful, dependent population in the camp who owe them specifically. That is the first brick of a real power base.
