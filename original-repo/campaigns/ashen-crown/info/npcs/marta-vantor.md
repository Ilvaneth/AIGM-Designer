# Marta Vantor

**Location**: Karsgate — Vantor Shipping Concern (offices in the Ledger, warehouse in Riverside)
**Role**: Owner and head of Vantor Shipping, the largest river-trade concern in Karsgate
**Disposition to party**: Unaware of them specifically
**Status in play**: Not yet introduced
**Tier**: Important — controls the largest river-trade concern in Karsgate; her underbidding campaign is tracked as its own threshold and directly intersects the party's own Gallowmere-docks ambitions.
**Class & Level**: Bard 5 (College of Lore) — not a fighter; her real weapon is capital and information. AC 12, HP ~33. Reflects a sharp negotiator who'd hire protection rather than fight personally.
**Purpose**: Built as Karsgate's dominant river-trade magnate — tasked with being the biggest lever on the party's own Gallowmere-docks smuggling ambitions, as either a rival or an eventual upgrade path.

## Surface

A half-elf, mid-sixties, dresses plainly for someone this wealthy — she made her money by outworking competitors who assumed a woman running a shipping concern would fail, and has never stopped dressing like she still needs to prove it. Sharp, fast with numbers, visibly impatient with people who waste her time. Three grown children, none of whom she considers ready to inherit yet.

## What she actually wants

**To make Vantor Shipping too large to route around.** Karsgate's river trade currently flows through several competing concerns; Marta's ambition is a controlling position, not just a large one — she wants the day to come when nobody moves cargo up or down the river without her knowing about it first. She is patient, methodical, and treats every deal as a small piece of that larger campaign.

## What she's hiding

- **She already knows about the Gallowmere smuggling route** — Karsgate river traffic and Gallowmere's own docks are connected by the same trade network the party's standing plan targets, and Marta has quietly profited from it for years without ever touching the illegal end herself. She'd notice immediately if someone new started operating in that space.
- **Tracked as a real threshold now**: `info/consequences.md`'s "Marta Vantor — The Underbidding Campaign" (1/3, pending).
- **She's currently underbidding a rival concern into bankruptcy** on razor-thin margins, funded by reserves she doesn't advertise — a temporary vulnerability if anyone needed quick leverage against her specifically.

## Current knowledge & stance

*Living record — update whenever she learns something new. See `.claude/rules/npc-consistency.md`.*

- **As of session 7, not yet introduced**: unaware of the party or their Gallowmere docks ambitions specifically.

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect her

- If approached with a genuine, profitable trade proposal → evaluates it purely on the numbers, faction affiliation or reputation aside — she's sold to worse people than a rumored elf and dragonborn if the margin justified it.
- If she learns the party is operating in "her" trade network (the Gallowmere smuggling route) without her knowledge → treats this as a competitive problem to solve, not a moral one — she'd rather absorb them into a working arrangement than fight a turf war she doesn't need.
- If offered real capital or leverage to help her finish underbidding her current rival → this is the fastest way to her genuine gratitude, which is a rare and valuable thing from her specifically.
- If crossed or cheated on a deal → she doesn't retaliate immediately or emotionally; she remembers, and the consequence arrives later, at a moment of her choosing, structured to look like ordinary bad luck.
