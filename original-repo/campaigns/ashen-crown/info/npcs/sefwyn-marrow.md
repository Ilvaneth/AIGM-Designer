# Sefwyn Marrow — "The Grey Tally"

**Location**: Mobile — Gallowmere, the northern road, wherever the work is
**Role**: Debt-collector, fixer, relic-hunter for hire
**Disposition to party**: Warming — met face-to-face day 25, first exchange went well for both sides
**Status in play**: Met face-to-face session 3 (day 25), en route to Hollowmoor Barrows. Traded the Magus Orell Tain lead for a look at the party's first (ossuary) fragment only — does not know about the second or third fragment. Re-initiated contact session 6, day 37 (Tain introduction arranged, confirmed for day 42 at Millward Crossing) — see `bug-log.md` for why this took 12 days longer than intended.
**Tier**: Important — an independent fixer with real Concordat access, a growing private knowledge-tally, and a self-directed exit plan. Moves `info/consequences.md`'s "Sefwyn Marrow — Private Tally" on their own.
**Class & Level**: Rogue 7 (Assassin) — precise, cold-blooded when the math calls for it, never for pleasure, per their own file. AC 15 (studded leather + Dex), HP ~48, Sneak Attack 4d6.
**Purpose**: Built as "The Grey Tally," a rival fixer who cased the same ossuary the party robbed — tasked with becoming a recurring transactional contact, the bridge to Orell Tain, and a live threat if their private skim-ledger is ever exposed.

## Surface

Human, mid-forties, lean, grey-clothed, unremarkable on purpose. Carries a bound tally book everywhere and writes in it constantly — debts, favors, names, deaths. Speaks softly and precisely, never raises their voice, never makes a threat they haven't already priced. People call them the Grey Tally because they settle accounts and because nobody remembers their face afterward.

## What they actually want

**To stop being someone else's instrument.** Sefwyn works for a Concordat-linked buyer, and has for three years, and is very good at it. They are also quietly skimming: every relic they recover, every ritual they witness, every name they overhear goes into a second, private tally. They are building a body of knowledge about the Ashen Crown that their employer does not know they have. When they have enough, they intend to sell it — to the Compact, to the Choir, to whoever pays most — and disappear rich and unowned.

They are not cruel. They are *precise*. Violence is a line item, used when it's the cheapest option and never for pleasure.

## What they're hiding

- **The footprints outside the Gallowmere ossuary were theirs** (session 1). They got to the door, read enough of the ward to know what it would cost to breach it wrong, and walked away rather than risk it. Then the party opened it. Sefwyn knows this — Fennick sold them the fact that two outsiders were asking about the ossuary, and the seal was broken the next day.
- **They are currently tracking the party**, at a distance, without hostility. They want to know what was in that ossuary, and they'd rather buy the answer than fight for it.
- **The private tally** — their skim-ledger. Its existence is worth their life if the Concordat learns of it.

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect them

- If they learn the party actually holds 3 fragments, not 1 → this significantly raises their private tally's value; may re-open negotiations for a better look, or reassess how much leverage the party actually has over the Crown project.
- If Tain and the party form a real alliance → Sefwyn gains indirectly (their bridge-building reputation improves with both sides) but has no reason to insert themselves further unless asked.
- If their skim-ledger's existence is ever threatened with exposure → self-preservation overrides every other consideration immediately, per their file; they are precise, not brave.

## Move List

Tied to `info/consequences.md`'s "Sefwyn Marrow — Private Tally" threshold (currently 1/3).

- **At Weight 1 (current, day 42)**: has been shown the first fragment only; the private tally is real but modest. No visible move.
- **At Weight 2**: if shown a second fragment, deeper Crown knowledge, or a genuinely valuable secret, Sefwyn's own behavior shifts subtly — slightly more generous with information, testing whether the party is worth cultivating as a long-term source rather than a one-time trade. Playable now: Sefwyn offers something unprompted for the first time (a piece of Concordat gossip, a warning) as a trial balloon.
- **At Weight 3 (crossed)**: decides the tally is enough — quietly disengages from the Concordat, sells the accumulated knowledge to the highest bidder (Compact or Choir), and disappears. This should be played as a real, felt loss if the party has come to rely on Sefwyn as a source — not a throwaway status change.
- **If it stalls**: continues working for the Concordat as normal, skimming quietly, showing no urgency either way.

## First contact — how to play it

Sefwyn should NOT open with violence. Their opening move is a conversation: they know what the party did, they say so plainly, and they make an offer. Possible offers depending on the table's mood:
- Buy the fragment outright (they can raise a genuinely tempting sum)
- Buy *information* about it — what it looks like, what the inscription said
- Propose a partnership: they have Concordat access the party doesn't, the party has a fragment they can't get

They become a genuine enemy only if the party makes them one. They make an excellent recurring rival, an occasional uneasy ally, and eventually — if the party plays it right — a source who betrays the Concordat for them.
