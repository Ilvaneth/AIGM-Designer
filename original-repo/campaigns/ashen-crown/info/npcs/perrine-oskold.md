# Perrine Oskold

**Location**: Encountered on the Cindermoor road, day 56, en route Kesh Deeps → The Maker's Undoing — deceased. Her preserved body, satchel, and survey notebook are in the party's possession (Kriv's Bag of Holding).
**Role**: Ashlord Concordat archivist, surveying regional barrows for ash-king symbol comparisons under Archivist-Magus Sarelle Duskbourne's own written authorization — the same assignment Nessa Wren was on.
**Disposition to party**: N/A — killed on first contact, no ongoing relationship possible.
**Tier**: Minor — a working field archivist with no independent agenda of her own; her narrative weight, like Nessa Wren's, lives in what she leaves behind (her body, her notebook), not in anything she could still do.
**Purpose**: A second instance of the same Concordat regional survey the party already disrupted once (Nessa Wren). Exists to make the pattern real — one dead archivist could go unnoticed for a while; two, on the same stretch of road, within days, is the beginning of something Sarelle's own people will eventually connect.

---

## Surface

A gnome woman, middle-aged, meticulous — thick leather apron over travel clothes, ink-stained fingers, fine brushes for cleaning worn stone carvings. Wears the Ashlord Concordat's grey-ash travel cloak, hood down, entirely absorbed in her work when found. Unarmed in the field; her only real tool is a rubbing kit and a notebook.

---

## What they actually want

Nothing beyond the job in front of her — catalogue barrow symbols, compare them against the Concordat's archives, report back. A competent professional with no larger ambition on record, exactly like Nessa Wren. She had no reason to distrust a approaching stranger until it was already too late.

---

## Current knowledge & stance

Was mid-survey when killed — never got the chance to notice or react meaningfully. Did not know Nessa Wren was already dead.

N/A — killed before any real choice was possible.

## What was found on her / left behind

- **Survey notebook** — ash-king symbol rubbings and comparisons, in the same format as Nessa Wren's. One page, more recent than the rest: a note that she was due to **compare notes with "N. Wren" at a planned rendezvous point** — overdue by several days when she died. A real, live signal that someone at the Concordat will eventually notice two archivists gone quiet, not just one.
- **Travel authorization papers** — bear Sarelle Duskbourne's seal, naming her (Perrine Oskold) and her assignment area (the Cindermoor barrows).
- **15 gp**, a rubbing kit (no resale value), assorted brushes and chalk.
- **Body** — preserved with Gentle Repose (day 56, ~10 days before it needs recasting) and taken, same treatment as Nessa Wren's — see `party/characters/kriv-shestendeliath.md`'s Notable Items. The party now holds **two** preserved Concordat archivists' bodies.
