# Vharkoss Shestendeliath — "Lord Vharken Sable"

**Location**: Karsgate, the Kingsward — a respectable townhouse under an assumed name
**Role**: The man who sold Kriv's family
**Disposition to party**: Unaware of Kriv's survival
**Status in play**: Not yet introduced
**Level range**: Reachable level 8-10
**Tier**: Important — the living answer to who destroyed Kriv's house; his own fifteen-year fear of exposure is a self-directed thread that could resolve independently of the party finding him.
**Class & Level**: Sorcerer 9 (Draconic Bloodline) — latent Ash-Warden dragon-blood, never trained as a fighter, comfortable relying on it only if truly cornered. AC 15 (natural + Mage Armor), HP ~68, spell save DC 15.
**Purpose**: Built as the specific answer to who sold out Kriv's house — tasked with being a scene, not a fight: killed, ruined, used, or reclaimed-through, once Kriv finds him.

## Who he is

Kriv's uncle. His father's younger brother. Bronze-scaled where Kriv is red, older now, heavier, entirely comfortable.

He lives in Karsgate as **Lord Vharken Sable**, a minor foreign noble of unremarkable origins who arrived with money about fifteen years ago and has been quietly respectable ever since. He sits on a guild board. He hosts dinners. He is well liked.

## What he did

He sold the Warden rotation and the fragment's location to a Concordat intermediary, and named the night the house would be thinnest. In exchange: his life, a name, and enough money to start over.

**His justification, which he genuinely believes**: the house was going to fall regardless. The Concordat had decided, the Compact had been hired, and the only remaining question was whether *anyone* survived. He chose someone. He chose himself. He has had fifteen years to make peace with it and has largely succeeded.

## What he doesn't know

- **That Kriv is alive.** He believes every hatchling died. Learning otherwise will not make him repentant — it will make him *frightened*, and frightened men are useful.
- **That Sethra is alive**, or that she joined the people who bought him.

## What he knows — and this is why killing him immediately is a waste

- The Concordat commissioned the destruction, not the Compact. The Compact only carried it.
- The name of the intermediary who paid him — a thread leading directly to Sarelle Duskbourne's predecessor, and therefore to Sarelle's inherited file
- **Where the other Ash-Warden houses were**, because the buyer asked him about them too
- That the fragment was never recovered that night. Someone got out with it. He has spent fifteen years quietly afraid of exactly that

## The oath

He is oath-sworn Shestendeliath, and he broke faith. **Kriv, as the last legitimate heir, can invoke the house oath and compel a truthful accounting.** Vharkoss cannot refuse it — draconic oath-magic doesn't care that he'd rather not. See `kriv-thread.md`.

This is a scene, not a skill check. Let it be one.

## What Kriv can do with him

- **Kill him.** Satisfying, and it closes three information threads permanently.
- **Ruin him.** Expose Lord Sable as a traitor dragonborn living under a false name. He loses everything and lives, which he would find worse.
- **Use him.** He is a respected man in Karsgate with guild connections, money, and a desperate need to not be exposed. Kriv could own him completely. For a Neutral Evil character rebuilding a house, an owned uncle in a city's high society is worth more than a corpse.
- **Take the house back through him.** Vharkoss's fortune is Shestendeliath money. It can be reclaimed.

## Move List

Tied to `info/consequences.md`'s "Vharkoss Shestendeliath — The Fifteen-Year Fear" threshold (currently 1/3).

- **At Weight 1 (current)**: fifteen years of quiet, private fear — no external trigger yet.
- **At Weight 2**: something reaches him — a rumor of a surviving Shestendeliath, or Sethra's own search getting close enough that he notices. Playable now: "Lord Vharken Sable" is described, in a Karsgate social scene, as unusually distracted or newly cautious for no visible reason.
- **At Weight 3 (crossed)**: he acts on the fear — quietly hires protection, prepares to disappear again, or (if he's identified Kriv specifically) tries to reach out first, preemptively, before the confrontation Kriv is owed happens on Kriv's own terms.
- **If it stalls**: he continues exactly as he has for fifteen years — comfortable, respected, and afraid underneath it.
