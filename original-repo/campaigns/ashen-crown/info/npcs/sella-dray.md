# "Iron" Sella Dray

**Location**: Karsgate, the Reeks — Red Tally turf, the Warrens
**Role**: Leader of the Red Tally, the largest of the Warrens' three gangs
**Disposition to party**: Unaware of them specifically
**Status in play**: Not yet introduced
**Tier**: Important — leads the Warrens' largest gang; her move against Coalback is tracked as its own threshold and would visibly redraw Karsgate's underworld territory.
**Class & Level**: Fighter 8 (Champion). AC 16 (studded leather, no shield), HP ~82, enforces her own rules personally per her file.
**Purpose**: Built as the Warrens' largest gang leader — tasked with being a real territorial power the party could ally with, provoke, or exploit if they operate in Karsgate's underworld, including a move against the Coalback gang.

## Surface

A half-orc, heavily scarred across the forearms from years of enforcing her own rules personally rather than delegating the ugly work. Wears an actual iron ring through one ear, the source of her name and a story she tells differently depending on how much she's had to drink. Runs the Red Tally like a business, not a mob — schedules, ledgers, a real accounting of who owes what.

## What she actually wants

**Legitimacy she'll never officially get, and the next best thing to it.** Sella didn't build the Red Tally to stay a street gang forever — she wants the kind of unofficial authority where the Ledger Watch House quietly defers to her on Reeks business rather than pretending she doesn't exist. She runs the most organized protection racket in the district specifically because organized is harder to dismiss than criminal.

## What she's hiding

- **She's already in a slow, deniable negotiation with a Ledger Watch officer** — not full corruption yet, closer to mutual non-interference, and it would ruin them both if it surfaced too loudly, too fast.
- **Tracked as a real threshold now**: `info/consequences.md`'s "Sella Dray — Moving on Coalback" (1/3, pending).
- **The Coalback gang's brutality is bad for her business** — their violence draws attention that lands on all three gangs equally, and she's seriously considering absorbing or eliminating them to clean up the district's reputation, which would also make Red Tally look like the responsible, negotiable option to anyone watching from above.

## Current knowledge & stance

*Living record — update whenever she learns something new. See `.claude/rules/npc-consistency.md`.*

- **As of session 7, not yet introduced**: unaware of the party.

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect her

- If approached by a competent outside party proposing to take Warrens territory → her first move is negotiation, not violence — she'd rather absorb a genuine threat as a partner than fight a war she might lose, provided the approach shows real strength, not empty bravado.
- If the party moves against Coalback specifically → she is quietly pleased regardless of the outcome, and would consider backing whoever does it, since it solves a problem she's been weighing herself.
- If threatened or disrespected directly → she responds with calculated, proportional force — enough to make the point, not enough to invite outside attention onto the Warrens generally. She's disciplined even when angry.
- If offered real legitimacy (a Watch appointment, a formal arrangement) → this is the one thing money can't easily buy her, and the fastest way to her genuine cooperation.
