# Halvard Verrick

**Location**: Ostwick — the old Corley farmstead, rented, just outside the village
**Role**: Head of a war-fleeing merchant family, quietly liquidating what they carried out
**Disposition to party**: Warming — first meeting, day 49, ended on genuine gratitude
**Status in play**: Introduced Session 8, day 49. Sold most remaining valuables to the party (90 gp, fair price) and gave Ilvaneth the family genealogy record after being moved by her honesty.
**Tier**: Minor — no independent agency over tracked campaign state; his situation may recur narratively (a lowballing rival dealer, a family trying to rebuild) but doesn't move any faction/clock on its own.
**Purpose**: Built as the human face of Corvin Thale's Verrick collection job — a proud, tired man in reduced circumstances, distinct from a faceless "quest giver."

## Surface

A half-elf in his forties or fifties, fine features gone gaunt, greying hair. Wears a once-good kaftan, now worn thin. Tired eyes, but a proud bearing that doesn't fully bend even now.

## What he actually wants

To be treated with dignity, not pity, while he sells off what's left of his family's life. Money matters — they're genuinely in need — but being cheated again (after the rival dealer's lowball offer three weeks ago) matters more to his pride than the gold itself.

## Current knowledge & stance

- **Day 49, first meeting**: sold the jewelry box, a small painting, and worked stones to the party for 90 gp — fair against Ilvaneth's own appraisal (~110 gp), a sharp contrast to the rival dealer's earlier lowball offer on the family's most valuable pieces (grandmother's tiara, father's seal). Genuinely moved when Ilvaneth asked for the family genealogy book out of honest curiosity rather than profit, and gave it to her outright rather than sell it. Doesn't know the party is acting for Corvin Thale — was told nothing about who sent them. When asked, gave what he knows of the rival dealer: visited 3 times, overheard called "Marsh" once, doesn't know his lodging — pointed the party to Wren Talbot. Disposition ended the visit at Warming.

## Decision logic

- If the party (or Corvin) returns with more business, fair dealing — he'd remember and trust more readily, given how this first meeting went.
- If he learns he was lowballed or misled after all → the goodwill built here would sour fast; he already has one recent experience of exactly that.
- Would speak well of the party to Wren Talbot or other Ostwick locals if asked — word of a fair deal travels in a village this size.

## Connections

- **Wren Talbot** (The Hollow Sheaf) — pointed the party toward the farmstead; villagers are aware of the Verricks' situation and generally sympathetic.
- **The rival dealer** (unnamed, "he seemed like a hasty, impatient man" per Wren) — bought the family's most valuable pieces 3 weeks before the party arrived, for far less than fair. Possibly connected to `npcs/corvin-thale.md`'s own trade rivals, not yet confirmed.
- **Corvin Thale** — the actual buyer for what the party collected here, though Halvard doesn't know it; the party was told not to use Corvin's name.
