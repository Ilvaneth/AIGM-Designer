# The Bertrand Family

**Location**: Shestendeliath Hold — newly relocated, day 81
**Role**: Farmers, now working the Hold's own land
**Disposition to party**: Loyal, grateful — recruited via Alis Wend from the Gallowmere refugee camp
**Status in play**: Recruited day 74-76, session 17, alongside Alis Wend's own group. Arrived and settled at the Hold day 81.
**Tier**: Minor — no independent agency over tracked campaign state, per direct DM instruction (2026-09-04): all NPCs gained this session are Minor, recorded under the Hold's household.
**Purpose**: Built as part of Phase 2's real living-staff headcount — the household's first working farmers, alongside Corrik (carpentry) and Vesper.

## Surface

A dwarf couple and their two sons, fled the Thornlands' war-torn farmland for Gallowmere's refugee camp. Practical, quiet, used to hard work and low expectations from the world.

## What they actually want

Land to work, safety for their sons, and to stop being refugees. Kriv's offer (through Alis) was exactly what they needed — they didn't ask who he was, only what the work would be.
