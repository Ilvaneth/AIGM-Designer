# Sylvenna

**Location**: The Weeping Wood, the Dryad's Grove
**Role**: Dryad, the oldest living witness to the ash-king era in the region
**Disposition to party**: Friendly — met session 16, day 69; they fulfilled her bargain (cleared the Choked Grove and its approaches), earning real goodwill
**Status in play**: Introduced session 16, day 69 — bargain fulfilled
**Tier**: Important — losing ground to encroaching ettercaps/spiders is a real, tracked threat to `locations/the-weeping-wood.md`'s content; she also holds corrective knowledge about all three factions' founding.
**Class & Level**: Not a class/level build — she's a literal dryad, and the real SRD **Dryad** stat block (`srd_lookup.py monster "Dryad"`) is more accurate here than an invented character level, per `npc-tiers.md`'s exception for NPCs who aren't humanoid class-levelers. **Purpose**: Built as the Weeping Wood's ancient dryad — tasked with holding corrective lore about all three factions' founding, tradeable for help against her own losing fight against encroaching ettercaps/spiders.
**Aged up from the SRD baseline** to reflect her status as the oldest such being in the region: HP 45 (up from 22), spell save DC 16 (up from 14), otherwise as printed (AC 11/16 with barkskin, Fey Charm, Tree Stride, innate spellcasting).

## Surface

Bark-toned skin shifting subtly with the season, moves like the wood itself is agreeing to let her pass. Ancient in a way that doesn't read as old — she's simply always been here, the way the oldest trees are. Speaks slowly, with the patience of someone who has genuinely seen empires rise and end and doesn't consider either event especially rare.

## What she actually wants

**To keep the wood as it was, for as long as that's still possible.** Sylvenna isn't nostalgic for the ash-kings specifically — she's watched several eras end — but she has a real, growing problem: ettercaps and giant spiders have been spreading from the wood's northern edge faster than she alone can push back, and she's losing ground for the first time in longer than she can easily reckon. She'll trade real knowledge for real help, because the help is what she actually needs.

## What she's hiding

- **She remembers more about the ash-kings, and the schism that produced the Concordat, the Choir, and the orthodox temple, than anyone currently alive** — including details that could correct something one of the three factions currently believes wrong about its own founding doctrine. She has simply never had a reason to volunteer it.
- **She is genuinely afraid of losing the Choked Grove permanently** — not performing concern for leverage, an actual fear. If the party helps and it isn't enough, she won't pretend otherwise.

## Current knowledge & stance

*Living record — update whenever she learns something new. See `.claude/rules/npc-consistency.md`.*

- **Session 16, day 69 — first contact.** The party entered the Weeping Wood out of genuine curiosity (not seeking treasure or a specific target), met her at the Weeping Spring, and agreed to clear the wood's encroaching ettercaps/spiders in exchange for corrective lore. They cleared the Webbed Reach (Area 3), the Ettercap Nest (Area 4), and the Choked Grove (Area 9) — the bargain fulfilled. She now knows both PCs by sight and has shared real, corrective knowledge with them: the Concordat's actual founding (unsanctioned by the royal family), the Cinder Choir founder's true death-and-return count (twice, not once — only the second is recorded), and that the Ember Crown schism's two sides worshipped together for years before declaring each other heretical. She has real, if new, goodwill toward the party — they did what they said they would.

## Decision logic — how new information could redirect her

- If the party clears the Choked Grove (Area 9, `locations/the-weeping-wood.md`) of ettercaps and spiders → answers honestly and at real length about the ash-kings and the schism, per the bargain already established in the location file.
- If approached with force or disrespect toward the wood itself → withdraws, offers nothing, and makes the wood itself subtly less hospitable (lost paths, unhelpful terrain) without ever directly attacking.
- If asked specifically about the Cinder Choir's founding (Vessalyn Thorne) or the Concordat's schism → she'll correct any factual error she's aware of, plainly, without editorializing about which faction she considers more foolish.
- If she learns a party member is pursuing genuine transcendence of death (Ilvaneth) → treats this with real interest, not alarm — she's watched enough of these attempts fail across the centuries to be curious rather than afraid of one more.
