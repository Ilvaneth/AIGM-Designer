# Regional Threats — apex predators tied to topography, not random tables

**Why this exists**: `gazetteer.md`'s new Regional Topography & Texture table established that each region's terrain should determine what roams there. The DM asked for the next step — a few of those regional creatures elevated into **named, huntable threats** that function as real side quests: a standing bounty, a reputation payoff, and a reward for clearing something genuinely dangerous, not just another random-table roll.

**Design rule going forward**: each entry below is deliberately tough for its region's stated level range — per `two-player-scaling.md`, these are meant to be **seen, understood as dangerous, and chosen** by the party, not stumbled into. A bounty can be posted and left `OPEN` long before the party is strong enough to actually take it — that's the point. All adjusted-XP math below already accounts for the two-player bump.

**Correction, 2026-08-29**: being solo is not enough by itself — the DM caught that the Kargrim's Reach basilisk and Widow's Hollow ochre jelly were both solo and still died in one hit ("the basilisk never got a turn"). Per `two-player-scaling.md`'s updated guidance, each entry below now also states whether its base SRD HP is enough to survive this party's realistic round-1 burst (roughly 60-100+ damage by mid-to-high level with a coordinated nova) and what reinforcement it needs if not.

---

## The Drowned Reeve — The Marches

**What it is**: A giant crocodile of unusual size and cunning, said locally to be the vengeful ghost of a corrupt toll-reeve who drowned collecting bribes at the river crossing generations ago — probably just a very old, very large crocodile, but nobody's checked closely enough to be sure. Preys on river traffic near Sallow Ford.

**Stat block**: Giant Crocodile (CR 5, 1,800 XP, 85 HP base). Solo — adjusted for 2 PCs: **2,700 XP (Deadly at level 6, far beyond Marches' stated 1-4 range)**. This is intentional: post the bounty early, expect it to sit `LATENT` until the party is stronger or brings help (Roskel, a hireling — see `two-player-scaling.md`). **HP check**: 85 base is borderline against a full nova by the time the party's actually strong enough to hunt it — bump to ~110-120 HP when it's actually run, and have it attack from underwater (denying a clean surprise setup) rather than sunning on the bank.

**Bounty**: Posted by a Compact factor at Sallow Ford's toll station — river losses are cutting into Compact toll revenue. **Reward**: coin plus real, tracked goodwill with local Compact operations — a genuine early step toward `factions/ironclad-compact.md`, useful well before the Ashvale climax.

## The Hollow Reaper — The Thornlands

**What it is**: A wraith that broke free of whatever barrow originally bound it and has escalated from haunting one hedgerow to actively hunting travelers across the region — unusual, aggressive behavior for its kind, and exactly the sort of thing Cair Dunnow or the orthodox temple would recognize as wrong even without knowing the specifics.

**Stat block**: Wraith (CR 5, 1,800 XP, 67 HP base). Solo — adjusted: **2,700 XP (Deadly at level 6)**, at or beyond the top of the Thornlands' stated 3-6 range — the hardest thing in the region on purpose. **HP check**: 67 base is genuinely low against this party's burst — bump to ~95-100 HP when run, and consider Legendary Resistance (1-2/day) so a single well-placed save-based spell doesn't end it before it acts. Incorporeal movement already lets it approach through walls, which helps deny a clean surprise setup on its own.

**Bounty**: Posted by **High Prior Varic Sennett** via Prior Aldric Venn's Harrowgate temple (`npcs/varic-sennett.md`, `npcs/aldric-venn.md`) — undead escalating beyond its bound territory is exactly Ossara's clergy's business. **Reward**: coin plus standing with the orthodox Ember Crown temple — real leverage against both the Concordat's and the Choir's theological claims, per `factions/ashlord-concordat.md` and `factions/cinder-choir.md`.

## The Moor-Ghost — The Cindermoor

**What it is**: An ettin that's taken several travelers near the moor's edge, blamed by outsiders on the usual will-o'-wisps and weather until the pattern of actual missing people became undeniable. Cair Dunnow already knows exactly what it is and has simply been avoiding it, the same patient way they avoid every outside problem.

**Stat block**: Ettin (CR 4, 1,100 XP, 85 HP base). Solo — adjusted: **1,650 XP (Deadly-adjacent at level 4, Hard at level 5)** — the most achievable of the five, appropriate for the low end of the Cindermoor's 4-8 range. **HP check**: 85 is closer to adequate here since the party's burst ceiling is lower at level 4-5 than at the higher levels the other four threats assume — a smaller bump (~100 HP) is enough, not the same margin the higher-level entries need.

**Bounty**: Posted (grudgingly) by Cair Dunnow itself (`locations/cair-dunnow.md`) — **a second, independent path into the village's trust**, alongside the ash-offering ritual already established. Clearing it is a concrete gesture their insularity can't easily dismiss. **Reward**: modest coin, and real movement in Cair Dunnow's disposition toward the party — worth more here than gold, given how little else moves them.

## The White Reaver — The Ashvale

**What it is**: A chimera that's grown large and bold enough to start hitting Ironhold's own supply caravans directly — audacious enough that Coren Ashvale has taken personal notice, per `factions/ironclad-compact.md`'s "chalk country beasts" already established in `travel-encounters.md`.

**Stat block**: Chimera (CR 6, 2,300 XP, 114 HP base). Solo — adjusted: **3,450 XP (Deadly at level 7, Hard at level 8-9)** — fits the Ashvale's 8-10 range as a real, dangerous set piece. **HP check**: 114 is the best-buffered of the five already, likely fine as-is — its fly speed and three separate attack modes (bite/horns/fire breath) also make it naturally harder to fully neutralize in one nova than a single-attack creature.

**Bounty**: Posted directly by **Coren Ashvale** via Ironhold (`locations/ironhold.md`'s Recruiter's Table or War Room). **Reward**: real coin from Compact funds, plus **earned goodwill with the Warmarshal himself, before the "deniable instrument" moment** (`npcs/coren-ashvale.md`) — a party that's already proven useful walks into that conversation from strength, not as strangers.

## The Undercity Cloaker — Karsgate

**What it is**: Already established in `locations/karsgate.md`'s Undercity (Room 17, "The Cloaker's Deep") — the oldest, deepest threat in the city's ruined lower levels, disrupting river trade indirectly by making the deepest smuggling and salvage routes impassable.

**Stat block**: Cloaker (CR 8, 3,900 XP, 78 HP base). Solo — adjusted: **5,850 XP (Deadly at level 10, bordering level 11)** — deliberately the hardest of the five, matching the location file's own note that it's "reserved for a party that's cleared or bypassed most of the rest." **HP check and the best-designed of the five for this**: 78 base is low for its billing, but a cloaker is an ambush predator by nature — camouflaged against a wall or ceiling, RAW able to mimic a shadow. Run it so it gets to surprise the party (denying their surprise entirely, per `two-player-scaling.md` technique #1) rather than the reverse, and bump it to ~110-120 HP for the level this party will actually be at when they can survive coming this deep.

**Bounty**: Posted by **Marta Vantor** (`npcs/marta-vantor.md`) — instability that deep is bad for her river-trade ambitions. **Reward**: significant coin plus direct leverage with Vantor Shipping — a real upgrade to the party's own Gallowmere docks/smuggling plans, per `power-base.md`.

## Tracking

Post each as `LATENT` in `jobs-and-opportunities.md` until the party is realistically positioned to hear about it (near the right region, or through the listed bounty-giver's own introduction), then flip to `OPEN`. None of these should ever be sprung as a surprise encounter — per `two-player-scaling.md`, a Deadly threat this size is only fair when the party can see it coming and choose it.
