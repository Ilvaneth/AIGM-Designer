## Description / Read-Aloud Text

> *A twig snaps in the dark past the firelight. Then another. Small shapes move low between the trees — quick, low to the ground, unhurried in the particular way of things that think they haven't been seen.*

### Special Abilities & Conditions

- **Nimble Escape.** Each goblin can Disengage or Hide as a bonus action every turn — they don't stand and trade, they dart in and out.
- **Stealth +6**, darkvision 60 ft. Approached under cover of dark; rolled a group Stealth of 10 against the party's Perception.

---

## Terrain & Environmental Features

| Feature | Description | Mechanical Effect |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Campfire | Low light source | Party is lit; goblins approaching from darkness have concealment until they close |
| Treeline | Surrounds the camp | Half cover once goblins reach it; Nimble Escape lets them retreat into it between attacks |
| Horses | Picketed nearby | Could spook — a complication if the fight goes long |

**Lighting:** Dim (firelight) at the camp, darkness beyond it.

---

## Tactics

**Opening move:** Skirmish — shortbows from the treeline, then scimitars if a target is isolated. Not disciplined soldiers; opportunists testing whether this camp is easy prey.

**Morale:** Breaks at 2 goblins down. The survivors Disengage and flee into the dark rather than fight to the last.
