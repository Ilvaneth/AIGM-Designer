# Encounter: The Ochre Pool — The Widow's Hollow, Room 12

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | The Widow's Hollow, room 12 |
| **Trigger** | Party reaches the dry ledge, must pass the jelly to continue |
| **Encounter Type** | Combat, environmental hazard |

## Enemies

| Name | Count | HP Each | AC | Speed | Initiative |
|------|-------|---------|-----|-------|------------|
| Ochre Jelly | 1 | 45 | 8 | 10 ft., climb 10 ft. | +2 |

### Attack Details

| Enemy | Attack | Bonus | Damage |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Ochre Jelly | Pseudopod | +4 | 2d6+2 bludgeoning + 1d6 acid |

**Split (Reaction):** subjected to lightning or slashing damage while Medium or larger and at 10+ HP, splits into two jellies (half HP each, one size smaller). Fire, bludgeoning, and other damage types do not trigger this.

## Notes

Visible, not hidden — blindsight 60 ft, no stealth. Slow (10 ft speed) — easy to outmaneuver if the party doesn't need to fight it at all.
