# Encounter: The Fungal Grove — The Widow's Hollow, Room 8

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | The Widow's Hollow, room 8 |
| **Trigger** | Party disturbs a disguised Violet Fungus while sampling; Shrieker alarms |
| **Encounter Type** | Combat, stationary ambushers |

## Enemies

| Name | Count | HP Each | AC | Speed | Initiative |
|------|-------|---------|-----|-------|------------|
| Violet Fungus | 1 revealed (1 more disguised, inactive unless approached) | 18 | 5 | 5 ft. | +2 |
| Shrieker | 1 | 13 | 5 | 0 ft. | — (no attacks, alarm only) |

### Attack Details

| Enemy | Attack | Bonus | Damage | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|-------|
| Violet Fungus | Rotting Touch (1d4 attacks/turn) | +2 | 1d8 necrotic each, reach 10 ft | Stationary — 5 ft speed means it can't chase far |

Shrieker: no attack, keeps shrieking (audible 300 ft) until disturbance leaves 30 ft range, +1d4 rounds after.

## Notes

Both fungi have False Appearance — indistinguishable from real fungus while motionless, no check can beat this before contact. The second Violet Fungus stays disguised and inert unless the party moves within its reach or attacks it.
