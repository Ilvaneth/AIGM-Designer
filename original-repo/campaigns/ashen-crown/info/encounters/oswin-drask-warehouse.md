# Encounter: Oswin Drask's Warehouse

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | A converted warehouse near the Gallowmere docks, night of day 75 (continuing straight from the garrison coup) |
| **Trigger** | Party decision — killing Drask to take his money/goods and settle Dallin's old debt |
| **Encounter Type** | Combat — Oswin Drask (Bandit Captain reflavored) + 3 Enforcers (Thugs) |
| **Difficulty** | ~1,875 adjusted XP for a level-7 two-PC party — Hard band |

---

## Description / Read-Aloud Text

> *The warehouse smells of river damp and old rope. A single lantern burns near the back, where a heavyset man counts coin at a scarred table — three others lean against crates nearby, unhurried, the kind of men who've never had to actually hurry.*

---

## Enemies

| Name | Count | HP Each | AC | Speed | Initiative |
|------|-------|---------|-----|-------|------------|
| Oswin Drask | 1 | 65 | 15 | 30 ft. | +3 |
| Enforcer A-C | 3 | 32 | 11 | 30 ft. | +0 |

**Oswin Drask**: SRD Bandit Captain, unmodified — Multiattack (2 scimitar + 1 dagger, or 2 thrown daggers), Parry reaction (+2 AC vs one melee hit).
**Enforcers**: SRD Thug, unmodified — Multiattack (2 mace), Pack Tactics (advantage if an ally is adjacent to the target).

## Tactics

Party's choice whether to announce themselves or attempt stealth — Drask and his men aren't expecting trouble tonight, so a quiet approach has real surprise potential.

## Rewards

### Loot

Drask's own coin (counting money on the table when found), whatever's stored in the warehouse (smuggled goods, presumably), and control of a real dockside location once he's dead — a `power-base.md` opportunity.

### XP

450 (Drask) + 3×100 (Enforcers) = 750 raw ÷ 2 = **375 XP each**.
