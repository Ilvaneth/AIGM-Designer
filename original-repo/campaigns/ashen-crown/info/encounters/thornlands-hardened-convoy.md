## Description / Read-Aloud Text

> *The convoy moves in loose pairs, not a column — six skeletal guards spaced along both wagons, two priests close to the cargo, and out front, alone, a tiefling woman in half-plate who never stops scanning the treeline. She is not relaxed. She is not tired. She is looking for exactly what's hiding in the trees.*

## Tactics

**Partial surprise only, per the DM's own reconnaissance resolution** — Ilvaneth's Stealth (15) beat every observer's passive Perception (13), fully undetected. Kriv's Stealth (10) did not; P1 (one of the regular priests) is already watching his position, suspicious but not yet alarmed. Ysolde and everyone else remain unaware.

- **P1 is not surprised** when combat starts — already alert to Kriv's general position.
- **Everyone else (Ysolde, P2, all 6 Skeletons) is surprised** — roll initiative for the whole encounter first, per `combat-rules.md`'s no-free-round rule, then skip their first turns.
- **Formation is deliberately spread** (see the published recon map) — S1/S2 and S3/S4 are ~40 ft apart, S5/S6 further south; no single 20-ft Fireball sphere catches more than one pair. V1 (center) likely carries the real cargo; V2 (forward, separate) is a plausible decoy.
- Ysolde does not open with spellcasting on a surprised turn (she has none) — her first real action comes on her own initiative slot, likely Spirit Guardians or Guiding Bolt once she's identified a target.
