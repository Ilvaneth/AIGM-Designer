| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | Shestendeliath Hold, the flooded lower cistern |
| **Trigger** | Kriv asked Kethrax to clear every remaining danger in the Hold |

| **Difficulty** | Medium-tier for level 5 two-PC party (CR 2, 450 raw XP — cleared `solo_burst_check.py`'s floor at 67 HP, no inflation needed) |

## Description / Read-Aloud Text

> *The cistern is a flooded chamber, black water standing knee-deep across most of the floor, the ceiling lost in dark above. Something in the water is not still — a mass of shifting flesh, too many mouths, too many eyes, half-submerged near the far wall, murmuring to itself in a language that isn't one.*

Stat block: SRD **Gibbering Mouther** (CR 2, 450 XP), unmodified base stats. Aberrant Ground (10-ft radius doughlike difficult terrain, DC 10 STR save or speed 0). Gibbering (DC 10 WIS save each turn within 20 ft while it can see a creature, or lose reactions + roll a d8 for a random action). Multiattack: Bite (+2 to hit, 5d6 piercing, DC 10 STR save or prone; a Medium-or-smaller creature killed by this is absorbed) + Blinding Spittle (recharge 5-6, DC 13 DEX save or blinded until the end of its next turn).

## Tactics

**Purely a hazard, not plot-connected** — per `shestendeliath-hold.md`. No morale, no negotiation; it attacks whatever's closest.

**Ilvaneth's failed Stealth check (7 vs. its passive Perception 10) alerts it before initiative is rolled** — it is NOT surprised. Kriv's Stealth succeeded (16); per the unseen-attacker rule he has advantage on his first attack roll until he attacks or is otherwise revealed.
