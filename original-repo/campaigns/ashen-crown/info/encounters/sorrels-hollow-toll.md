# Encounter: The Toll

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | The northern road, near Sorrel's Hollow |
| **Trigger** | Party travels the Thornwick road (roll 7 on the Thornlands travel table), or approaches the camp openly |
| **Encounter Type** | **Social first**, combat only if it goes that way |
| **Difficulty** | Hard if fought (2-character party) — 100 raw × 2.5 = **250 adjusted** |
| **Suggested Level** | 2-3 |

---

## Description / Read-Aloud Text

> *Four men step out of the treeline ahead and spread across the road without hurrying — the movement of people who have done this often and have never yet had to run. Their gear is army issue, worn past the point of repair, and the crossbow the youngest is holding is pointed at the ground in a way that says he'd rather keep it there.*
>
> *The one in front raises an open hand.*
>
> *"Road's kept," he says. "Kept costs. Ten a head, or turn around — either's fine by us."*

## Tactics

**Opening move:** They demand the toll. They genuinely prefer payment — it's why the arrangement works.

**Priority targets:** If it turns to violence, the crossbowman shoots the spellcaster. The other three engage whoever's armored.

**When bloodied:** Sergeant's training holds until two are down. Then it breaks.

**Morale:** **They break at two casualties** and run for camp — and if they reach it, the party has a dozen angry armed men and Roskel to deal with. Letting a runner escape has consequences.

**The horn:** The leader carries one. If blown, reinforcements arrive in ~10 rounds: **6 more bandits and Roskel (Veteran, CR 3)**. That is 1,000+ raw XP and unsurvivable for two level-3 PCs. Telegraph the horn.

## DM Notes

- **This encounter's real function is information, not combat.** These men will talk — they're bored, underpaid, and not fanatics. Casual conversation gets: their sergeant served House Vaelthorn twenty years, they deserted when pay stopped, and they'd rather be soldiering than robbing.
- **They can be recruited.** Not here, not by four privates — but this is the party's introduction to a dozen trained men who deserted over money, which is a solvable problem. See `power-base.md` and `two-player-scaling.md`; recruiting even two of them meaningfully fixes this party's action-economy problem.
- **The full camp is not a fight.** 12 bandits + Roskel = ~4,000 adjusted XP for two PCs — Deadly through level 8. If the party wants the camp, they need to negotiate, ambush at night in pieces, turn Roskel, or bring help.
- Killing all four and leaving bodies on the road means Roskel comes looking, and he's competent.
