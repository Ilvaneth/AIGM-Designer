# Encounter: The Barrow Road, Night 1

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | The Thornlands, en route Shestendeliath Hold → The Unquiet Barrow, Night 1 (day 66) |
| **Trigger** | Travel roll (`travel-encounters.md`, Thornlands, Late tier) — Category d12 [2] → Combat (late), Subtable d6 [3] → Undead, escalated, sub-roll d4 [4] → 1x Ghast + 2x Wight (all SRD, unmodified) |
| **Encounter Type** | Combat — the party is caught mid-camp, before Tiny Hut goes up. Both Wights (Stealth 21) and the Ghast (Stealth 22) beat both PCs' passive Perception (Ilvaneth 15, Kriv 11) — **full surprise for the undead, both PCs surprised.** |
| **Difficulty** | 1,850 raw (450+700+700) → ×2 (3 monsters) → ×2.5 (2-PC bump) = **4,625 adjusted — well past Deadly** (2,800 at level 6). Late-tier "escalated" result, honestly rolled — not built past Deadly by design, but landing there by the dice. Real danger: the Ghast's Claws (DC 10 CON save or paralyzed) is the single biggest threat in the opening round. |
