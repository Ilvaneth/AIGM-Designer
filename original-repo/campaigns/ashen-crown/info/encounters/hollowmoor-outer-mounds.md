---

## Scaling note

The site's location file (`info/locations/hollowmoor-barrows.md`) states this whole dungeon as level 4+ raw (level 6+ effective for this two-player party) — this specific outer-mound encounter is intentionally scoped lighter than that, as a fair fight rather than the site's real test. **The deep barrow's guardian (a Wight, CR 3) is a separate, much harder encounter — Deadly on its own for this level-3 party (≈1,050 adjusted XP vs. an 800 threshold) — and must be telegraphed and chosen, not sprung.** Do not scale this outer encounter up to match that; they're deliberately different tiers.

## Description / Read-Aloud Text

> *Two bone-piles against the far wall, too orderly to be debris, uncoil at once — the scrape of ancient armor scraps, the click of a jaw finding its hinge. Empty sockets catch the torchlight.*

### Special Abilities & Conditions

- Vulnerable to bludgeoning damage (double damage from bludgeoning sources).
- Immune to poison damage and the poisoned condition.
- Mindless — no morale, no retreat, no communication. Fight until destroyed.

## Tactics

**Opening move:** Both skeletons animate simultaneously and close to melee — no ranged opening since the party is already close.

**Priority targets:** Nearest target, no tactical preference (mindless).

**Morale:** None. They fight to destruction.

## DM Notes

- This is the "outer guardian" tier — a fair, winnable fight that establishes the site is actively warded without gating the party behind the site's real (much harder) threat.
- **Do not let this fight bleed into a false sense that the deep barrow is equally survivable.** The deep barrow's Wight is a different order of danger — see the scaling note above and `info/locations/hollowmoor-barrows.md`.
