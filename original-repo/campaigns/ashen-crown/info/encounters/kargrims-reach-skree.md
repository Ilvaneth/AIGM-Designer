## Who she is

**Skree** — kobold warren-mother, small even for a kobold, sharp-eyed and constantly calculating. Paranoid in the specific way of someone who's kept her clan alive against a bigger, meaner enemy for a year through wit rather than strength. She postures aggressively at first contact (kobold instinct) but drops it fast if the party doesn't attack on sight.

**What she wants**: the bugbears gone, permanently. She's been sabotaging their water (see location file, room 7) as a slow war of attrition. A party that offers to deal with Groth — or simply proves it already has — gets real cooperation: safe passage through the warren, what she knows about the Shaft's safe winch operation, and a warning about the depths (room 19-20) freely given, because she genuinely doesn't want strangers waking that thing near her tunnels.
