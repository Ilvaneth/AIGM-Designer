# Encounter: The Roost — The Widow's Hollow, Room 4

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | The Widow's Hollow, room 4 |
| **Trigger** | Party passes beneath the crevice roost |
| **Encounter Type** | Combat, ambush predators |
| **Difficulty** | Easy-Medium — 4 × 25 = 100 raw × 2 = **200 adjusted** |

## Enemies

| Name | Count | HP Each | AC | Speed | Initiative |
|------|-------|---------|-----|-------|------------|
| Stirge | 4 | 2 | 14 | 10 ft., fly 40 ft. | +3 |

### Attack Details

| Enemy | Attack | Bonus | Damage | Special |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|---------|
| Stirge | Blood Drain | +5 | 1d4+3 piercing, then attaches | Attached: 1d4+3 HP drain per stirge turn until detached (action to remove) |

Passive Perception 9 — easily surprised by a cautious approach.

## Notes

Roosting, not hunting; they attack anything that passes beneath and don't coordinate. Low HP (2 each) means most hits kill outright. Real danger is multiple attaching to the same target and draining over several rounds if ignored.
