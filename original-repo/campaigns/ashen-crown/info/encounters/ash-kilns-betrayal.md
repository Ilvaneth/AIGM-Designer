**Odric Fenn** — Cleric 6 (Death Domain), built on the SRD Priest block, scaled: Mace +5 to hit, 1d6+2 bludgeoning. **Channel Divinity: Touch of Death** (melee hit adds 1d8 + twice cleric level necrotic — 1d8+12 — 1/rest). Spellcasting (6th-level, WIS, save DC 13, spell attack +5): Cantrips — *sacred flame, light, thaumaturgy*. 1st — *cure wounds, guiding bolt, inflict wounds*. 2nd — *spiritual weapon, spike growth*. 3rd — *animate dead, vampiric touch*.

**Cultists**: unmodified SRD stats (Scimitar +3, 1d6+1).

---

## Tactics

**Total surprise on Fenn** — mid-conversation, trusted the party, no warning. **The 3 cultists are elsewhere on-site (the shed/around the kilns), unaware combat has started** — treat as surprised too unless the party's approach was loud enough to alert them first (it wasn't; this opens from a standing conversation). Fenn, per his own file's "against the party" trigger, would rather release what's caged in the fourth kiln than lose the site if he gets the chance to act — a real risk if the fight runs long enough for him to reach it.
