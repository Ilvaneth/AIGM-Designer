## Description / Read-Aloud Text

> *The great hall is the one room fire didn't finish. The high table still stands, warped and black. Fifteen chairs, fifteen years of dust — except the air here doesn't sit still. It moves against itself, a slow cold current with no source. Something in the dark above the table is watching before you see it — and it isn't alone.*

## Design history

**Session 9, 2026-08-30 (see `bug-log.md`, `combat-geometry`).** Went through three versions before this one, each validated with a disposable-test-campaign live playthrough before being made canon:

1. Single wraith, 95 HP (raised from base 67 to clear a computed burst floor) — still died round 1 for zero party damage.
2. Two wraiths, 80 HP each — ran a real 2-round fight, validated live, briefly made canon.
3. **Three wraiths, 67 HP each (unmodified SRD) — this version.** Live-tested and ran the best fight of the three: a full 4 rounds, genuine round-to-round danger (the party avoided damage only through real luck — several near-misses and a fully-spent Mirror Image), and needed zero HP-inflation math. Confirmed as the standing default going forward in `two-player-scaling.md`: for any "more enemies, not more HP" fix, default to fully unmodified stats and tune difficulty via enemy count.

---

## Tactics

**No surprise by design.** Positioned 20+ ft apart per `two-player-scaling.md`'s group-spacing technique.

**Each wraith prioritizes the lowest-HP or most exposed PC** rather than whoever hit it.

**Kethrax and the other three retainers do not join this fight** — bound to the gate, not this hall.
