# Encounter: The Widow's Hearth

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | The Widow's Hollow, room 15 |
| **Trigger** | Party reaches the hag's hearth room |
| **Encounter Type** | **Social first, by design** — combat is available but not the point |
| **Difficulty** | Hard if fought (700 raw, single monster ×1) |
| **Suggested Level** | 4-5 |

---

## Who she is

A **green hag** (SRD stat block, CR 3) who has lived in these caves longer than anyone in the region has been alive. She has no name she gives out — "the Widow" is the locals' word, not hers, and she finds it funny. She is not evil in a doctrinal sense and not aligned with anything bigger than her own comfort; she trades, she trades fairly *by her own definition* of fair, and that definition has teeth.

**Physical presence**: when she chooses to be seen as she is — hunched, warty, ancient, eyes like wet river stones. More often she wears an Illusory Appearance: an old woman, kindly-looking, exactly unthreatening enough to make people relax.

## Voice

Warm, amused, unhurried. Never raises her voice. Laughs easily, including at herself. The warmth is real — she genuinely likes company — which is what makes her dangerous; nobody braces for a threat that seems delighted to see them.

## What she wants

Not power, not doctrine, not the Crown, not either PC's ascension — **she wants a good bargain and a good story**, in roughly that order. She's bored in the way only someone centuries old in a cave can be bored. Guests are rare and precious.

## Opening line

*"Well. It's been a season of Sundays since anyone clever enough to find the old cache bothered to keep walking. Sit — I don't bite the ones who ask nicely."*

## The bargain

She'll offer real value — a look at the hoard's contents, safe passage back out, even a minor boon (advantage on one future save, a cryptic true answer to one question, something small and real) — in exchange for something that costs the party more than they'd expect at first:

- A **name**, freely given (hers to use later — a hag's classic price; low real risk for this campaign since she has no connection to anything that would exploit it, but let it feel weighty at the table)
- A **story** — something true and personal the party hasn't told anyone else (this is the "good story" she actually wants; a genuine roleplay beat is worth more to her than coin)
- A **memento** — something small and meaningful, not valuable. She's not a fence; she wants a token, not a price tag.

**She never lies about the terms.** Everything she offers, she delivers exactly as stated — the unease is in how the exchange feels, not in being cheated.

## If it goes to violence

She doesn't want to fight and says so plainly — but she also isn't afraid to. If pressed or attacked:

- **Opens** with *Invisible Passage*, vanishing to reposition and cast *vicious mockery* from an unexpected angle.
- **Illusory Appearance** may already be active before the fight starts — if the party attacks the "kindly old woman" they saw, the illusion doesn't hold up to a hit landing (it's cosmetic only, not defensive), but it can cause a round of hesitation worth playing for real.
- **Bloodied** (41 HP or less): drops the pleasant tone entirely, just once — *"Oh, you *do* mean it. How disappointing."* — then fights to actually win, no longer holding back.
- **On downing a PC**: doesn't gloat. *"There. That's done now. Shall we talk again, or is this how it ends?"* — genuinely offers a chance to stop.
- **Death**: no melodrama. *"...huh."* — mild surprise, nothing more. She's lived a very long time and this was always possible.

## Tactics if fighting

- Claws (+6, 2d8+4) when melee is unavoidable.
- Uses *dancing lights* and *minor illusion* to create confusion in the dark, not damage.
- Retreats via *Invisible Passage* if bloodied and disengaging is viable — she'd rather vanish and let the party leave with less than die for a cave.
- Does **not** summon the rest of the dungeon's monsters — they tolerate her, they don't serve her.

---

## Rewards

### If bargained with (no fight)
Whatever was agreed — access to room 16's hoard is a fair, generous outcome for a good story or an honest bargain. XP awarded in full per `progression.md`'s non-combat policy.

### If fought and won
| Enemy | XP |
|-------|-----|
| Green Hag | 700 |
| **Per character** (÷2) | **350** |

Room 16's hoard is available either way once she's dealt with — dead, bargained-with, or driven off.

### If the party simply leaves without engaging her
Also valid. She doesn't chase people who walk away politely. No hoard access this way, but no XP loss or judgment either — a cave that never has to be resolved is still a real choice.

---

## DM Notes

- **The whole point of this room is that it doesn't need to be a fight.** If the players lean into talking, reward that generously — a great story from the table is worth more than mechanical treasure, and she should treat it that way too.
- **Nothing here connects to the Ashen Crown, the three factions, or either PC's personal arc — on purpose.** Resist the urge to retroactively wire her into the main plot unless the table specifically wants that later.
