# Encounter: What the Water Kept

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | The Drowned Mill, flooded ground floor |
| **Trigger** | Party enters the water, makes significant noise above, or falls through the second floor |
| **Encounter Type** | Combat |
| **Difficulty** | Deadly at level 3, Hard at level 4 (2-character party) — 400 raw × 2 = **800 adjusted** |
| **Suggested Level** | 4 |

---

## Description / Read-Aloud Text

> *The ground floor is a black pool, waist-deep and colder than the night outside. Grain sacks float in it, swollen and split. Your light throws everything back at you off the surface.*
>
> *Then two shapes stand up out of the water where nothing was standing before — grey, hairless, and far too thin, water sheeting off shoulders that shouldn't hold that much weight. They don't blink. They were down there the whole time.*

| Ghoul | Claws | +4 | 2d4+2 (avg 7) | Slashing | DC 10 CON save or **paralyzed 1 min** |

### Special Abilities & Conditions

- **Paralysis:** On a claw hit, DC 10 CON save or paralyzed for 1 minute, repeating the save at the end of each of its turns. Attacks against a paralyzed creature within 5 ft are **automatic critical hits**.
- **★ Elves are immune.** Ghoul paralysis does not affect elves. **Ilvaneth cannot be paralyzed by these.** Kriv can — and if he goes down paralyzed in the water with two ghouls on him, he is dead in about two rounds.
- **Damage immunity:** poison. **Condition immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, poisoned.

## Terrain & Environmental Features

| Feature | Description | Mechanical Effect |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Waist-deep water | Fills the entire floor | **Difficult terrain** for the party. Ghouls are unaffected — they've been in it for years |
| Floating debris | Grain sacks, broken timber | Half cover (+2 AC) if used deliberately |
| The stairs | South end, rising out of the water | The only retreat. Ghouls will pursue up them |
| Drowning risk | A paralyzed creature in waist-deep water | If a paralyzed character falls prone, they begin drowning — see `07 combat.md` |

**Lighting:** Total darkness. Ghouls have darkvision 60 ft; the party does not, except Ilvaneth (60 ft).

**Map Notes:** ~30×20 ft, water throughout. Stairs south. Grain chute from above lands mid-room.

---

## Tactics

**Opening move:** Both ghouls close on the nearest target and lead with **claws**, not bite — they want something paralyzed, not dead.

**Priority targets:** They go for whoever they can paralyze. Once they learn (in-fiction, quickly) that the elf doesn't go down, both switch to Kriv.

**When bloodied:** They don't retreat. They fight harder — hunger, not tactics.

**Morale:** None. Fight to destruction. They will pursue up the stairs and out of the mill, but not into daylight.

## DM Notes

- **Do not run this at level 3 for two PCs unless they've chosen it knowingly.** 800 adjusted XP is exactly Deadly at level 3.
- **Avoidable.** The stirges and the strongbox are upstairs; the ghouls are down. A careful party never comes down here. Reward that.
- The elf-immunity is a genuine tactical gift — let Ilvaneth's player discover it in play rather than announcing it. Kriv holding the stairs while she works the water is the fight this room wants.
- These were the smuggling crew whose cache is upstairs. If the party reads the ledger, they'll realize they just killed the men they're looting.
