# Encounter: The Weeping Tower — Harn's Bound Guardian

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | The Weeping Tower entrance/lower rooms, marsh country west of Gallowmere |
| **Trigger** | Party enters the tower |
| **Encounter Type** | Social-first, explicitly — the guardian challenges before it fights |
| **Difficulty** | Deadly-and-then-some on its own for this level-4 two-player party — CR 4 (1,100 raw) × 1.5 = **1,650 adjusted**, well past the level-4 Deadly threshold (1,000). **Do not run as a forced fight.** |

---

## Who/what it is

Sixty years dead, Ovelt Harn bound something to guard his work before the end — reflavor an SRD **Ghost** (CR 4) as the preserved, still-dutiful remnant of an apprentice or familiar spirit Harn anchored here on purpose, not a random haunting. It is intelligent, can speak, and is following instructions rather than raging blindly. It is not evil — it's doing a job it was given six decades ago, faithfully, past the point anyone would blame it for stopping.

**What it wants**: to know who's in front of it. Student or thief — that's the actual test. It has no stake in violence for its own sake.

## Social path — the default, per the location file

> **Challenge**: it manifests calmly, asks who they are and why they've come. Lying is possible and can work — this is a Deception/Persuasion moment, not an automatic-fail — but a bad answer or hostile first move triggers real combat.

If Ilvaneth identifies herself honestly as a wizard pursuing the same question Harn spent his life on (preserving the self past death), that's a true answer the guardian can recognize as legitimate — a strong, even automatic opening rather than requiring a hard-won roll, since it matches exactly what it was built to watch for.

## Stat block

**1 Ghost** (SRD, CR 4, 1,100 XP), reflavored as Harn's bound spirit-guardian. AC 11, HP 45, flies (hover), incorporeal. Withering Touch (+5, 4d6+3 necrotic), Horrifying Visage (DC 13 WIS or frightened, possible aging on a bad fail), Possession (DC 13 CHA). Resistant to nonmagical physical damage — **this fight is very bad news for Kriv's mundane approach** if it comes to blows; the Barrow-King's Blade (radiant, magical) actually bypasses the resistance where a normal weapon wouldn't.

## If it comes to violence

- **Horrifying Visage and Possession are both severe against a 2-person party** — a possessed PC is a PC turned into an extra enemy attacker. Telegraph this danger physically (the guardian's voice changing, its shape flickering with hostile intent) before it triggers, per `combat-rules.md`'s established practice of not springing save-or-suck effects with zero warning.
- **This is exactly the kind of Deadly-they-can-see-coming fight** `two-player-scaling.md` describes — real retreat/de-escalation options should stay open even mid-negotiation, not just before it starts.

## Rewards

- **Harn's Spellbook** — several spells above Ilvaneth's current level, real transcription cost. The genuine prize.
- **Research notes** — a mechanical/narrative goldmine for Ilvaneth's personal arc (per `locations/the-weeping-tower.md`): the campaign's clearest look yet at what her ambition actually costs, and (discoverable, not stated outright) the hint that a Crown fragment is the missing variable Harn never got to test.
- Alchemical supplies, minor.
- **Award full XP (1,100, split 550/character) whether resolved by honest conversation or combat** — matching this table's established precedent (talking past a guardian is worth the same as beating it).
