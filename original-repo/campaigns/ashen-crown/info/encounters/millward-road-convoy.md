# Encounter: Karsgate Road — Concordat Convoy (Ambush)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | The Karsgate road, en route to Millward Crossing (session 6, night 2) |
| **Trigger** | Party ambushes a Concordat convoy suspected of carrying a new Ashen Crown fragment |
| **Encounter Type** | Combat, travel ambush — improvised, not a prepared dungeon site |
| **Difficulty** | 6 Skeletons (50 XP each) + 2 Priests (450 XP each) = 1,200 raw × 2 (two-player bump) = **2,400 adjusted** — Deadly on paper, mitigated heavily by a full-surprise alpha strike (same shape as the session 3 convoy ambush, which the party wiped with zero casualties) |

## Enemies

| Name | Count | HP Each | AC | Speed | Initiative |
|------|-------|---------|-----|-------|------------|
| Skeleton | 6 | 13 | 13 | 30 ft. | +2 |
| Priest | 2 | 27 | 13 | 25 ft. | +0 |

Skeleton: Shortsword +4/1d6+2 piercing, or Shortbow +4/1d6+2 piercing (80/320 ft). Vulnerable to bludgeoning, immune to poison, immune to exhaustion/poisoned.

Priest: Mace +2/1d6 bludgeoning. Divine Eminence (bonus action, expend a spell slot for +3d6 radiant on a hit, +1d6 per slot level above 1st). 5th-level spellcaster (cantrips at will: light, sacred flame, thaumaturgy; 1st: cure wounds, guiding bolt, sanctuary; 2nd: lesser restoration, spiritual weapon; 3rd: dispel magic, spirit guardians). Spell save DC 13, +5 spell attack.

## What's here

A sealed wagon matching the warding style of the fragment shipment ambushed in session 3 — strongly suggestive, not confirmed, of a second Ashen Crown fragment shipment. Formation: 2 skeletons vanguard, 2 priests flanking the wagon, 2 skeletons rear guard, tightly clustered (all within ~10 ft of each other at the front).

## Rewards

### Treasure
If the wagon does carry a fragment: a third Ashen Crown fragment, plus whatever mundane valuables the priests carry (holy symbols, coin).
