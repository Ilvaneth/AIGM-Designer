# Encounter: Harpy Flight, the Cindermoor Road

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | A rocky pass on the road to Kesh Deeps, the Cindermoor, night 2 of travel (day 54) |
| **Trigger** | Travel roll, Combat category, Early tier |
| **Encounter Type** | Combat — 3 harpies, unmodified SRD stats |
| **Difficulty** | Hard for level 5 two-PC party (3× CR 1, 600 raw XP, ×2.5 multiplier for 2 PCs = 1,500 adjusted) |

---

## Description / Read-Aloud Text

> *Wingbeats echo off the rock walls before you see them — three shapes dropping from the dark above, women's faces on vulture bodies, talons catching the moonlight. One opens its mouth and a melody spills out, warm and inviting and completely wrong coming from that throat.*

---

## Enemies

| Name | Count | HP Each | AC | Speed | Initiative |
|------|-------|---------|-----|-------|------------|
| Harpy A | 1 | 38 | 11 | 20 ft., fly 40 ft. | +1 |
| Harpy B | 1 | 38 | 11 | 20 ft., fly 40 ft. | +1 |
| Harpy C | 1 | 38 | 11 | 20 ft., fly 40 ft. | +1 |

Stat block: SRD **Harpy** (CR 1, 200 XP each), unmodified. Multiattack (claws + club). **Luring Song**: DC 11 WIS save or charmed for any humanoid within 300 ft that can hear it — charmed target must move toward the harpy each turn, incapacitated. A bonus action each turn to continue singing; ends if the harpy is incapacitated. A target that saves is immune to that harpy's song for 24 hours.

---

## Tactics

No surprise either way — open ground, night, both sides perceived each other closing. Harpies open with Luring Song from range (they prefer to lure a target off a ledge/into bad footing before closing to melee) — the rocky pass terrain makes a charmed target's forced movement toward the harpy a real fall/positioning risk, not just flavor.

## Rewards

### Loot

Feathers from all three harpies, harvested carefully post-combat — alchemical-grade, sellable to Cole (Vestry & Cole, Harrowgate).

### XP

600 raw ÷ 2 = **300 XP each**.
