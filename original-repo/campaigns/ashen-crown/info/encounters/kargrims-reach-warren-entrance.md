| **Difficulty** | 2 Kobolds × 25 = 50 raw × 2 (two-player bump) = **100 adjusted** — trivial in a straight fight, real danger is the alarm |

## Who's here

Two kobold sentries on watch, propped timbers and old/new tunnel supports around them. Cowardly alone, dangerous together (Pack Tactics) — their real job is to flee and warn Skree's Warren (room 16), not to win a fight. A party that lets even one escape should expect room 16 to know they're coming before they arrive.

## Enemies

| Name | Count | HP Each | AC | Speed | Initiative |

Standard Kobold (same stat block as `kargrims-reach-skree.md`): Dagger +4/1d4+2, Sling +4/1d4+2, Pack Tactics (advantage on an attack roll if an ally is within 5 ft of the target), Sunlight Sensitivity (disadvantage on attack rolls and Perception checks relying on sight in sunlight — irrelevant underground).

## Tactics

If they notice the party before being noticed, one breaks immediately to run for room 16 while the other tries to delay (or also flees). If surprised/killed before either can act, no alarm reaches Skree — this is the stealth payoff for approaching quietly.
