## Tactics

**Mixed surprise, per individual awareness**: Duergar A is alerted (heard Ilvaneth's misstep) and is NOT surprised — actively searching. Duergar B, C, D have noticed nothing and ARE surprised. Kriv remains unseen (Stealth 21) — his first attack this fight gets advantage as an unseen attacker regardless of the surprise round's outcome, until he attacks or is otherwise revealed.

Per `two-player-scaling.md`'s standing group-spacing technique, the four are spread across the chamber (working stations, not clustered) — no single AoE catches all four at once.
