## Tactics

**Surprised.** Per successful party Stealth (Ilvaneth 16 vs his passive Perception, Kriv noticed but not identified), Marsh does not know he's being followed until the attack lands. Roll initiative for everyone per `combat-rules.md`'s standing rule — surprise means he loses his first turn, not that initiative is skipped.

**If he somehow survives to act**: tries to flee (Cunning Action Dash) rather than fight — he's a dealer, not a killer, and knows it. Would surrender/bargain if cornered rather than die, per his own self-interest (see `npcs/marsh.md` if built out further).

**Morale**: none needed if surprise + a level 5 party's opening action resolves him in round 1, which is the expected outcome.

## Rewards

### Loot

Whatever he's actually carrying — his deerskin bag (mentioned on arrival at the tavern), coin, and possibly the lowballed heirlooms he bought off the Verrick family 3 weeks ago (grandmother's tiara, father's seal) if he still has them rather than having already sold them on. Resolve on search, don't assume contents ahead of the actual search.

### XP

200 raw ÷ 2 = **100 XP each** (standard split, per `progression.md`).

## DM Notes

This is the party's own premeditated choice, stated explicitly OOC — per `dm-notes.md`'s standing content rule (no lines/veils, this table confirmed gore/cruelty in scope for two Neutral Evil PCs), run it straight: real initiative, real rolls, real consequences (a body, noise, what happens to it after), not softened or moralized. `new-capabilities.md`'s intake questions still apply after the fact — witnesses, physical evidence, does anyone come looking for a dealer who never returns from his rounds.
