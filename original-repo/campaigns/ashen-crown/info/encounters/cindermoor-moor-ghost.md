# Encounter: The Moor-Ghost's Den

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | A collapsed barrow-mound, half a day east of Cair Dunnow, the Cindermoor |
| **Trigger** | Self-directed — the party offered to clear Cair Dunnow's Moor-Ghost bounty (`regional-threats.md`, `jobs-and-opportunities.md`), Nairne Thistle gave real directions. Both PCs stealthed in successfully (Ilvaneth 17, Kriv 20, vs. the ettin's passive Perception 14) — full surprise. |
| **Encounter Type** | Combat — 1 Ettin, surprised |
| **Difficulty** | 1,100 raw → ×1.5 (solo, 2-PC bump) = **1,650 adjusted** (Medium-Hard band at level 7, per `two-player-scaling.md`) — but resolved with full surprise against a party well above the bounty's intended level (4-5); a quick, decisive win is the expected shape here, not a set piece. |

## Enemies

| Name | Count | HP Each | AC | Speed | Initiative |
|------|-------|---------|-----|-------|------------|
| The Moor-Ghost (Ettin) | 1 | 85 | 12 | 40 ft. | -1 |

Stat block: SRD **Ettin** (CR 4, 1,100 XP). Two Heads: advantage on Perception checks and saves vs. blinded/charmed/deafened/frightened/stunned/unconscious. Wakeful: never fully asleep. Multiattack: Battleaxe (+7, 2d8+5 slashing) and Morningstar (+7, 2d8+5 piercing). Passive Perception 14.

**Surprised** — loses its first turn in the initiative order (per `combat-rules.md`: initiative is still rolled for it, it just doesn't act on round 1 until its surprise ends).

## Rewards

### XP
1,100 ÷ 2 = 550 each.

### Bounty payoff
Real movement in Cair Dunnow's disposition toward the party (Nairne Thistle, `npcs/nairne-thistle.md`) — per her own Decision logic, this is worth more here than coin. Modest coin/goods possible from the den itself (victims' scavenged effects), to be determined from what's actually found.
