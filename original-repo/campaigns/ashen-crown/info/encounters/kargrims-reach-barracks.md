# Encounter: The Barracks — Kargrim's Reach, Room 4

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | Kargrim's Reach, room 4 |
| **Trigger** | Party attacks the off-duty bugbear squad |
| **Encounter Type** | Combat, surprise available |
| **Difficulty** | 3 Bugbears × 200 = 600 raw × 2 = **1,200 adjusted** — Medium-Hard at level 5 |

## Enemies

| Name | Count | HP Each | AC | Speed | Initiative |
|------|-------|---------|-----|-------|------------|
| Bugbear | 3 | 27 | 16 | 30 ft. | +2 |

### Attack Details

| Enemy | Attack | Bonus | Damage |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Bugbear | Morningstar | +4 | 2d8+2 piercing (Brute — extra die included) |
| Bugbear | Javelin | +4 | 2d6+2 melee / 1d6+2 ranged (30/120 ft) |

**Surprise Attack**: any bugbear that gets surprised and is hit in round 1 takes an extra 2d6 — this cuts both ways if the party is spotted first.

## Notes

Off-duty, relaxed, not expecting a fight — good odds for a surprise round if the party is quiet approaching. If the fight goes loud, room 3's gatehouse guards (2 more bugbears) may hear and investigate within a few rounds.
