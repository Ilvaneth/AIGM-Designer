# Encounter: The Fourth Kiln

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | The Ash-Kilns, upriver from Gallowmere — the shed and kiln yard, at night |
| **Trigger** | Party investigates the kilns after dark, or is caught by Fenn during the day |
| **Encounter Type** | Mixed — infiltration, then social or combat |
| **Difficulty** | Hard (2-character party) — 550 raw × 2.5 = **1,375 adjusted** |
| **Suggested Level** | 5 |

---

## Description / Read-Aloud Text

> *Three brick kilns stand cold in the yard, their mouths black. The fourth — the one furthest back, half-hidden by a stack of cordwood — is lit, and has been for some time; heat comes off it in a wave you can feel from thirty feet.*
>
> *There's a shed against the far wall with a light in it, and voices, low and even, in something that isn't quite a conversation. It has the shape of a recitation.*
>
> *The yard between you and the shed is ankle-deep in grey ash. There is no way to cross it quietly.*

### Special Abilities & Conditions

- **Fenn — Spellcasting (WIS, DC 11):** *command*, *inflict wounds*, *hold person*, *spiritual weapon*. **Hold person on Kriv is the fight-ender** — telegraph it.
- **Fenn — Dark Devotion:** advantage on saves vs charmed or frightened.
- **Ghast — Stench:** DC 10 CON save on starting turn within 5 ft or **poisoned** until the start of your next turn.
- **Ghast — Paralysis:** as ghoul, but **elves are NOT immune to a ghast's paralysis**. Ilvaneth is vulnerable here — a deliberate reversal of the Drowned Mill.
- **The ghast is caged**, not loose. It only enters the fight if the kiln is opened — by the party, or by Fenn if he's losing.

**Lighting:** Dim from the lit kiln; darkness elsewhere.

**Map Notes:** Yard ~50×40 ft. Three cold kilns north, lit kiln northwest behind cordwood, shed south. River path east, town road west.

## Tactics

**Opening move:** Fenn talks first. He genuinely believes he's the reasonable one, and if the party is carrying a fragment he wants to know *whether they have died* before he decides anything.

**Priority targets:** Fenn opens with *hold person* on the armored one. Cultists swarm whoever is held.

**When bloodied:** Fenn falls back to the fourth kiln and pulls the bar. He would rather release what's inside than lose the site.

**Morale:** Cultists break if Fenn falls. The ghast doesn't break.

## DM Notes

- **Fenn is not a raving fanatic.** He is a man who burns the town's dead and has concluded, reasonably, that someone must return. Play him calm, sad, and certain. He is more unsettling that way.
- **The fragment changes this encounter.** If Fenn learns the party carries one, his doctrine says they are heretics — *unless one of them has died and returned*. That question is a live conversational thread and an enormous piece of future leverage. See `faction-subplots.md`.
- **This can be resolved without a fight.** Fenn can be bargained with, blackmailed with his own records, or turned. A party that takes the kilns *with* Fenn still running them has a cult cell working for them — which is a far better outcome for this party than a pile of bodies.
- The ghast is the Choir's attempt to *manufacture* a returner. It failed. Fenn knows it failed and keeps it anyway.
