# Encounter: The Rafters

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Location** | The Drowned Mill, second floor |
| **Trigger** | Party reaches the second floor, or makes noise on the stairs |
| **Encounter Type** | Combat |
| **Difficulty** | Hard (2-character party) — 100 raw × 2.5 = **250 adjusted** |
| **Suggested Level** | 2-3 (for this two-person party; would be Easy for four PCs) |

---

## Description / Read-Aloud Text

> *The stairs come up out of the black water into air that's barely better — thick, still, smelling of rot and old grain. The second floor is dry, at least: warped boards, a collapsed grain chute, and a ceiling lost in shadow above the reach of your light.*
>
> *Something up there shifts. Then something else. A dry, leathery rustle, like a purse being turned inside out — and it is coming from several places at once.*

### Special Abilities & Conditions

- **Stirge — Blood Drain / Attach:** On a hit the stirge attaches. While attached it stops attacking, but at the start of each of its turns the target automatically loses 1d4+3 HP. No save. It detaches after draining 10 HP total, or when the target dies. A creature can use its **action** to detach one.
- **This is the danger.** Two stirges attached to Ilvaneth (8 max HP) kill her in two rounds with no attack rolls required. Spending an action to pull one off means not fighting the other three.

## Terrain & Environmental Features

| Feature | Description | Mechanical Effect |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Rotted flooring | Boards give near the north wall | DC 12 DEX save or fall through to the flooded ground floor — **which wakes the ghouls** |
| Collapsed grain chute | Diagonal shaft to the ground floor | Usable as cover, or an escape route down |
| Low rafters | Beams at ~8 ft | Stirges gain no cover but can break line of sight |

**Lighting:** Darkness. Party light source only. Stirges have darkvision 60 ft.

**Map Notes:** ~30×20 ft. Stairs at the south end, strongbox wedged in the rafters at the northwest. One exit besides the stairs: the grain chute.

---

## Tactics

**Opening move:** Two stirges drop on round 1, targeting whoever is holding the light. The other two hang back a round, then dive.

**Priority targets:** Whoever is smallest and least armored — mechanically, Ilvaneth. They are animals; they go for easy blood.

**When bloodied:** They don't reason. An attached stirge fights nothing; a detached one that has fed flies for the rafters and does not return.

**Morale:** Once two are killed, the remaining two disengage after one more round and retreat to the rafters. They will not pursue out of the mill.

## DM Notes

- The strongbox is **above** the fight — a party that grabs it and runs can skip the ghouls entirely.
- If a character falls through the floor (rotted flooring, DC 12), they land in the flooded ground floor and the **ghouls wake**. Run `drowned-mill-ghouls.md` immediately, with the party split across two floors. That is a genuinely lethal situation for two PCs — telegraph the bad boards clearly before anyone steps on them.
- Attached stirges are the teaching moment for this party's action-economy problem. Let it land, but not fatally: `two-player-scaling.md`.
