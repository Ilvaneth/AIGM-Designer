| **Difficulty** | 3 Bugbears × 200 = 600 raw × 2 (two-player bump) = **1,200 adjusted** — Medium-Hard at level 5 |

## Who he is

**Groth Half-Ear** — a bugbear raider captain, missing the top half of his left ear (a kobold dart, years ago, and the reason his hatred of them is personal now, not just territorial). Loud, blunt, genuinely dangerous, not stupid — he reads a losing fight and disengages rather than dying for pride, unlike his fresher recruits.

**Surprise Attack**: if any bugbear here surprises the party and hits in round 1, that attack deals an extra 2d6.

## Tactics

Groth fights loud and confident until bloodied (14 HP or less), then reassesses — genuinely willing to negotiate or flee rather than die, especially if his bodyguards have already fallen. He respects strength shown plainly; a party that's clearly winning may get an offer instead of a death fight.

## Rewards

### Treasure
**Groth's morningstar** — plain-looking but old dwarven make, engraved beneath the grime. Worth real money to a collector or smith who recognizes the work (Groth has no idea what he's carrying).
