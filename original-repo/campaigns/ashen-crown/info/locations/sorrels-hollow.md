# Sorrel's Hollow

**Where**: A wooded draw off the northern road, roughly halfway between Gallowmere and Thornwick
**Difficulty**: Level 2-4
**Type**: Bandit camp — humanoid enemies, moderate treasure, and a personal thread for Kriv

**⚠ Two-character party**: this range assumes four PCs. For Ilvaneth and Kriv read it as roughly **+2 levels** — see `../two-player-scaling.md`.

## What it is

A camp of about a dozen **deserters** from the succession war — not monsters, not raiders by trade, but soldiers who walked away from an army that stopped paying them and discovered the refugee road was easier money than the front. They rob travelers, shake down traders, and have taken to charging "tolls."

They are people. They have a cook, arguments, a dog. This should be a fight with faces on it — which matters more for an evil party than a good one, because nothing about it is morally simple in the party's favor either.

## Who leads them

**Sergeant Dain Roskel** — a former **House Vaelthorn** man-at-arms. Twenty years in service to the Vaelthorn line, discharged into nothing when the succession collapsed.

**Roskel is the Kriv thread.** He served in the capital during the years when houses were being broken by intrigue. He didn't orchestrate anything — he was a sergeant, not a schemer — but he *was there*, he saw who came and went, and he remembers which houses fell and roughly in what order and at whose word. He is a living witness to the period in which Kriv's family was destroyed.

He will not hand this over for free. He might trade it. He might be beaten into it. He might, if handled with unusual care, be recruited — a veteran sergeant and a dozen soldiers is precisely the muscle a party building a power base needs.

## What's in it

**The danger**: ~12 deserters, one sergeant, poor equipment but real training and discipline. A straight fight at level 2-3 is genuinely dangerous. Negotiation, ambush at night, or turning them are all viable and more interesting.

**The treasure**: Accumulated toll money, stolen trade goods, decent (if worn) military gear, and Roskel's own kit — including a Vaelthorn house token he never threw away.

## Why it matters beyond loot

- **Hesta Bram** in Thornwick wants them gone; clearing or removing them buys her loyalty outright, and with it the crypt eyewitness account. See `info/npcs/hesta-bram.md`.
- **Corran** has paid them before to lean on a competitor, and would rather the party didn't find that out. See `info/npcs/corran.md`.
- **Recruiting them instead of killing them** is the single fastest way for the party to acquire actual armed force in Chapter 1. See `info/power-base.md`.
- Roskel knows something about Kriv's house. Killing him closes that door.

## The interweave

A bandit camp — a standard, expected D&D encounter — that is simultaneously: a favor a key NPC wants done, a secret another NPC wants kept, a recruitment opportunity, and the only living lead on the campaign's biggest personal question. Whatever the party does here costs them one of the other options.
