# Stonebridge

**Region**: IV. Karsgate (the river crossing on the Karsgate road, the last real chokepoint before the city walls)
**Level range**: 5-10 (Karsgate's own range) — a checkpoint scene first, real fight potential only if it escalates
**Site type**: A single fixed location, city-watch controlled (not faction-owned)

## Concept

A genuinely old stone bridge — older than the current walls, old enough that nobody in Karsgate remembers who actually built it — carrying the Karsgate road across the river a half-day out from the city. Every wagon, courier, and traveler coming from Harrowgate or Millward Crossing crosses here; there's no practical way around it without a multi-day detour. That makes it the natural place for Karsgate's city watch to run its actual checkpoint, rather than at the gates themselves where the volume is too high to check anyone carefully.

**Not faction-owned** — this is deliberate. The watch here answers to Magistrate Ilvarra Sonn's nominal authority, not the Concordat or the Compact. But per `npcs/orell-tain.md`'s own testimony this session (Sarelle's operational hardening after two lost convoys), Concordat agents have been leaning on the watch captain here to flag anyone matching certain descriptions — including, unconfirmed to the party, "an elf and a dragonborn." The checkpoint itself stays officially neutral; the pressure behind it isn't.

## What's here

- **The Checkpoint** — a manned barrier at the bridge's near end, four watch guards on a normal shift, a log book of who's crossed and when. Routine, unless a name or description raises a flag.
- **The Old Piers** — worn stone footings from an earlier, narrower version of the bridge, still standing in the shallows on either side. Passable on foot at low water — a way across (or around the checkpoint entirely) for someone willing to get wet and be seen doing something suspicious.
- **Watch Captain Rennit Dhale** — runs this post. Not corrupt exactly, but tired and underpaid enough that a genuine bribe or a good enough story goes a long way; the Concordat's flagging request sits in his log as one more administrative annoyance among many, not a personal cause.

## Watch Captain Rennit Dhale

Half-orc, forties, visibly unenthusiastic about his post — river crossings in bad weather, checking wagon manifests, breaking up the occasional drunk courier fight. Not looking for trouble, not eager to create it.

**Tier**: Minor — enforces a checkpoint but has no independent agency over tracked state; the actual lever is the Concordat's pressure on him, not his own agenda.

**Decision logic**: if the party matches the flagged description and he notices, he won't attack outright — checkpoint guards don't start fights over a maybe-match. He'd detain and question first, giving the party a real window to talk, bribe, bluff (Disguise Self is exactly the kind of tool this scene exists to make relevant again), or otherwise defuse it before it becomes a fight.

## If it turns violent

Should only happen if the party actively resists a detention attempt. Per `two-player-scaling.md`'s group techniques: 4 Guard-statted watchmen (SRD block, `srd_lookup.py npc "Guard"`) plus Rennit himself — spread across the checkpoint rather than clustered (two at the barrier, two covering the piers), not an ambush, no surprise round on either side since this starts as a conversation, not an attack. This is meant to be a tense social/stealth puzzle, not a set-piece fight — treat an actual combat outcome as a sign the scene should de-escalate in the fiction if at all possible, the same guidance already established for Orell Tain.
