# Sallow Ford

**Region**: I — the Marches
**Level range**: 1-4
**Population**: ~400, plus whoever's waiting to cross

**⚠ Two-character party**: this range assumes four PCs. For Ilvaneth and Kriv read it as roughly **+2 levels** — see `../two-player-scaling.md`.

Two days west of Gallowmere, where the river runs shallow enough to ford. Everything moving north from the Marches crosses here, and everything that crosses pays.

## What it is

A toll town, and barely more than that. A stone bridge (built by the old dynasty, maintained by nobody), a ford beside it for those who won't pay bridge rates, a customs house, and the businesses that grow around a place where people are forced to stop.

**The Ironclad Compact holds the toll contract.** Not the crown, not a lord — a mercenary company collects the kingdom's road tax here and remits an agreed fraction. Nobody pretends otherwise.

## Locations

- **The Customs House** — Compact-run. **Serjeant Ottil Vane** commands twelve soldiers and processes everything crossing. Bribable, but institutionally — there's a *rate*, and haggling below it insults him.
- **The Long Wait** — inn and stable, permanently full of people waiting for weather, papers, or coin. The best place in the Marches to overhear where things are moving.
- **Marda's yard** — a horse trader with a reputation for not examining brands closely. **Relevant: the party is riding two stolen Gallowmere horses.** Marda will buy them, at a steep discount, no questions. She'll also sell them something less traceable.
- **The pilgrim camp** — Cinder Choir pilgrims heading north, tolerated because moving them on is more trouble than letting them pass. First place the party can observe the Choir without being inside a cult cell.

## Why the party comes here

- **It's the road west and north.** Any journey out of the Marches that isn't the Thornwick road goes through Sallow Ford.
- **Laundering the horses.** A live, immediate problem with a solution here.
- **Compact contact, cheaply.** Vane is a low-level Compact officer — the easiest first rung on the ladder that eventually reaches Coren Ashvale, and far less dangerous than walking into the Iron House in Karsgate cold.
- **Information.** People stuck waiting talk. Roll on `rumors.md` freely here.

## Standing tensions

- Vane is skimming the toll — modestly, professionally, the way everyone does. He is *terrified* of the Compact finding out, which makes him leverageable by anyone who notices.
- The pilgrim camp is growing and the town wants it gone. Someone will eventually do something about it, and it will be ugly.
