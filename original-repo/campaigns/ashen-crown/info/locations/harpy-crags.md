# The Harpy Crags

**Region**: III — the Cindermoor
**Nominal level range**: per `gazetteer.md`'s Cindermoor tier (4-8), but this specific site is scaled below that as a Tier-1 side stop
**Status**: fleshed out 2026-08-26, on the party's approach (session 3) — previously a `[sketch]` entry only

Rocky, wind-scoured outcrops rising out of the moor. Two harpies nest among the crags. At the base, half-buried in scree, is the wreck the party's Draconic-script treasure map pointed to — the remains of an earlier attempt.

## Combatants

**2 Harpies** (SRD, CR 1 each, 200 XP) — see `srd_lookup.py monster "Harpy"` for the full block. **Luring Song is the real danger**: DC 11 WIS save or charmed, forced to walk toward the harpy, ignores opportunity attack risk. With only two PCs, one charmed target is a serious action-economy hit — call for the save the moment a harpy starts singing, don't let it slide.

**Two-player math**: 2 × 200 = 400 raw, bumped one multiplier step (`two-player-scaling.md`) to ×2 = **800 adjusted — exactly Deadly** at level 3. This is a real fight, not a mook clear. The party is approaching with eyes open (scouted first), which is the intended way to walk into a Deadly encounter per `treasure.md`'s guidance.

## The wreckage

Matches the treasure map's note ("there were three of us, only I came out" — a party of three, only one came out). Two sets of old remains are half-buried in the scree, gear long since picked over by weather and scavengers but not by the harpies (they don't value coin). What's left:

- **35 gp** in scattered, weathered coin
- **A Potion of Healing**, intact in a sealed case — the one thing the dead adventurer's companions didn't get to use
- **A rusted but serviceable set of manacles and a broken holy symbol** — flavor, no mechanical value, a hook if the DM wants to build on who these three were later

## Running it

- The harpies roost above the wreck and will use Luring Song on whoever approaches first, trying to pull them close before the second harpy joins. **Establish positions before combat starts** — where are the harpies perched, how far is the wreckage, what's the terrain between (loose scree = difficult terrain, worth calling out).
- They flee if reduced to roughly a quarter HP each rather than fight to the death — chaotic evil scavengers, not fanatics.
- If the party retreats or is charmed badly, the harpies are opportunistic, not tactically patient — they won't pursue far from the crags.
