# The Sunken Barge

**Region**: I. The Marches
**Level range**: 1-3
**Site type**: Single small site, social-first by design
**Location class**: Non-dungeon (social-first single site, not a multi-room structure; added 2026-09-02 per `dungeon-design.md`'s standing classification rule)

**⚠ Two-character party check** (`two-player-scaling.md`): the full camp is a dozen lizardfolk — fought all at once, that's roughly 600 raw XP at the 15+ monster multiplier (bumped to ×4 for two PCs) = **2,400 adjusted, catastrophically Deadly for this level range.** This is exactly why the site is built social-first: a party that provokes the entire camp at once is not meant to win that fight. If it ever comes to blows, it should be a handful of defenders (2-3), not the whole band.

A merchant barge wrecked in the shallows of a marshy river bend, claimed by **lizardfolk** who are more interested in trade than violence — the region's second faction-free, no-strings site alongside `the-widows-hollow.md`, and deliberately built social-first the same way.

## What's here

The barge itself, half-sunk and settled at an angle, still holds a portion of its original cargo — dry goods gone to mildew, but also a sealed cargo hold the lizardfolk haven't been able to force open (a Strength check or the right tool solves what their claws can't). A small camp of a dozen or so lizardfolk lives around and on the wreck, using it as shelter and a lookout point over the marsh.

## The lizardfolk's actual interest

They don't want a fight and don't need one — they want **dry-land goods** they can't easily get themselves: metal tools, cloth, preserved food. They're willing to trade genuinely useful marsh knowledge (safe paths, what lives in the deeper water, a shortcut route between two Marches locations) for exactly this kind of mundane trade goods, and would rather guide a well-behaved group past their territory than fight one.

**If the party approaches with obvious hostility first**, this plays as a straightforward fight instead — a band of lizardfolk defending their claimed shelter, no different from any other territorial monster encounter. The social path only exists for a party that looks for it.

## What the cargo hold has

Once opened: mixed trade goods (cloth, preserved food, minor tools) worth roughly 80-120 gp if sold properly, plus one item of real interest — a sealed oilcloth packet of **letters between two merchant houses**, discussing a shipment of "ash-marked goods" through the Marches months before the ossuary job. Not conclusively Crown-related, but exactly the kind of loose thread `sandbox-index.md`'s sandbox layer rewards a curious party for pulling on.
