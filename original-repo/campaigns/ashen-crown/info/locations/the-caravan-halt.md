# The Caravan Halt

**Region**: IV. Karsgate (a fixed waystation on the Karsgate road, roughly a day's travel out — the last stop before the city)
**Level range**: Any — a social/exploration site, not a combat encounter
**Site type**: Recurring travel waystation, no faction ownership (though all three factions pass through)

## Concept

A permanent stopping point on the Karsgate road — a well, a stable, a low stone wall enclosure, and a scatter of semi-permanent stalls — that exists because the last day's travel into Karsgate is long enough that almost nobody makes it in one push. Merchant caravans, faction couriers, pilgrims, refugees, and the party itself (on the Millward Crossing → Karsgate leg) all funnel through here. Unlike the Driftway Fair, it isn't neutral by deliberate agreement — it's neutral because everyone's just passing through and nobody's picked a fight worth delaying their trip over.

Run, loosely, by **Tam Ferro**, a retired caravan guard who settled here rather than keep walking, and now keeps the well working and the peace kept in exchange for a customary coin from anyone who stops.

## What's here

- **The Well and Yard** — the actual reason the halt exists; clean water, a place to make camp, room for wagons.
- **Tam's Stable** — a handful of stalls for hire, mostly used by couriers swapping tired horses for fresh ones.
- **The Traders' Row** — three or four semi-permanent stalls (dried food, tack repair, basic supplies) run by people who live here full-time, betting on steady low-margin trade over chasing the Driftway Fair's monthly crowd.
- **The Notice Post** — a weathered board, less organized than the Driftway Fair's Rumor Post but serving the same function: travelers pin requests, warnings, and gossip, often about the road itself (what's been seen near Millward Crossing, which patrols are active).

## What it's actually for

A believable place for the party to overhear regional news specifically about *movement* — who's on the road, what's been seen, which faction patrols are currently active between Harrowgate and Karsgate — in a way a city market or a private meeting can't provide. A natural point to pick up a fresh travel rumor, hear a warning about a specific stretch of road, or encounter a courier/traveler with their own small, self-contained need. Recurring, like the Driftway Fair, but themed around *transit* rather than trade — the party could pass through here more than once on any future Harrowgate-Karsgate run.

## Tam Ferro

Halfling, sixties, moves like someone whose knees remember thirty years of caravan work. Not a fighter anymore by choice, but reads a crowd well — knows which travelers are trouble before they've said a word.

**Tier**: Minor — keeps the halt running, no independent agency over tracked campaign state.

**What he offers**: Genuinely current road conditions (more reliable than a rumor roll, since he hears it from everyone passing both directions), and a fair stable rate. Doesn't ask questions about who anyone is — decades of caravan work taught him that's usually the safer policy.
