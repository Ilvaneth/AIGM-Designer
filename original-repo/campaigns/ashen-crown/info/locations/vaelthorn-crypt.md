# The Vaelthorn Crypt

**Where**: On the hill immediately north of Thornwick
**Status**: Visited by the party, session 2 — fragment already gone
**Type**: Investigated site with an unresolved hook

## What it is

The ancestral burial crypt of House Vaelthorn — the imperial line. Small, grey, more grief than grandeur. Cordoned off with rough timber barriers and Concordat seals (now weathered and half-collapsed); the door stands ajar.

## What the party found (session 2)

- Sarcophagi displaced, lids pried, bones pushed aside carelessly
- A crude ritual circle of ash and chalk on the floor before the largest sarcophagus — same hand as the Gallowmere ossuary seal
- An empty socket at its center where something had been seated
- **A torn Concordat delivery order** naming **Archivist-Magus Sarelle Duskbourne** and a destination: **Ashvale Necropolis**

Concordat agents excavated this site *months before* Emperor Ashkar Vaelthorn's death. The party holds physical proof of that.

## Resolved (session 2, part 2) — the ward stone found, left alone

Acting on Hesta's tip, the party searched thoroughly. Kriv found a loose stone behind the empty socket (Perception 21 vs DC 15) concealing a Concordat **ward stone** — Ilvaneth identified it (Arcana 22): a notification ward, bound with a piece of its placer's own essence, alerts them if the stone is moved or taken (destroying it in place is untested — she doesn't know if that also triggers it). Detect Magic (ritual) confirmed only two auras in the room: the ward stone, and a faint, near-spent residue at the socket itself matching the same "family" as the party's own fragment — confirming a fragment genuinely sat there. **The party chose to leave the stone untouched and departed** — no signal sent, no further clue taken. The mystery of who removed the fragment before the party arrived remains open.

## The unresolved hook — they came back

**Hesta Bram watched the excavation** and knows something the party doesn't: the Concordat came back a **second time**, weeks after taking the fragment, and on that visit they *left something behind* rather than removing anything.

The party has not searched the crypt for it, and Hesta hasn't told them. What it is stays open — strong candidates:
- A ward or beacon, so the Concordat knows if anyone else comes looking
- A body. Someone who failed Sarelle, interred where nobody would think to check
- A decoy fragment, planted to lure and identify rival Crown-hunters

Whichever it is, it means the party's visit may already have been noticed.
