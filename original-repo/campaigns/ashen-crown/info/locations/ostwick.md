# Ostwick

**Region**: II. The Thornlands
**Level range**: 3-5
**Population**: ~200

Hamlet/village tier (`dm-notes.md`'s settlement-scaling table, under 500 = 3-4 locations) — a farming village that keeps a secret out of loyalty, not fear.

## The hook

**There's a wereboar in the surrounding woods, and the villagers know exactly who it is.** Eight years ago, a local farmhand named **Dunnel Ashe** (`npcs/dunnel-ashe.md`) took a curse defending the village from a bandit raid that would otherwise have burned it — nobody asked him to, and by the time anyone understood what had happened to him, he'd already withdrawn into the woods rather than risk hurting someone he knew. The village has quietly supported him ever since: food left at a fixed spot, nobody hunting the woods too closely, a story ready for any outsider who asks about "the boar." They protect him because he protected them first, and they consider the debt still open.

## Locations

| Location | What it is |
|----------|------------|
| **Ostwick Green** | The village center — a well, a market square on trade days, where most real business happens. |
| **The Hollow Sheaf** | The village's one tavern — warm, unremarkable, and where the cover story about "the boar in the woods" gets told smoothly to anyone who asks. |
| **The Ashe Farmstead** | Dunnel's old home, still kept up by his sister, who leaves food at the woods' edge and has never told an outsider the truth. |
| **The Millward Shrine** | A small, unclaimed communal shrine — no dominant faction, matching `dm-notes.md`'s guidance for a smaller settlement's religious presence. |

## Running this well

**⚠ Two-character party check** (`two-player-scaling.md`): a wereboar is CR 2 (450 raw XP), solo — adjusted 675, a fair Hard fight at level 2-3, reasonable for this site's range. Dunnel fights alone; he has no pack or allies to bring the total higher.

A party that investigates carefully (tracking, careful questions, a good Insight check against the tavern's cover story) can find the truth without violence. A party that hunts "the wereboar" as a monster finds a frightened, cornered man who doesn't want to hurt anyone and will only fight if genuinely forced to — this should read as a tragedy if it comes to blows, not a trophy kill.
