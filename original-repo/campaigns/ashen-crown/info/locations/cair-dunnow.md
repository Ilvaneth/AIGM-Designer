# Cair Dunnow

**Region**: III. The Cindermoor
**Level range**: 4-7
**Population**: ~150

Hamlet/village tier (`dm-notes.md`'s settlement-scaling table, under 500 = 3-4 locations) — a moor village that predates the current kingdom's funerary doctrine entirely, and knows it.

## The hook

**Cair Dunnow practices funeral rites nobody else does, and knows more about the ash-kings than any scholar in the Hollow Kingdoms — because they aren't remembering the ash-kings from records. They're the last community still practicing something close to the ash-kings' own original funerary tradition**, predating the schism that produced the orthodox Ember Crown temple, the Ashlord Concordat, and by extension the Cinder Choir. Every other faction's version of "ash-magic" and death-ritual is, from Cair Dunnow's perspective, a corrupted or misremembered fragment of what they still do correctly, unbroken, in a moor village nobody important visits.

They do not explain this to outsiders. Ever. Being right and being ignored for centuries has made them insular rather than proud about it.

## Locations

| Location | What it is |
|----------|------------|
| **The Moot Circle** | An open stone circle at the village's heart, older than any building around it — where Cair Dunnow's actual governance happens, by consensus among the eldest households. |
| **The Long House** | Where the village's elders live communally — the closest thing to an authority the party could actually approach. |
| **The Ashway** | A processional path leading out onto the moor itself, used only for the village's funeral rites — walking it uninvited is the single fastest way to turn the whole village hostile. |
| **The Old Hearth-Shrine** | Not an Ember Crown shrine — older, tied to the same original tradition as the funeral rites themselves. No priest; the village's eldest woman tends it by rotation. |

## How to actually earn something here

Hostility, threats, or a Concordat/Choir affiliation gets nothing — Cair Dunnow already distrusts both for what it considers doctrinal theft. The way in is **respect for the correct form**: the village's rites use an ash-offering ritual structurally identical to the one Ilvaneth already used to bypass the Gallowmere ossuary's guardian in session 1. A party that recognizes this and performs it correctly, unprompted, at the Old Hearth-Shrine earns real, if grudging, attention — proof they've actually encountered the true tradition already, not learned a corrupted version of it from the Concordat or the Choir.

## What they actually know

Real, unfiltered ash-king history — including, at the DM's discretion, a genuine correction to something the Concordat or Choir currently believes wrong about their own founding doctrine. This should land as a real "oh" moment if the party ever gets this far, not a data dump — Cair Dunnow doesn't lecture, it answers exactly what's asked and no more.
