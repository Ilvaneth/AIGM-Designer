# Karsgate

**Region**: IV — the regional seat
**Level range**: 5-10
**Population**: ~30,000

**⚠ Two-character party**: this range assumes four PCs. For Ilvaneth and Kriv read it as roughly **+2 levels** — see `../two-player-scaling.md`.

The city the campaign was missing. Walls, guilds, nobility, an undercity, and open chapterhouses for both the Ashlord Concordat and the Ironclad Compact — the first place the party can operate at a scale above "two people with a knife and a grudge."

**Why the party comes here**: to sell something Gallowmere can't buy, to research the Crown properly, to reach a faction directly, or because a power base in the Marches means nothing until Karsgate acknowledges it.

**Rebuilt 2026-08-28** against `dm-notes.md`'s city-tier standard (15+ locations, organized by district, multiple religious sites) after being caught marked `[full]` while actually holding 7 named locations and zero religious sites — see `bug-log.md`. Now carries real presence for all three factions built out the same session (`factions/*.md`), per the DM's direct request that a city this central to the plot should show them actually doing business here, not just existing on paper.

## The Kingsward — high city

Nobility, the magistrate's court, walled townhouses. Old money that has stopped pretending the succession will resolve in its favor.

| Location | What it is |
|----------|------------|
| **The Grey Hall** | Ashlord Concordat chapterhouse. Archivists, a lending library, and **Magus Orell Tain**, who opposes Sarelle's project from inside. → `factions/ashlord-concordat.md` |
| **The Magistrate's Court** | Magistrate **Ilvarra Sonn** — honest, overworked, aware her authority is decorative. Can be an ally to anyone who makes her look effective. |
| **The Ember Crown Cathedral** | **The region's principal orthodox temple — the religious site this city was missing entirely.** Serves Ossara, Keeper of Names. Led by **High Prior Varic Sennett** (`npcs/varic-sennett.md`). Formal, well-funded, and quietly aware that both the Concordat and the Choir trace their doctrine back to arguments this cathedral's own predecessors lost. |
| **The Sonnward Promenade** | Where nobility parade and trade gossip in place of honesty. The fastest way to learn who's actually maneuvering for the throne this season. |
| **House Varn's Townhouse** | A noble family betting heavily on outlasting the succession war rather than picking a side — old money playing a long, patient game. A plausible rival or silent partner for a party power base. |
| **The Ivory Court** | A private gaming and social club for nobility who want to be seen losing money gracefully. Entry is a status symbol in itself — smaller and far more exclusive than the Wagered Crown below. |
| **The Hall of Wards** | The city's licensed magic-item registry and inspection office — the legitimate way to identify and sell enchanted goods, at the cost of the transaction being on record. |
| **The Sundered Garden** | A public memorial garden for the war dead, somber and well-tended. Both Concordat archivists and unnoticed Choir observers visit it for their own reasons — a place worth watching, not just walking through. |
| **The Old Armory** | Ceremonial now — a museum of past wars kept by the crown's decorative remaining authority. |
| **The Widow's Hearth** | Ember Crown-run charity for war widows and orphans — the orthodox faith's practical, unglamorous presence in the city, distinct from the cathedral's formal one. |
| **Ilvarra Sonn's private residence** | Modest for a magistrate, deliberately — she doesn't want to be seen benefiting from a position she considers mostly ornamental. |
| *(An unnamed noble residence)* | Where the city's secret is — see "The city's secret," below. Deliberately unplaced on this list. |

## The Ledger — merchant quarter

Guildhalls, counting houses, the grain exchange.

| Location | What it is |
|----------|------------|
| **The Iron House** | Ironclad Compact chapterhouse. **Factor Bexel Grine** handles regional business and reports to Coren Ashvale — the party's realistic route to the Warmarshal. → `factions/ironclad-compact.md` |
| **The Grain Exchange** | Where the region's food is priced. Controlling Marches grain shipments is a direct power-base upgrade. → `power-base.md` |
| **Halvern & Sons, Curios** | Buys relics without asking questions, and knows what it's looking at — the honest way to learn a fragment's worth, and the fastest way for word to reach the Concordat that someone has one. |
| **The Counting House** | The city's central banking and moneylending guild — where a serious power base eventually needs a line of credit. |
| **The Wrights' Guildhall** | Smiths, carpenters, and engineers organized as one body. Commissions, not walk-in trade. |
| **Spice Row** | Exotic foreign goods and the merchants who travel with them — a natural place to pick up rumors from outside the Hollow Kingdoms entirely. |
| **The Assayer's Office** | Verifies gold and gem purity for a fee — the unglamorous, necessary stop after any real haul. |
| **A quiet office above a tailor's shop** | The Grey Tally's actual Karsgate front, unmarked — Sefwyn Marrow's own network reaches this far. → `npcs/sefwyn-marrow.md` |
| **Vantor Shipping Concern** | Controls the bulk of river trade through Karsgate. Run by **Marta Vantor** (`npcs/marta-vantor.md`) — the single biggest lever on Riverside's docks. |
| **The Weavers' Exchange** | Textile trade guildhall — unglamorous, steady coin, a plausible front for anyone needing a legitimate-looking business. |
| **Blackwater Provisioners** | The city's serious outfitter — expedition gear, rations, rope, the boring things that make a dungeon delve survivable. |
| **The Ledger Watch House** | A real, functioning city guard post — a deliberate contrast to the Reeks' poor policing, and a reminder that Karsgate's authority is selectively applied by wealth. |
| **The Wagered Crown** | **The city's great casino** — multiple floors, open to anyone with coin (unlike the Ivory Court's nobility-only door), and the single largest neutral-ground meeting  ground in Karsgate. Nobility slums here to lose money among strangers; merchants close real deals at the tables; mercenaries and criminals alike are simply other gamblers. Owned and run by **Orvenna Kest** (`npcs/orvenna-kest.md`), who extends credit, launders coin for anyone who tips well, and remembers every debt exactly. |

## The Reeks — working city

Tanneries, slaughterhouses, dense tenements, the majority of the population. Poorly policed. This is where an outsider crew can actually take root.

| Location | What it is |
|----------|------------|
| **The Broken Wheel** | A tavern functioning as a hiring hall for people who don't ask what the job is — **and the front for Karsgate's actual dirty-contract broker, Corla Vint** (`npcs/corla-vint.md`). Theft, kidnapping, and — for the right price and the right client — worse, all arranged through her at a back table nobody's ever seen her leave. |
| **The Warrens** | Tenement blocks held by three rival gangs — see below. The most direct way for the party to hold real territory in Karsgate. |
| ↳ **Red Tally turf** | Led by **"Iron" Sella Dray** (`npcs/sella-dray.md`) — the largest, most organized of the three, running protection rackets. The gang worth displacing first if the party wants the Warrens taken seriously. |
| ↳ **Coalback turf** | Smaller, brutal, holds territory through fear more than organization — a softer target than Red Tally, but with nothing to leverage once beaten. |
| ↳ **Ninefinger crew** | **Karsgate's actual stolen-goods network** — thieves and fences rather than muscle, moving anything from lifted purses to a wagon's worth of cargo. The gang most willing to cut a deal instead of fighting for their corner, and Corla Vint's usual first call when a contract needs actual hands, not just an introduction. |
| **Tanner's Row** | The tanneries themselves — the smell that gives the district its name, and the grinding labor that keeps it running. |
| **The Slaughteryards** | Where gang muscle is quietly recruited — strong backs looking for worse-paying, more dangerous work than the killing floor. |
| **Gallow's Rest** | A cheap tenement inn for the transient and the desperate — the kind of place nobody remembers a guest's face. |
| **The Sump** | An open drainage canal running down to the Undercity — one of several ways in. Wererat scouts are known to use it. |
| **Mother Ilsbeth's Kitchen** | A communal soup kitchen doubling as an information hub for the district's poor — she hears everything eventually. |
| **The Rustworks** | A scrapyard and salvage yard — mundane, but the kind of place useful junk (and the occasional real find) turns up. |
| **The Bonecart Yard** | Where the district's dead are collected before cremation. A natural, unremarkable place for a quiet Choir or Concordat interest in who's recently died. |

Everything moving north or south passes through. The smuggling routes out of Gallowmere terminate here.

| Location | What it is |
|----------|------------|
| **The Long Quay** | Karsgate's main dock and customs house — the legitimate front door for river trade. |
| **Vantor Shipping's Warehouse** | Marta Vantor's largest holding — see `npcs/marta-vantor.md` and the Ledger listing above. |
| **Smuggler's Row** | The unofficial docks — where the Gallowmere smuggling route the party's own standing plan targets actually terminates. → `power-base.md`'s docks/smuggling row |
| **The Drift Market** | An informal floating market on lashed-together barges — cheap, unregulated, and a good place to sell something you'd rather not have appraised. |
| **The Ferryman's Rest** | A dockworkers' tavern, loud and honest in its own way — real gossip, no pretense. |
| **The Harbor Watch Tower** | Customs and harbor patrol, one of the city's few consistently-staffed watch posts. |
| **The Foundered Warehouse** | A half-flooded, officially abandoned warehouse — actually a working entrance into the Undercity, known to smugglers and little else. |
| **Fisherman's Row** | A modest, largely apolitical fishing community — the ordinary working city that keeps functioning underneath everything else happening above it. |
| **The Drowned Bell** | A half-submerged old shrine and bell tower, pre-dating the current cathedral — eerie, rarely visited, structurally connected to the Undercity's flooded reaches below. |

Distinct in class and character from the Kingsward's grand cathedral: where nobility worship formally, Riverside worships practically, asking Ossara for safe passage across water in the same breath as any other river hazard. |

## The Undercity

**Rebuilt to `dungeon-design.md`'s standard**, not just a monster list — the Undercity functions as a real dungeon underneath a living city, and gets the same four-phase treatment.

**Concept**: The original Karsgate, built before the current walls, abandoned after a flood collapsed its lower quarters generations ago. The current city was built on top rather than around it — Karsgate literally stands on its own corpse.

**Ecology**: Wererats have run the upper tunnels for at least two generations, trading in what falls or is dumped from above. Otyughs, carrion crawlers, and gray oozes live off the city's waste, draining down through the Sump and other channels. A cloaker holds the deepest, oldest reaches, older than the wererats' claim. **The Cinder Choir's second cell** (per `factions/cinder-choir.md`) has quietly operated out of the old burial vaults for years, using the Undercity's obscurity the same way Fenn uses the Ash-Kilns' mundane cover.

**Multiple entrances**: The Sump (from the Reeks), The Foundered Warehouse (from Riverside), and at least one unconfirmed passage rumored to open somewhere in the Kingsward — nobility have their own reasons to need a way down nobody watches.

| # | Location | Content |
|---|----------|---------|
| 1 | **The Sump Gate** | Entry from the Reeks; wererat scouts watch this approach specifically |
| 2 | **The Drowned Concourse** | A vast collapsed plaza, half-flooded — echoes carry, footing is treacherous, the old city's scale is visible for the first time here |
| 3 | **The Wererat Warren** | A den, negotiable if approached without obvious hostility — they trade information for safe passage as often as they fight for it |
| 4 | **The First Wall** | A remnant of the original city's defenses — worn carvings, a forgotten name for Karsgate before it was Karsgate |
| 5 | **The Gray Cistern** | Gray ooze territory — a genuine environmental hazard, not just a monster fight |
| 6 | **The Otyugh's Midden** | A garbage-eater's lair — foul, and exactly the kind of place something valuable gets thrown away and never retrieved |
| 7 | **The Carrion Tangle** | A narrow, collapsed tunnel network — carrion crawler territory, a real choke point |
| 8 | **The Choir's Undercity Cell** | A disguised mortuary-shrine among the old vaults, led by **Brother Kessic Ambrey** (`npcs/kessic-ambrey.md`) — on Yeva Ashwright's circuit, per `factions/cinder-choir.md` |
| 9 | **The Foundered Cellar** | Connects up to Riverside's warehouse entrance — smugglers and the Choir cell both use this route without officially knowing about each other |
| 10 | **Vault of the First Kings** | Pre-dynastic burial vault — untouched grave goods, and the kind of ancient funerary craft Corvin Thale would pay well to see |
| 11 | **Vault of Ash** | A second burial vault — ash-preserved remains, and a working theory (unconfirmed) that this predates the Ashlord Concordat's own naming |
| 12 | **Vault of the Nameless** | The third vault — deliberately unmarked graves. Exactly the kind of place a Crown fragment could have sat unnoticed for four hundred years, per the site's original design intent |
| 13 | **The Old Mint** | The original city's treasury — looted long ago, but not completely; a real, if modest, coin find for a thorough search |
| 14 | **The Kingsward Passage** | The rumored noble entrance — unconfirmed until the party actually finds it, and a plausible link to the city's secret (below) |
| 15 | **The Bone Market** | An illegal trade in remains and minor relics, run by opportunistic scavengers independent of the Choir cell — the kind of morally grim, faction-free complication this sandbox is supposed to have |
| 16 | **The Flooded Archive** | A partially submerged pre-dynastic records hall — real history here, legible if someone can work underwater or drain a section first |
| 17 | **The Cloaker's Deep** | The oldest, deepest point — genuinely dangerous, reserved for a party that's cleared or bypassed most of the rest. **Now a tracked regional bounty** — see `regional-threats.md`, "The Undercity Cloaker," posted by Marta Vantor |

**DM pacing note**: the Undercity should apply real resting pressure — it's directly beneath a living city, not an isolated ruin, so a long rest here should feel like a deliberate risk (wererat patrols, the Choir cell's own security, general instability), not a free default.

## The city's secret

Someone in Karsgate high society is not human. A **rakshasa** or **succubus** has been operating in the Kingsward for years, patiently steering the city's noble houses. It has no interest in the Crown — its game is longer and more local — but it will notice anyone who starts moving the city's pieces, and it will treat the party as either a tool or an infestation.

This is deliberately *not* Crown-related. Not everything should be.
