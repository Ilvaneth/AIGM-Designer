# Hallowmere Chapel

**Region**: II. The Thornlands
**Level range**: 2-4
**Site type**: Single small site
**Location class**: Non-dungeon (a lore-and-atmosphere single chapel, not a multi-room structure — own "Running this well" section explicitly frames it as "more than a combat one"; added 2026-09-02 per `dungeon-design.md`'s standing classification rule)

**⚠ Two-character party check** (`two-player-scaling.md`): 3 specters together is 600 raw XP → adjusted **1,500 XP**, Deadly beyond level 3 for this party. **2 specters is the maximum encountered at once** — a third, if present at all, should be drawn off or arrive later, never all three simultaneously.

A ruined roadside chapel, older than Thornwick itself, abandoned when the road it once served shifted course a generation ago.

## What's here

**Specters** — 2, restless rather than actively malevolent, tied to the chapel's own burial ground rather than roaming. They react to disturbance (footsteps, light, voices) more than they hunt, giving a careful party a real chance to avoid or thin them out before a full fight.

**The burial register**: the chapel kept meticulous burial records for nearly two centuries before it closed, and the ledger — brittle but legible — survives in a sealed lectern at the front. A successful History or Investigation check (DC 13) finds something specific: **an interment from the Crown era, marked with an annotation using the same "vow and return" phrasing found on the Gallowmere ossuary's own inscription.** Not a confirmed fragment location — a real, checkable lead the party can choose to chase, in the same spirit as Sister Mave's shrine records at Thornwick.

## Running this well

This is a lore-and-atmosphere site more than a combat one — the specters are a genuine threat if provoked carelessly, but a party that moves quietly through the chapel and focuses on the register can walk away with a real research lead and minimal risk. Reward patience, don't punish curiosity.
