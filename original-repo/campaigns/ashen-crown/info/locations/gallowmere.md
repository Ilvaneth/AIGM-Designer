# Gallowmere

Border town on the southern edge of the Hollow Kingdoms, ~1,200 people and swelling. Lives off refugees, smugglers, and everyone the capital would rather not see. Walled, but the wall is old and the town has spilled outside it.

The party's home base for Chapter 1. **This town is the prize** — the Chapter 1 arc ends with them holding real power here (see `dm-notes.md`).

## Districts and locations

### Inside the walls

- **The lower street** — cramped, commercial, smells of fish and wet stone. **Mother Vey's junk shop** is here, back room bolted. See `info/npcs/mother-vey.md`.
- **The market square** — daily market, thinner every month as trade routes close. The **Demirnal stable** sits on its edge; the party stole two horses from it in session 2. Nobody has connected it to them yet, but the stablemaster is loudly angry and has offered a small reward for information.
- **"The Last Coin"** — Fennick Orle's tavern, the town's gossip exchange. See `info/npcs/fennick-orle.md`.
- **The garrison house** — by the north gate. Captain Kell's command, ~30 soldiers, morale poor. See `info/npcs/doreth-kell.md`.
- **The old graveyard** — northeast corner, half-abandoned. The **sealed ossuary** the party breached in session 1 is beneath a collapsed mausoleum here. Currently open and unattended; the dormant guardian is still inside.
- **The Cinderpath Shrine** — a modest, roofless ash-shrine at the edge of the graveyard, built and maintained by whoever needs it that week, not by any one faith. Refugees burn their dead here and keep small ash-offerings in wall niches when they can't afford a proper rite. No resident priest, no faction claim — the Concordat, the Choir, and the orthodox temple have all eyed it and none has moved to claim it yet, since doing so would mean planting a flag in a town none of them fully controls. A genuine power vacuum: whoever formalizes a claim on it first gains real legitimacy with the refugee population.
- **The Salt Lantern** — the town's only real inn, wedged between the lower street and the market square. Run by **Genna Ostler** (dwarf, weathered, missing two fingers on her left hand — an old smuggling-run accident she doesn't explain). Rooms are cheap and thin-walled; the common room is where every rumor in town gets a second telling before it reaches Fennick's tavern. Genna keeps a ledger of who's slept there and when — she'll sell a look at it to the right buyer, and has never once been asked for it by anyone who mattered until now.
- **Coalsmoke Forge** — the town's smithy, on the lower street near the wall, run by **Torv Adder** (half-orc, burn-scarred forearms, permanently out of temper about something). Repairs, shoeing, basic gear; the succession war means steady work fixing looted and battle-damaged arms and armor for whoever's paying, no questions about where the pieces came from. Torv's short on good iron, same complaint as every smith south of the capital — a supply problem the party could solve and be owed for, same shape as Thornwick's Berrin.

### Outside the walls

- **The refugee camp** — tents and wagons against the outer wall. Several hundred people, no authority, no sanitation, enormous untapped potential as a labor and loyalty pool. The Wend family is here. See `info/npcs/alis-wend.md` and `info/power-base.md`.
- **The docks** — river landing south of town. The smuggling route runs through here, and it runs through Kell. See `info/power-base.md`.
- **The Ash-Kilns** — the town's crematorium, upriver. See `info/locations/the-ash-kilns.md`.

## Standing tensions

- The Concordat convoy arrived (or is arriving) to find the ossuary already emptied. Someone will be asking questions in town, and the party is not there to manage it — they left for Thornwick.
- The stablemaster's stolen horses.
- Vey's unresolved suspicion about what the party actually found.
