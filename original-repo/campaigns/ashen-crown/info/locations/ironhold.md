# Ironhold

**Region**: V. The Ashvale
**Level range**: 5-10
**Population**: ~8,000

Market-town/small-city tier (`dm-notes.md`'s settlement-scaling table, 3,000-10,000 = 10-15 locations) — the Ironclad Compact's actual company headquarters, built on what used to be the Ashvale family's ancestral seat before nobility gave way to mercenary enterprise. See `factions/ironclad-compact.md`'s Phase 1 founding for the full history.

Coren Ashvale is here when he's anywhere (`npcs/coren-ashvale.md`).

## Locations

### The Keep — command
| Location | What it is |
|----------|------------|
| **Ashvale Keep** | Coren's seat — functional, unornamented, a working command post more than a noble residence. |
| **The War Room** | Maps and status boards tracking every contract the Compact currently holds, region by region — a real, physical representation of `faction-subplots.md`'s "sell to everyone, ensure no decisive advantage" strategy. |
| **The Armsmaster's Hall** | Where officers are actually vetted and trained before promotion — Sethra Shestendeliath rose through here. |
| **The Ashvale Crypt** | The family's own remaining burial vault — small compared to what the Necropolis once meant to them, and a quiet, poignant contrast the party can notice: Coren's family still has *some* ground of their own, which makes the occupied Necropolis sting worse, not less. |

### The Barracks Quarter — the actual company
| Location | What it is |
|----------|------------|
| **The Drillyard** | Open training ground, always in use — the Compact's discipline is visible, not just claimed. |
| **The Long Barracks** | Housing for rank-and-file contracted soldiers. |
| **Officers' Row** | Separate, more comfortable housing for officers — rank has real, visible privilege here. |
| **The Armory** | Weapons and armor storage at a genuinely large scale — a real, if heavily guarded, resource. |
| **The Recruiter's Table** | Where new mercenaries actually sign on — clear, itemized terms, per `factions/ironclad-compact.md`'s Phase 3 recruitment section. A plausible entry point for a PC or companion wanting direct Compact work. |

### Market Row — civilian support
| Location | What it is |
|----------|------------|
| **The Provisioner's Yard** | Supplies for the garrison, also open to paying travelers. |
| **The Paymaster's Hall** | Where contracts are settled and mercenaries are actually paid — the physical heart of "the contract is the entire moral universe." |
| **The Hammer and Chain** | The town's main tavern, named for the Compact's own seal — off-duty soldiers, real gossip, no pretense. |
| **The Broker's Chapel** | **The town's religious site — and, deliberately, also its dirty-contract exchange.** Small, informal shrine to "the Broker's Ledger," the half-mythic patron mercenaries swear contracts by (see `factions/ironclad-compact.md`'s Phase 1). Leave a sealed request and a coin offering at the shrine, and — for jobs too dirty for even the Compact's official no-questions contracts (theft, kidnapping, an assassination the Recruiter's Table would never put in writing) — someone answers, anonymously, days later. **Whether "the Broker" is a real, actual deity taking an interest, or just whoever's currently running the racket behind the myth, is deliberately never confirmed in-fiction — see `npcs/the-broker.md` for the DM's own answer.** |
| **The Old Ashvale Quarter** | The remnant of the family's former noble holdings — mostly repurposed now, quiet, a little sad if anyone stops to look. |
| **The Ashvale Archives** | Old noble records, contracts, and — per `kriv-thread.md`'s Layer 1 — very possibly a physical paper trail of the Compact's own role in House Shestendeliath's destruction, independent of whether Coren chooses to cooperate. A real, findable alternative if the direct approach to Coren doesn't work. |
