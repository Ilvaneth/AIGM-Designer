# Hollowmoor Barrows

**Where**: Two days north of Gallowmere, off the Thornwick road — a moor studded with low grass-covered burial mounds
**Difficulty**: Level 4+ for the outer mounds; the deep barrow is level 6+
**Type**: Medium dungeon site — lore, treasure, undead

**⚠ Two-character party**: this range assumes four PCs. For Ilvaneth and Kriv read it as roughly **+2 levels** — see `../two-player-scaling.md`.

## What it is

Dozens of burial mounds from the era *before* the current dynasty — the same period as the Ashen Crown's forging. This is the campaign's great **false lead**, and it should be built to look exactly like a main-quest site: ash-and-bone motifs, the same craftsmanship as the Gallowmere ossuary seal, the same funerary language.

**There is no Crown fragment here.** There never was.

## What's in it

**The danger**: The outer mounds hold **skeletons and a wight** or two — old guardians, mindless. The deep barrow at the moor's center holds a **barrow-wight of real power** and its retinue, still guarding a king who was never a king.

**The treasure**: Genuine grave-wealth — old gold, a **magic weapon** (a blade or spear of the ash-king era, ideal for Kriv), and ceremonial pieces worth real money to a collector or a Concordat archivist.

**The lore** — and this is the real prize: inscriptions here explain *why* the Crown was broken. Not just that it was dangerous, but the specific event: the last ash-king assembled it, and what came back afterward was not him. The barrows are full of the people who put him down. This is the single best source in Chapter 1 for understanding what the Crown actually does to the person who completes it — which is exactly the question Ilvaneth needs answered.

## Why it matters beyond loot

- It answers Ilvaneth's central question and complicates it. She wants to transcend death; this site's entire message is *someone already tried, and it worked, and it was worse than dying*.
- **Sefwyn Marrow has already been here** and recorded it in their private tally. If the party meets them, Sefwyn can trade this location — or the party can discover Sefwyn's been ahead of them the whole time.
- The magic weapon is a real level-appropriate reward for a party that's outgrown starting gear.

## The interweave

The party will approach this convinced it's a fragment site. It isn't. What they get instead is the truth about the price — delivered as a dungeon rather than a lecture. Play the false lead honestly: let it look right until it doesn't.
