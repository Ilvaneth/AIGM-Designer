# The Gallow Tree

**Region**: I. The Marches
**Level range**: 2-4
**Site type**: Single evocative site, not a numbered dungeon
**Location class**: Non-dungeon (single-scene site — a burial spot and a short fight, not a multi-room structure; added 2026-09-02 per `dungeon-design.md`'s standing classification rule)

Gallowmere's namesake — a great dead oak outside the walls where the old lords hanged people. Nothing grows within twenty feet of it.

## What's actually here

**Buried at the roots is the Cinder Choir's first failure — decades before the Ash-Kilns' current one.** A predecessor cell, working alone and without the resources Fenn's operation has now, attempted to manufacture a returner here long before the doctrine had a name for what went wrong when it failed. What came out was buried under the tree by the cell's own survivors, in shame, the same way the fourth kiln's ghast is being kept now — this is not a coincidence, it's the pattern repeating, which is exactly the point: **a faction that cannot find its prophesied figure eventually decides to make one, and this has already happened before.**

**The shadow(s)**: one or two **shadows** (SRD) haunt the tree, the restless remnant of what was buried — hostile, drawn to warmth and life, and a fair fight for a level 2-4 party who doesn't linger.

**The Choir connection**: the offerings left here are not random folk superstition — they're the Choir's own quiet, informal pilgrimage site, a place cell members visit to leave small tokens for a failure they've never officially acknowledged. If the party is ever seen here by a Choir agent, it reads as either deep doctrinal knowledge or a serious threat, with no comfortable middle ground.

## What a search finds

A successful Investigation or Religion check at the roots (DC 13) turns up fragments of old bone and a corroded ash-grey token matching the Choir's own sigil style — physical proof, if the party ever needs it, that the Choir's manufacture problem isn't new and isn't isolated to Gallowmere.
