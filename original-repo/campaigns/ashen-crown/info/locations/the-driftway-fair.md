# The Driftway Fair

**Region**: IV. Karsgate (a fixed meadow site, a short ride from the city walls)
**Level range**: Any — a social/exploration site, not a combat encounter
**Site type**: Recurring neutral gathering, faction-free

## Concept

A traveling fair that sets up on the same open meadow roughly once a month, drawing merchants, entertainers, and travelers from every direction — Marches refugees, Thornlands farmers, Karsgate traders, the occasional Ironhold-adjacent mercenary spending coin between contracts. **No faction runs it, polices it, or claims it** — its entire value to everyone who attends is that it's neutral ground, and the loose coalition of regular vendors who organize it enforces that neutrality themselves (no weapons drawn, no faction colors flown, disputes settled by the fair's own informal council or you don't come back next month).

## What's here

- **The Vendor Ring** — the actual market: mundane goods, food, minor curiosities, the occasional real find among the junk
- **The Wagering Tent** — informal betting on everything from arm-wrestling to foot races, open to anyone, much lower stakes and much less dangerous than Karsgate's Wagered Crown
- **The Rumor Post** — a literal board where travelers pin notices, requests, and gossip; a natural place to seed a job, a rumor, or a companion-recruitment opportunity
- **The Council Fire** — where the fair's organizing vendors settle disputes and decide who's welcome back; a party that causes trouble here gets talked about, not attacked

## What it's actually for

A pressure-free, faction-blind place to sell odd goods, hear rumors from every region at once, gauge how "wanted" the party currently is in different areas (traveler gossip is honest here, since nobody's performing for a faction), and pick up a job that isn't wired into anyone's agenda. The closest thing in this campaign to a genuine neutral hub, and deliberately recurring rather than a one-time site — the party can return to it.
