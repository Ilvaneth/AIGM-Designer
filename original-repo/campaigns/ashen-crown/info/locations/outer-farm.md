# Outer Farm

**Region**: IV. Karsgate (the working farmland ringing the city, a half-day's walk from the walls)
**Level range**: 5-10 (Karsgate's own range) — a social/moral-choice site, minimal fight potential
**Site type**: A single working location, no faction ties

## Concept

One holding among the dozens of farms that feed Karsgate — chosen as *the* Outer Farm because it's the one the party actually reaches first, wherever the map originally intended it. Wheat and root vegetables, a few dozen acres, worked by more hands than the land technically needs — because most of them aren't hired labor. They're war refugees, taken on by the landowner as "workers" in exchange for food and a roof, on terms that were never actually written down.

The owner, **Halda Merrow**, isn't a villain by the standards of a Neutral Evil campaign's own antagonists — she took people in when the Marches and Thornlands started emptying out, and by the letter of it, she's kept her word: nobody starves, nobody's beaten. But the terms are hers alone, the "debt" for food and shelter never quite clears, and nobody working her fields has anywhere else to go. It's exploitation with a friendly face, the kind the succession war has made almost unremarkable.

## What's here

- **The Farmhouse** — Halda's own home, modest but comfortable, a stark visual contrast to the refugee bunkhouse.
- **The Bunkhouse** — where roughly twenty refugee laborers sleep, eat, and live. Crowded, functional, not cruel — just clearly temporary in a way that's stopped being temporary.
- **The Threshing Yard** — where the actual work happens; a good place to talk to laborers away from Halda's earshot.
- **The Old Well** — disused, boarded over, at the field's edge. One of the laborers (see below) knows it's not as dry as it looks.

## Halda Merrow

Half-elf, older than she looks (long enough to have weathered more than one regional war), sun-worn, speaks about her "workers" with what sounds like real fondness and never once uses the word refugee. Not physically dangerous, not hiding weapons or muscle — her leverage is entirely that leaving means leaving with nothing.

**Tier**: Minor — no independent agency over tracked state; the actual weight here is a moral choice for the party, not a plot lever.

**What she wants**: To keep running the farm at a profit, with labor she doesn't have to pay real wages for. Genuinely doesn't think of herself as doing anything wrong.

## The hook

One of the laborers — a gnome, a former Thornlands farmhand named **Corwin Aldis** — has been quietly stockpiling coin and supplies in the old well, planning to slip away with as many others as will come, once he has enough. He'll approach the party cautiously if they spend any real time in the Threshing Yard, sizing them up as either a threat to report to Halda or genuine help. What he actually needs: enough money or muscle to make the break credible (a wagon, real travel supplies, protection for the first day or two on the road) rather than a fantasy that ends with everyone starving on the roadside.

This plays however the party wants it to. A Neutral Evil party might: help Corwin's people escape (a real cost, a genuine good deed with no angle — or a future favor bank); sell him out to Halda for a reward; extract a price from *both* sides; or simply walk past it entirely, which is itself a choice this campaign's own philosophy (`dm-notes.md`'s roleplay standing rule) treats as valid, not a failure state.
