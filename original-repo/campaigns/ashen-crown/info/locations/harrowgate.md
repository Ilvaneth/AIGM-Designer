# Harrowgate

**Region**: II — the Thornlands
**Level range**: 3-6
**Population**: ~4,000

**⚠ Two-character party**: this range assumes four PCs. For Ilvaneth and Kriv read it as roughly **+2 levels** — see `../two-player-scaling.md`.

The market town that serves the whole Thornlands. Half a day south of Thornwick. Walled, prosperous by regional standards, and the first place the party can spend serious money or buy something better than village goods.

## Locations

- **The Market** — real trade, four days a week. Weapons, armor, horses, supplies, and merchants from as far as Karsgate. The place to actually equip.
- **The Temple of the Ember Crown** — the old imperial cult. Not the Cinder Choir — this is the *orthodox* faith the Choir splintered from, and the two loathe each other. **Prior Aldric Venn** presides: genuinely devout, politically shrewd, and increasingly frightened by what he's hearing from the north.
  - **The temple archive** holds imperial cult records going back to the dynasty's founding, including accounts of the Crown from the orthodox perspective — which flatly contradicts the Concordat's version. Real research value.
- **The Magistrate's House** — nominal crown authority for the Thornlands. Currently vacant; the previous magistrate left for Karsgate eight months ago and has not been replaced. **The Thornlands are effectively ungoverned**, which is why the deserters at Sorrel's Hollow operate freely — and an enormous opening for anyone who wants to fill a vacuum.
- **The Garrison House** — the town's actual soldiery, a few dozen men, nominally crown troops but (same pattern as Gallowmere's Captain Kell) quietly answering to **Ironclad Compact** retainer money now that Karsgate isn't paying attention. Low-key, professional, not looking for trouble. This is where Roskel's session-3 task (find a corruptible senior Compact contact) actually happened — he found only low-ranking soldiers who wouldn't talk, and **one suspicious corporal** (unnamed, unconfirmed risk, might recognize Roskel on sight) is stationed here. No senior officer identified yet.
- **The Hanged Man** — inn, and the region's actual news exchange
- **Vestry & Cole** — apothecary and alchemist; potions, components, and the only person in the Thornlands who can identify a magic item
- **The Scriptorium** — secular scribes' and record-keepers' hall, separate from the temple archive. Maps, land deeds, trade records, copied books — where the temple archive holds imperial-cult history, this is where the town's *actual paperwork* lives. Run by a harried master scribe, **Master Ellin Voss**, who'll copy or sell a map or document for a fee and knows exactly who owes what to whom in Harrowgate (a second, more mundane rumor source than Gerwin).
- **The Ashgate Conservatory** — not a real wizards' academy, just three hedge mages and a retired battle-caster who pool resources, teach a handful of apprentices, and sell spell components and the occasional spellbook page. The closest thing to arcane infrastructure in the Thornlands. A plausible place for Ilvaneth to research, trade spells, or hear arcane gossip.
- **The Sable Casket** — a locked, appointment-only antiquities and curiosities dealer. Doesn't have a shelf of magic items for casual sale (this economy doesn't work that way — see `items-and-loot.md`), but the owner, **Corvin Thale**, occasionally has *one* real piece and always knows who's buying and selling relics regionally. A rumor source for magic-item leads, not a shop to browse.
- **Ironmonger's Row** — the smiths' and armorers' quarter, a dozen forges going at once. Where Kriv's Smith's Tools proficiency would actually mean something — repairs, custom work, or information about who's been buying unusual quantities of metal or commissioning strange pieces.
- **The Merchants' Assembly Hall** — where the self-organizing merchants meet, informally, to do the vacant magistrate's job for him. Not open to outsiders by default, but exactly where someone would go to start building influence in a town quietly changing hands. See "Standing tensions" below.

## Why the party comes here

- **To equip properly.** Gallowmere and Thornwick can't sell what a levelling party needs.
- **To research the Crown from the orthodox side.** Venn's archive is the counterweight to Concordat doctrine — and the orthodox account is the one that says what the Crown *costs*.
- **To identify what they're carrying.** Vestry & Cole is the nearest realistic option.
- **Because the magistracy is empty.** An ungoverned market town of 4,000 is a power-base target an order of magnitude above anything in Gallowmere. → `power-base.md`

## Standing tensions

- **Orthodoxy vs. the Choir.** Prior Venn knows Choir cells are operating in the region and has no means to act. He would be *deeply* grateful to anyone who dealt with one — for instance, the cell at the Ash-Kilns. That gratitude comes with archive access and clerical legitimacy.
- **The empty magistracy.** Karsgate has not sent a replacement. Local merchants have started organizing their own arrangements, which is how towns quietly change hands.
- **Deserter tolls** on the Thornwick road are strangling market traffic. The merchants would fund someone to fix it.

## Selling & buying

Harrowgate is a real market, better than Thornwick's single trading post. Selling follows the RAW baseline (`items-and-loot.md`): **50% of assessed value** for standard SRD-priced goods (weapons, armor, mundane gear) is the anchor, with a Persuasion/Deception haggling check moving the actual price somewhat above or below it (strong roll → near 60-65%, weak roll → near 35-40%) rather than a pre-shifted band. *(Corrected 2026-08-26 — this previously read "50-70%," which drifted from the actual SRD rule; see `bug-log.md`.)* Full SRD prices apply when *buying* — this is a real market, not a village post charging a markup.

**Monster trophies and non-SRD curios** (heads, claws, hides, components) have no listed price — value them case by case against the creature's CR and the buyer's actual use for the part, then apply the same 50% anchor. A CR-3 owlbear's head and claws, sold to an apothecary/alchemist (Vestry & Cole is the obvious buyer) for their potential component value, land in the **15-30 gp** range before haggling.
