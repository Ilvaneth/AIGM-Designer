# Thornwick

Small village north of the capital, ~4-5 days from Gallowmere. ~35-40 households, mostly wooden buildings, moss-covered roofs. Living under the shadow of the succession war — tense, wary of outsiders, half the households look ready to flee at short notice. A half-scraped royal emblem survives on one house wall, left neither fully erased nor repainted.

Home to the old **Vaelthorn ancestral crypt** on the hill just north — see `info/locations/vaelthorn-crypt.md`.

## Locations

- **The Log** — tavern and inn, the village's social hub, run by **Hesta Bram**. A low-stakes dice game runs most evenings. Rooms available. She won't buy goods — she'll point sellers at Corran. See `info/npcs/hesta-bram.md`.
- **Corran's trading post** — on the square. The honest place to buy and sell. Also a moneylender, land speculator, and keeper of a locked collateral room worth robbing. See `info/npcs/corran.md`.
  - **Buying prices**: trinket-tier goods (small jewelry, minor grave finds, curios) fetch **2-5 gp** each. Corran pays the RAW baseline (`items-and-loot.md`) — **50% of market value** as the anchor — on anything with a real SRD price, haggling (Persuasion/Deception) moving it somewhat either way, in line with his "fair, not generous" character. *(Corrected 2026-08-26 — this previously read "40-60% of market value," which drifted from the actual SRD rule; see `bug-log.md`.)* He does **not** buy livestock carrying a brand he doesn't recognize — he's a speculator, not a fence, and won't take title risk on a horse that can be traced back to its owner.
- **The smithy** — Berrin, a competent village smith with no apprentice left (the boy went south). Repairs, basic gear, will do commission work slowly. Complains constantly that good iron stopped coming through months ago — a supply problem the party could solve, and be owed for.
- **The old shrine** — at the village edge, tended by **Sister Mave**, elderly and nearly the only person in Thornwick who'll talk to a stranger unprompted. Keeps the village's burial records going back generations, including Vaelthorn-era entries. A genuine research resource for anyone chasing Crown-era history, and she has no idea what she's sitting on.
- **The empty houses** — a growing number. Corran owns most of them now.

## Standing tensions

- The deserters at Sorrel's Hollow are choking the village's trade road. See `info/locations/sorrels-hollow.md`.
- Hesta and Corran openly dislike each other, and the village is quietly taking sides.
- Families are still leaving. The Wend family already did, and went to Gallowmere — where the party conned them. See `info/npcs/alis-wend.md`.
