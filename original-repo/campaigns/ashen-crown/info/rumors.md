# Rumor Pool

Roll d12 or pick when the party drinks, listens, asks around, or buys gossip. Fennick Orle is the best single source; taverns, refugee camps, and markets all work.

**Tag key**: `[TRUE]` accurate · `[HALF]` true but distorted · `[FALSE]` wrong, sometimes dangerously

Log which rumors have been given out at the bottom so they don't repeat.

## Situational rumors

Use these when the timing fits, not on a roll:

- After the Concordat convoy reaches Gallowmere: *"Black-robes at the graveyard all morning. Somebody got there first and they are not happy."* `[TRUE]`
- After the horse theft becomes known: *"Demirnal's offering coin for word on two horses. Says he'll know the buyer by the brand."* `[TRUE]`
- Once Alis Wend realizes she was conned: *"Two of them. An elf and a dragonborn. Told her they'd found her a home."* `[TRUE]` — and this one names the party
