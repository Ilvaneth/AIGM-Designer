## Setup

Following the unnamed rival relic-hunter (first met and let go on the road the previous night, then deliberately tailed once she turned out to be heading for the same "sealed door" rumor) the party watched her enter the Cave Mouth (Room 1) and press on into Room 2 without pausing. Moments later, a scream — a Cockatrice erupted from the dark and sank its beak into her shoulder. She began turning to stone, failed her first save, then wrenched free on her second, gray skin retreating to flesh, bleeding and shaken but alive. Ilvaneth watched from the doorway with Kriv right behind; the party chose to intervene rather than let her die.

## Aftermath — the relic-hunter

Kriv turned on the still-recovering woman: *"Buralar sana göre değil haa burada ne arıyorsan, her şeyi anlat, elindeki haritayı da bana ver... Sonra sana önerim arkana bile bakmadan buradan uzaklaş ki ölüp kalma..."* **Intimidation: 9+5=14** — trivially enough, given she'd just nearly died and was facing two armed strangers. Her resistance collapsed; she handed over her map (surface detail only — the entrance location and a few external markings, no real dungeon-layout intelligence) and fled the cave without looking back. She gave no name and is not expected to reappear.
