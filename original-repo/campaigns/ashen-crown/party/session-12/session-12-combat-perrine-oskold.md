## Setup

Night 1 on the Cindermoor road toward The Maker's Undoing (after the party's earlier redirect toward Harrowgate was retconned and deleted by the DM — see conversation log). A lantern-lit figure was spotted near a stone barrow: a gnome woman, middle-aged, in a Concordat grey-ash travel cloak, hood down, entirely absorbed in cleaning carved symbols with fine brushes — the same regional survey assignment Nessa Wren had been on. Kriv approached from behind, hood up as per his standing habit, intending a direct kill as with the previous archivist.

**Surprise**: Contested — Kriv's Stealth failed to beat her passive Perception, so she was alerted before any attack landed. No surprise round for either side.

## Post-Combat

Ilvaneth looted the body and the abandoned worksite: a survey notebook (ash-king symbol rubbings, matching Nessa Wren's format), Concordat travel authorization papers bearing Sarelle Duskbourne's seal and naming her **Perrine Oskold**, and 15 gp (split 8/7). The notebook's most recent page held an unfinished note — she had been overdue to compare findings with "N. Wren" at a planned rendezvous, a meeting that never happened.

The party carried the body to a quiet spot; Ilvaneth cast Gentle Repose (ritual, no slot). Kriv placed her in the Bag of Holding, beside Nessa Wren's already-preserved body.

**Consequence**: Perrine's own note is the first hard link between the two disappearances. `info/consequences.md`'s "Ashlord Concordat — Missing Archivists" threshold advanced to **2/3** — the party now holds two preserved Concordat archivists' bodies, and nobody at the Concordat yet knows either is missing.
