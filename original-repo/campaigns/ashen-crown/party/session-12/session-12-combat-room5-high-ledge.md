## Setup

Room 4 (Old Stair) came first: a narrow, dark stairway descending into the mountain, with a rockslide trap rigged into the loose stonework. Ilvaneth's passive Perception (15) spotted the danger before it triggered (DC 13) — Kriv jumped clear over the weak steps, Ilvaneth eased carefully along the edge. No damage, no combat.

Beyond it, Room 5 opened onto a high ledge — a Griffon's nest. The party pressed on stealthily, unfamiliar with the dungeon and unwilling to walk any room in blind.

**Surprise**: Contested. Kriv's Stealth (9+1=10) failed against the Griffon's passive Perception (15, Keen Sight) — spotted. Ilvaneth's Stealth (13+4=17) succeeded — she stayed hidden. The Griffon fixed on Kriv, feathers bristling, readying to strike.
