## Setup

Moving on from Room 2 (Resonance Key 1 and Journal Fragment 1 in hand), the party advanced carefully — Ilvaneth scouting ahead, Kriv close behind. Ilvaneth's Stealth (12+4=16) beat the two Cockatrice's passive Perception (11) cleanly, spotting the pair nesting close together around a bone pile in a natural cave chamber, mid-sized with a low ceiling but real room to maneuver.

**Surprise — corrected mid-scene**: Kriv's own Stealth check came back a natural 1 — an automatic failure, not a near-miss. His boot caught a bone with a sharp crack; both Cockatrice turned and fixed on him. Ilvaneth remained unseen. *(The DM's first pass had wrongly ruled Kriv "stopped in time, unnoticed" — corrected immediately per the DM's direct catch: a nat-1 total is an automatic failure, full stop, regardless of modifier.)*

## Round 1

**► Ilvaneth Duskmere** — Fireball, 3rd-level slot. Cockatrice A's DEX save (11 vs DC 15) failed — full damage. Cockatrice B's DEX save (16 vs DC 15) succeeded — half damage. Damage (8d6 fire): **4, 4, 5, 6, 5, 5, 6, 3 = 38 total.**
- **Cockatrice A**: 38 damage — instantly reduced to ash, far past its 27 HP.
- **Cockatrice B**: half of 38 = **19 damage** — badly burned, feathers scorched. *Cockatrice B at 8/27 HP.*

**► Kriv Shestendeliath** — Two attacks at Cockatrice B (8/27 HP): **18+9=27** and **8+9=17**, both vs AC 11 — **hit.** Damage (1d12+6 radiant): first attack **11→17** — kills Cockatrice B outright. Second attack's damage roll not needed; target already dead.

*Neither Cockatrice ever took a turn — both died before their own initiative came up.*

## Combat Over

Both Cockatrice dead, one round, no damage to either PC. Rooms 1-3 complete.

## Room search / salvage

Ilvaneth searched the room (nothing hidden here, unlike Room 2 — just old animal remains and broken shells). Kriv checked the bodies (nothing carried). Kriv then attempted to harvest the Cockatrice petrification glands (Nature check, Ilvaneth assisting for advantage): raw rolls **9, 11 → 11+0=11 vs DC 13**, a near-miss/partial success. One gland (and its beak) came out cleanly; the second was ruined in the attempt. **Result: 1 intact petrification gland recovered** — a rare, unsold alchemical trade good, buyer/use not yet determined.
