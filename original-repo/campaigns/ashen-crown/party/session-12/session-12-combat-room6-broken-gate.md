**► Kriv Shestendeliath** — Still unseen, attacking Cockatrice A (27/27 HP) with advantage on the first strike. Attack 1 (advantage): **14+9=23** vs AC 11 — **Hit.** Attack 2 (now visible after the first swing, flat roll): **13+9=22** vs AC 11 — **Hit.** Damage (1d12+6 radiant each): **12→18** and **10→16** — **34 radiant damage total.** Cockatrice A dies instantly.
