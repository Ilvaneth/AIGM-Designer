### Morning — Harrowgate → road to Ostwick — Departure & continuity correction

Session opened with a state-drift correction: Night 1 of the Harrowgate→Ostwick leg (a merchant convoy, rumor #12 traded) had already been played at the tail end of Session 7, but `state.md` had never been advanced to match. ### Morning, Day 49 — the road to Ostwick — Wereboar sign

Continuing the journey, the party found fresh, unusual tracks on the path — a warning sign nearby reading "don't approach." Kriv and Ilvaneth attempted to read the sign (Survival, DC 13): Kriv 11+1=12, Ilvaneth 2+2=4 — both failed. The trail was ambiguous: mixed two-legged/four-legged prints, unclear age, lost where the ground turned rocky. The party chose not to pursue further and continued toward Ostwick.

### Afternoon, Day 49 — Ostwick — First arrival

*Cinematic (first arrival): the ridge opened onto tilled fields, a distant windmill; Ostwick's rooftops with afternoon smoke; the village well and empty market stalls; a curious child darting off to spread word of strangers. Landed on Kriv's boots on the damp road and Ilvaneth's eyes finding the Hollow Sheaf's sign.*

Ostwick added to Visited Locations.

### Afternoon — The Hollow Sheaf — Wren Talbot

Full exchange logged separately: `session-8-npc-01-wren-talbot.md`.

Summary: entered the tavern, met innkeeper **Wren Talbot** (halfling, 50s). Kriv asked about the warning sign and tracks; Wren gave the village's standard "old boar" cover story. Kriv pressed ("İzler bir domuzunkinden çok daha büyük bir canlıya işaret ediyor... beni kandırmaya mı çalışıyorsun") — Wren grew visibly defensive. Insight check (Kriv 16+1=17, Ilvaneth 16+2=18 vs DC 14, both succeeded): confirmed she was covering for someone, not lying about an animal — no name revealed. Kriv smoothed it over ("sadece yoldan geçiyoruz... adını hatırlayamıyorum ama kimi arıyorsak") without naming Corvin, ordered drinks, asked about the Verrick family. Wren identified them, pointed the party to the old Corley farmstead, and mentioned a "sabırsız antikacı" who'd visited repeatedly, last seen 2 days prior, said he'd return in a few days.

### Midday — The Corley Farmstead — Halvard Verrick

Full exchange logged separately: `session-8-npc-02-halvard-verrick.md`.

Summary: found **Halvard Verrick** (half-elf, 40s-50s), the family patriarch, guarded but let the party in after Kriv's opening ("elinizde çıkarmak istediğiniz mallar varmış" — corrected to "ailenin eşyaları" by Halvard). Ilvaneth appraised the sale items (Investigation 14+7=21): silver jewelry box (~20 gp), a small painting (~40 gp), worked stones (~50 gp), and a family genealogy book (low resale value, clearly not meant for sale). Kriv drew on shared "fallen house" history (Persuasion 16+5=21) — Halvard opened up about a rival dealer who'd lowballed the family's most valuable heirlooms (grandmother's tiara, father's seal) three weeks prior. Ilvaneth asked for the genealogy book out of honest curiosity; Halvard initially refused, then relented after Ilvaneth's sincere explanation (Persuasion 18+1=19) and gave it to her freely. Final sale: 90 gp for the three items (offered by the party, accepted as "adil, gerçekten adil" against the rival's earlier lowball). Halvard named the rival dealer's overheard nickname: "Marsh."

### Afternoon — back at The Hollow Sheaf — the antiques dealer

Returned to Wren, bought two more drinks (Kriv overpaid by 1 gp as a tip). Asked about the dealer; Wren confirmed: stayed at her tavern repeatedly, impatient, complained about prices, last seen 2 days prior, said "birkaç güne dönerim."

### Afternoon — approaching the village — a frightened villager

On the way back toward the village, stopped a dwarf farmhand and asked about the sign/tracks. He repeated the same rehearsed "old boar" story, less smoothly than Wren. Kriv pressed directly, accusing him of lying (Intimidation 9+5=14 vs DC 13, succeeded). He broke: confirmed it's not an animal — a man from the village, cursed eight years ago defending it, still alive in the woods, protected by the whole village. Named the family: **Ashe**. Begged the party not to hurt him.

### Late afternoon — The Ashe Farmstead — Maren Ashe

Met **Maren Ashe** (human, weathered), Dunnel's sister, already aware strangers had been asking pointed questions in the village. Kriv opened with genuine sympathy ("Kaybınızı duyduk, geçmiş olsun") — Maren corrected the framing (he's not dead) but visibly softened, since no one had approached her this way before. Asked why the party was really there; the conversation moved to the curse itself. Ilvaneth asked how it happened — Maren recounted the bandit raid eight years ago, Dunnel taking the curse defending the village, withdrawing into the woods afterward. She asked directly whether Ilvaneth knew anything about curses, hoping for a cure.

**Arcana check (Ilvaneth, natural 20 + 7 = 27)**: confirmed lycanthropy is a true magical curse, and that *Remove Curse* (3rd-level Abjuration, confirmed on the Wizard list) can end it — genuine, credible information, not yet in Ilvaneth's spellbook. Maren was visibly moved — the first real hope she'd had in eight years. The party chose not to push further (no meeting with Dunnel arranged this session) and left on that note.

### Evening — The Hollow Sheaf — card game & the wait for Marsh

The party stayed the night at the inn. Ilvaneth joined a small, casual card game (Insight 17+2=19, full bet) and won roughly 12 gp over the evening. A tipsy fellow player passed along tavern gossip — Rumor #9 (Mother Vey's daughter in the capital) — delivered here.

### Day 50 — Ostwick — waiting, Detect Thoughts, and the ambush

The party spent the day watching for Marsh's return, reasoning (correctly) that the timing lined up. He arrived at the tavern that afternoon — a tiefling, sharp-dressed, visibly impatient. Ilvaneth cast Detect Thoughts, reading his surface thoughts (frustration, financial worry, considering leaving the region). She then attempted to probe deeper; his Wisdom save succeeded (rolled 20) — the probe failed and he became aware someone had touched his mind, though not who. The party let him leave and followed him at a distance (Stealth: Ilvaneth 16+4=20 succeeded unnoticed; Kriv's higher of two advantage rolls, 11+1=12, failed narrowly — a stray footstep made Marsh tense and quicken his pace, but he didn't spot anyone).

He led them into a narrow, empty alley. The party had already decided, going in, to kill him and take his goods.

**Combat — the alley ambush** (see `info/encounters/ostwick-alley-ambush.md` for the full stat block): initiative Ilvaneth 21, Kriv 12, Marsh 9 (surprised). Round 1: Ilvaneth's Fire Bolt hit (5+7=12 vs AC 12) for 7 fire damage. Kriv, with Extra Attack and advantage (Marsh still unaware): two hits with the Barrow-King's Blade (10+9=19 and 18+9=27, both vs AC 12) for 11 and 15 radiant damage — Marsh died before he ever acted. 100 XP each.

Kriv folded the body and Marsh's bag into the Bag of Holding entirely — no corpse, no evidence beyond a small blood stain on the alley stones. Searched later at the inn: 45 gp, a personal business ledger (routes, client names, no faction ties), the Verrick grandmother's tiara (~150 gp), the Verrick father's seal ring (~20 gp, sentimental, not yet returned), and a handful of minor antiques (~30 gp).

### Day 50-51 — road to Harrowgate — return leg

Departed Ostwick that evening. Night's travel (Thornlands table, roll 5 — merchant convoy, minor news exchange, no incident). Arrived Harrowgate the morning of day 51.

### Morning, Day 51 — Harrowgate — The Hanged Man & The Sable Casket

Took a room at The Hanged Man. Went to Corvin Thale's shop to deliver the Verrick job. First attempt at explaining "no complications" was walked back when the DM flagged that no actual details had been given yet — restarted the scene properly.

Laid out the jewelry box, painting, worked stones, and the tiara. Corvin recognized the tiara immediately — he'd heard it was already sold to a rival dealer, and asked pointedly how they got it back. Kriv deflected ("sen tacı istiyor musun istemiyor musun de bize"); Corvin caught the dodge but still wanted the piece, offering 120 gp for it with a warning that if this came back on him, the arrangement would change.

Ilvaneth gave a false account (Deception 13+4=17 vs DC 15, succeeded): claimed the full 150 gp advance went to buying the tiara from "a merchant," and that the party spent 100 gp of their own on the rest — hiding both the killing and the real 90 gp cost. Corvin paid out 265 gp total (100 gp "reimbursement" + 45 gp job cut + 120 gp for the tiara), never learning the truth. The 60 gp genuinely left over from his original advance was never mentioned.

Corvin then showed two new items for sale: a Wand of Magic Missiles (300 gp) and a Ring of Protection (900 gp). Kriv negotiated with Ilvaneth's Help (20+5=25): both discounted to 250 gp and 750 gp. Ilvaneth took both — the Ring later attuned during a short rest.

### Late morning — Merchants' Assembly Hall — Renata Kroll

Went to answer Renata Kroll's standing-force offer, pending since session 4. Kriv deferred again, citing gathering resources first. Renata's patience visibly shortened — a direct warning that she wouldn't wait forever. Kriv responded by telling her to "solve it your own way," warning that haste would mean failure. Renata took this as a real decline: she's now looking for another candidate. `consequences.md`'s "Renata Kroll — The Standing Force" threshold crossed (3/3) and was applied immediately — this specific Harrowgate power-base opportunity is closed.

### Afternoon — The Ashgate Conservatory — Yveth Corrane

Met **Magus Yveth Corrane** (gnome), previously an unnamed background NPC now properly filed. Browsed her stock: Counterspell scroll (150 gp), Dispel Magic scroll (130 gp), a Headband of Intellect (retracted mid-scene — the party already sits at 18 INT/STR, a standing "don't offer flat stat-floor items" rule was written down properly this session), and — in its place — an Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location (500 gp), directly relevant to the Concordat's active search for "an elf and a dragonborn."

The party bought the Amulet (500 gp) and the Dispel Magic scroll (130 gp), and asked about *Remove Curse* — not in stock, but Yveth agreed to source it through her network for 220 gp, due on delivery, about a week out (tracked as a new clock in `campaign-clock.md`, ~day 58).

Session ended here, at the Conservatory, day 51.
