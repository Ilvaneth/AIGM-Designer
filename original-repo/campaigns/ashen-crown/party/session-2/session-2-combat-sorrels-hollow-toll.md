## Setup

The party stopped short of the toll point to scout rather than walk in openly. Ilvaneth (Perception 20) and Kriv (Perception 13) both spotted the four deserters spread across the road ahead — relaxed, not expecting trouble, one holding a crossbow pointed at the ground. Smoke from the camp was visible beyond them. The party chose to ambush rather than parley: dismounted, left the horses tied south, and moved up through the west treeline. Ilvaneth cast Mage Armor (AC 14 → 17) before closing. Both PCs closed to ambush range undetected — Ilvaneth (Stealth 20) and Kriv (Stealth 15), both clearing the deserters' passive Perception of 10.

Deserters A–D were arrayed west to east across the road at roughly 5 ft intervals. Ilvaneth advanced 25 ft (of her 30 ft speed) to stand 5 ft from A, positioning to catch A, B, and C in Thunderwave's 15-ft cube while leaving D (20 ft off) out of range. Kriv held position at the road's edge, between Ilvaneth and A, ready to charge.

**Surprise**: One side surprised — all four deserters were unaware of the party and surprised for their first turn.
