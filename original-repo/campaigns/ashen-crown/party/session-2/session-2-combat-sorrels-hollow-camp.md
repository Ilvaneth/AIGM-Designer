## Setup

The party approached the clearing at pre-dawn after successfully scouting in (Stealth: Kriv raw 9+1=10, Ilvaneth raw 16+4=20, both clearing/matching the sentries' passive Perception of 10). Kriv opened by sneaking up on the dozing fire-sentry for a surprise attack — resolved as his round 1 action once initiative was set, with advantage for the unaware target.

**Surprise**: Sentry 1 was unaware and killed before it could act. Sentry 2, alert but not yet alarmed, was not surprised. Roskel and Deserter 3, asleep in tents, were surprised for their first turn (per the noise-and-detection standing rule: a single non-vocal kill doesn't wake sleepers).

## Round 3

**► Ilvaneth Duskmere** — **Thunderwave** (Self, 15-ft cube), positioned to include Roskel but not Kriv. Roskel's CON save: **18** (correct mod +1, not +2 as first stated) vs DC 13 — **success**, half damage, no push. Damage: raw 2d8 (6+7=13), halved = **6**. Roskel: **58 → 52/58 HP**.

**► Kriv Shestendeliath** — **Second Wind** (bonus action): raw 8 + 2 = **10 HP**. Kriv: **6 → 16/23 HP**. **Attack** (Greataxe): raw 14+5 = **19** vs AC 17 — **hit**. Damage: raw 8, +3 = **11**. Roskel: **52 → 41/58 HP**. **Action Surge** — second attack: raw 16+5 = **21** vs AC 17 — **hit**. Damage: raw 10, +3 = **13**. Roskel: **41 → 28/58 HP**.

**► Roskel** — Multiattack (2× Longsword) at Kriv: **18** and **8** vs AC 16 — one **hit**, one **miss**. Damage: **7**. Kriv: **16 → 9/23 HP**.

## Round 4

**► Ilvaneth Duskmere** — **Burning Hands** (Self, 15-ft cone), positioned to exclude Kriv. Roskel's DEX save: raw 6 + 1 = **7** vs DC 13 — **fail**, full damage. Damage: raw 3d6 (4+4+4=12). Roskel: **28 → 16/58 HP**.

**► Kriv Shestendeliath** — Attack: raw 12+5 = **17** vs AC 17 — **hit** (exact). Damage: raw 8, +3 = **11**. Roskel: **16 → 5/58 HP**.

**► Roskel** — At 5 HP, breaks off rather than pressing the attack. Drops his weapon on Kriv's demand and opens a parley — recognizes Kriv's fighting style as Shestendeliath (Ash-Warden), confirms the house fell fast, by betrayal, with Ironclad Compact swords involved, and that he doesn't know who commissioned it. Accepts Kriv's offer to join — the entire rest of his camp (11 others, across this fight and the earlier toll fight) is already dead, which he only learns when the party tells him directly.

## Key Moments

1. A clean stealth opener took out the fire-sentry before the fight even properly began.
2. Roskel's Multiattack proved genuinely dangerous — 17 damage in one round nearly ended Kriv, the first real brush with death since level 1.
3. Kriv's Second Wind + Action Surge turn (heal, then two full attacks) was the swing that broke Roskel's HP total in half.
4. Careful spell positioning (Thunderwave, then Burning Hands) let Ilvaneth hit Roskel without ever catching Kriv in the blast — geometry checked both times per table standing rule.
5. Roskel's surrender and recruitment closes out a Kriv-thread beat (`kriv-thread.md`) and stands up the campaign's first-ever companion, via a rules gap found and fixed live (`info/companions.md`).
