### Day 87 — The Drowned Cathedral — full clear

First arrival, cinematic wide→close delivered. Entered via the Vestry Air-Pocket (Room 3, alternate dry route — Kriv led, Athletics 24) rather than the main flooded stair.

- **Room 4** (Priesthood's Quarters): Investigation 23 found a priest's fear-filled note — *"...Taç bir insanı taşıyamaz demiştim, kimse dinlemedi... Eğer bunu okuyorsan ve ben burada değilsem, aşağı inme."*
- **Room 5** (Reliquary): ceremonial forging tools recovered (tongs, hammer, measuring cord) for Corvin Thale; an inventory plaque reveals "the Measure of Ash" is missing from the original set.
- **Room 6** (Wight's Vigil): 2 Wight, above-Deadly at level 7 (4,200 adjusted). Full stealth approach (both PCs rolled 18 vs passive Perception 13) — total surprise. Kriv's opening natural-20 Goading crit (56 dmg) killed Wight B outright; redirected attacks killed Wight A next round. **Zero party damage.** 1,400 XP total.
- **Room 7** (Flooded Nave): a Shambling Mound, engaged from the Choir Loft (Room 8) at 70-80 ft — beyond its 60 ft blindsight, so it never once perceived the party across 7 rounds of Toll the Dead/Fire Bolt/Blight. 900 XP total, zero damage taken.
- **Room 9** (Drowned Forge Chamber): Arcana 22 confirmed the Crown's reassembly ritual requires a living vessel/conduit — both PCs' ascension stages are a controlled preview of what full assembly would do, permanently.
- **Room 10** (Elemental's Deep): a Water Elemental, no surprise (Kriv's Stealth fell short of passive Perception). A real fight — Kriv dropped to 38/87 from one critical hit, healed via Second Wind + Hit Dice back to full during a subsequent short rest. 900 XP total.
- **Room 2** (Flooded Narthex, later): Room 1's patrolling Water Elemental encountered here instead. Another real fight (Kriv took a second crit, 23 dmg). 900 XP total.

**DM feedback on rewards, verbatim**: "Bu Dungeona maddi ödül konulmamış, bu kadar Dungeon bir eşya çıkmalı. Dm bir eşya ver Dungeondan." — a **Ring of Free Action** (Rare) was added, found in the Elemental's Deep, given to Ilvaneth (carried, not yet attuned — her 3 attunement slots are full). `dungeon-design.md` updated with a permanent standing rule: non-Treasure-heavy Payoff types still owe real material reward scaled to combat effort.

Short rest taken after Room 10: Kriv spent Second Wind (16 HP) + 4 Hit Dice (41 HP) to return to full; all short-rest features restored. Ilvaneth's retroactively-missed Blight slot (cast against the Shambling Mound, never deducted at the time) was corrected, then Arcane Recovery restored it.

Site fully cleared — all 10 rooms resolved.
