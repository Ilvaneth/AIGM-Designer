## Session 11 Summary

No in-fiction play occurred. The party remains exactly where session 10 left them — outside Kesh Deeps, the Cindermoor, day 56, evening. The entire session was DM-Claude collaborative design and tooling work, triggered by a post-session observation that Kesh Deeps “didn’t feel like a dungeon, felt like an invaded mine.” Investigation traced this to `dungeon-design.md` having no vocabulary for a site’s structural type, and to Kesh Deeps’ ecology/lore rooms going undelivered without anything flagging it.

The core fix: **Phase 0** was added to `dungeon-design.md`, adopting the DMG’s six-type dungeon taxonomy (Ruins/Lair/Maze/Mine/Planned Dungeon/Cave Complex) alongside a separate **Payoff type** field (Treasure-heavy/Strategic-info/Territory). A standing rule now requires every dungeon’s Room-by-room table to carry an explicit Category column and the **DMG content-ratio check** (`dungeon_content_ratio_check.py`) to be run before any site is presented to the party — verifying room mix falls within DMG stocking bands (~50–60% Combat, 10–15% Trap, 10–15% Special, 15–20% Structural). Refined twice in session: Payoff-type-aware bands (Mine/Strategic-info sites get more Lore headroom, since Kesh Deeps’ 22%-combat/44%-lore shape was the cautionary extreme a flat universal band could not distinguish from a well-built mine), and a Maze-type override setting the combat floor to 40–50% (Maze deprioritizes combat by its own definition). A **Location class** header (`Dungeon` / `Non-dungeon`) was added to every location file after a sweep-queue accidentally included The Gallow Tree — explicitly labeled “not a dungeon” in its own file — because nothing had ever read that label mechanically. `location_class_check.py` now makes it a one-command check.

Ten existing unplayed Dungeon-class sites were upgraded to the new standard — Phase 0 type/payoff tags, Category columns, and ratio-verified. Group A (five sites already at full “Dungeon” depth, needing only new header tags and ratio verification): Riverwatch, The Weeping Wood, The Drowned Cathedral, The Unremembered Shrine, The Chalk Warrens. Group B (four “Small dungeon” candidates expanded to full room-by-room structure): The Drowned Mill, The Tithe Barn (+1 room and a Shambling Mound boss per DM request), The Gnoll Battlefield, The Feral Menagerie. Four new dungeons were built from scratch: **The Maker’s Undoing** (Cindermoor, Planned Dungeon/Vault, 20 rooms — built end-to-end to test the new standard, artificer Marek Stillwright); **The Debased Mint** (Karsgate undercity, 18 rooms — House Doskarn’s secret ash-mint, a confirmed Crown fragment site, 9 distinct creature types, a deliberate thematic mirror to Kriv’s ascension: the family was consumed by unmanaged exposure to their own fragment, and is still there, transformed); **Ashvale Necropolis** (22 rooms, the Chapter 1 climax — Concordat garrison vs. the necropolis’s own Ashvale ghost-guardians, an exploitable faction conflict; Sarelle survives this site by design, she is the ongoing central antagonist, not Chapter 1 content to conclude); and **The Unquiet Barrow** (Thornlands, 18 rooms — the campaign’s first Maze-type dungeon, self-rearranging rooms, a single recurring Xorn predator narrated as the same creature reappearing, whoever is buried there deliberately never named). The campaign now holds 14 fully-standard, Category-tagged, DMG-ratio-verified Dungeon-class sites.
