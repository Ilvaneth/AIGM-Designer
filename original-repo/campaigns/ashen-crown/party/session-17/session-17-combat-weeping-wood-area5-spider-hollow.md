## Round 1

**► Giant Spider C** — Bite at Kriv: miss.

**► Giant Spider A** — Bite at Ilvaneth: hit, 4 piercing damage → *Ilvaneth 41/45*. Poison follow-up: DC 11 CON save, Ilvaneth rolled 17+3=**20**, success — half poison damage. **4 poison damage** (9 halved) → *Ilvaneth 37/45.*

**► Ilvaneth Duskmere** — Fireball (3rd-level slot) centered on the web ceiling, targeting Spider B and D (spell geometry checked first: the two floor spiders were already in melee contact with the PCs, so a blast reaching them would also catch Kriv/Ilvaneth — only the ceiling pair was a clean target). Spell Save DC 15.
  - Spider B: DEX save 7+3=10 — fail.
  - Spider D: DEX save 2+3=5 — fail.
  - Damage: 8d6 = 6+6+5+2+4+6+6+1 = **36 fire damage** to both. *Spider B and D — DEAD* (26 HP each).

**► Kriv Shestendeliath** — Extra Attack at Spider C:
  - Attack 1: 8+9=17 vs AC 14 — hit. Damage 9+6=**15** radiant → *Spider C 11/26.*
  - Attack 2 (Goading Attack, 1 Superiority Die spent): 9+9=18 vs AC 14 — hit. Damage 8(d12)+3(d8)+6=**17** radiant → *Spider C DEAD* (32 total).

  Great Weapon Master bonus action offered and taken (killed Spider C with a melee weapon) — bonus attack at Spider A, then Action Surge declared for two more attacks, all resolved one hit at a time:
  - GWM bonus attack: 7+9=16 vs AC 14 — hit. Damage 9+6=**15** → *Spider A 11/26.*
  - Action Surge attack 1: 9+9=18 vs AC 14 — hit. Damage 3+6=**9** → *Spider A 2/26.*
  - Action Surge attack 2: 12+9=21 vs AC 14 — hit. Damage 4+6=**10** → *Spider A DEAD* (overkill).
