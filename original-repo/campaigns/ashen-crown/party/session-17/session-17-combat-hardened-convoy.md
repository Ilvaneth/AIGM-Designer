## Setup

Reconnaissance from the treeline: Ilvaneth's Stealth (15) beat every observer's passive Perception (13) — fully undetected. Kriv's Stealth (10) did not; Concordat Priest B noticed his general position but hadn't raised alarm. Formation was deliberately spread per `two-player-scaling.md` (S1/S2, S3/S4, S5/S6 pairs ~40 ft apart, two wagons — V1 center, likely real cargo; V2 forward, likely decoy). A recon map was published (`Hardened Convoy` Artifact).

**Surprise**: partial. Priest B was NOT surprised (already alert to Kriv). Everyone else (Ysolde, Priest A, all 6 Skeletons) was surprised — initiative rolled for the whole encounter first per `combat-rules.md`'s no-free-round rule, then surprised combatants skipped their Round 1 turns.
