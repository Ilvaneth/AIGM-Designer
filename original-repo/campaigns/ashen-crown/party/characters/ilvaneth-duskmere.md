## Notes
Conditions: Shrine's favor (advantage vs natural/animal dangers, 24h, from day 61)


**Resolved, day 51**: Corvin's 150 gp advance was spent 90 gp on the Verrick collection (jewelry box, painting, worked stones), leaving 60 gp genuinely unaccounted for. When reporting back, Ilvaneth told Corvin a false accounting — that the full 150 gp went to buying the Verrick tiara "from a merchant" (actually looted off a killed rival dealer, "Marsh") and that the party spent 100 gp of their own on the rest (actually 90 gp, from his own advance). Deception 17 vs DC 15 — he believed it. Net effect: the 60 gp leftover advance is quietly kept, never mentioned or returned, and Corvin paid out 265 gp total (100 gp "reimbursement" + 45 gp job cut + 120 gp for the tiara) believing costs that were never actually incurred. A real, successful con — not logged anywhere Corvin can see.

**Backstory (confirmed session zero)**: Abandoned as a child when her con-artist mother's grift went bad and she vanished, Ilvaneth ended up in an orphanage on the edge of the Hollow Kingdoms. There she met a young, newly orphaned Dragonborn — Kriv — whose family had just been destroyed by betrayal and political intrigue. Neither had anyone else. Both learned early that the world takes what it wants and calls it fair — so they decided to do the taking themselves. Whatever either of them does to everyone else, they have never lied to or betrayed each other, and never will.

**Long-term goal (confirmed session zero)**: Transcend death itself. Power and reputation are means, not the end — what Ilvaneth actually wants is to never die, never be erased, never be nothing again the way she almost was as a child. She's drawn to forbidden lore on preserving the self beyond death, and the Ashen Crown's ash-magic — literally built from what's left of dead sovereigns — is exactly the kind of secret she's been hunting for. This should quietly steer her toward Necromancy/Abjuration-leaning choices and toward the Ashlord Concordat's archives as she levels, and pays off directly in the Chapter 2 endgame (lichdom / ascension via the assembled Crown is her personal victory condition, whether or not it aligns with Kriv's).
