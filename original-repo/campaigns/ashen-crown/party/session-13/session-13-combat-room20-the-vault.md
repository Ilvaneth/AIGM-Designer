## Key Moments

1. Kriv's Precision Attack + GWM combo (offsetting the -5 penalty with a Superiority Die on the roll) landing all 4 attacks for 81 damage in round 1 — nearly half the Golem's HP before it ever acted.
2. Correctly ruling Undead Thralls only boosts zombie damage, not to-hit, when asked directly.
3. The Golem's Berserk trigger turning on the party's own zombies instead of the PCs — Undead Fortitude failing for Zombie A (destroyed), and Zombie B surviving with a permanently halved max HP.
4. Ilvaneth's wand, recharged at the long rest's dawn, delivering a genuine 8-dart Magic Missile nova.
5. Zero party damage across a CR 9 boss fight, built with real HP margin specifically to avoid a first-round trivialization — the margin held, and the fight still took real rounds and cost real resources without ever threatening the PCs directly.
