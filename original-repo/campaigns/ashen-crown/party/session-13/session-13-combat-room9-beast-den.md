## Key Moments

1. Ilvaneth's natural 20 Fire Bolt crit opening the fight, followed immediately by Kriv's double natural-20 round — Manticore B went from full health to 2 HP before it ever got a turn.
2. A DM self-correction mid-fight: Manticore A's "Surprised" status was cleared once the fiction (a nearby packmate's violent death) made continued unawareness implausible — caught by direct DM question, logged as a real pattern to watch for.
3. Manticore B's fatal mistake — fleeing without Disengage, cut down by an opportunity attack mid-air.
4. Manticore A learning from that death and successfully escaping via Disengage — the fight's one real enemy tactical adaptation.
5. Kriv taking the session's first real damage (7 HP) — the whole night's earlier fights had gone entirely unscathed.
