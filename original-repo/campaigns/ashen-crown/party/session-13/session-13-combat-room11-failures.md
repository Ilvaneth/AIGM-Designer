## Setup

The party crossed from Wing 1 into Wing 2 — worked stone replacing raw cave, a dead ventilation system, Marek Stillwright's own maker's mark on the wall. Room 11 held a motionless "statue" (a Gargoyle, False Appearance). The party searched the room and talked openly with no stealth attempt; given the noise of Room 10's fight moments earlier, the DM ruled the Gargoyle had plausibly already noticed them and patiently waited, striking the moment they declared moving on "quietly."

**Surprise**: the party, not the Gargoyle — a direct, earned consequence of their own inconsistency (loud activity, then assumed stealth).

**► Kriv Shestendeliath** — The Barrow-King's Blade (+9) at the Gargoyle:

- Attack 2 (Distracting Strike, natural 20 — automatic crit): 2d12 (9,3) +6 = 18, + Superiority Die 2d8 (8,7) = 15. Total **33 radiant**. *(Distracting Strike's effect: Ilvaneth's next attack on this target gets advantage.)*
Total: **48 radiant damage**. *Gargoyle at 4/52 HP.*

**► Ilvaneth Duskmere** — Fire Bolt at the Gargoyle, **with advantage** (Distracting Strike): raw rolls **19, 3** — take 19. **19+7 = 26** vs AC 15 — **Hit**. Damage: 5+4 = **9 fire**. *Gargoyle at 0/52 HP — **DEAD**.*

## Key Moments

1. A DM self-correction before the fight even started: the party's own inconsistent behavior (loud searching, then assumed stealth) meant the Gargoyle had plausibly already noticed them — surprise flipped onto the party instead of the usual other way around.
2. Kriv's natural-20 Distracting Strike, both for the crit damage and for handing Ilvaneth's finishing Fire Bolt advantage.
3. A quick, clean kill despite the surprise reversal — the Gargoyle only ever landed one hit all fight.
