## Surface

**Race assigned day 62, session 14** — never previously stated in play; per `narration.md`'s race-variety rule, given one now that he's genuinely on-screen for the reunion at Harrowgate. A **half-orc**, broad and weathered, grey threading through a close-cropped beard. Carries himself with a soldier's stillness — economical movement, nothing wasted. A long scar crosses one tusk, old enough to have gone smooth.

## Background

Twenty-year man-at-arms, formerly House Vaelthorn. Discharged into nothing when the succession war broke his pay. Led the ~12-man Sorrel's Hollow deserter band until the party dismantled it — he is its **sole survivor**; everyone else died across the toll fight and the camp assault.

Recognized Kriv's fighting style as Shestendeliath / Ash-Warden — seen among house guards in the capital years ago, not something he was personally taught. Confirmed the house fell fast, by betrayal, with **Ironclad Compact** swords involved (hired mercenaries, not the house's own men). Doesn't know who commissioned or covered it up — the honest limit of a sergeant's-eye view of capital intrigue. Pointed toward Compact contract records as the only lead he has, unknowingly toward **Coren Ashvale** (`info/kriv-thread.md`).

## What he wants

To serve something real again. Banditry was never who he was — it was what was left after the pay stopped and the house fell. A living Shestendeliath heir, asking for his sword, is closer to his actual life's purpose than anything he's done in years.

## Connections

- **Kriv** — the man he serves; his only real chain of command.
- **Ostrig Kelmar** — the hired foreman; Roskel oversees his crew's work day to day.
- **Ser Kethrax** — met day 65 at the household's first dinner; soldier-to-soldier respect ("we do the same work in this house now"), but no deep history.
- **Maren Ashe, Dunnel Ashe** — household staff under his watch.
- **No connection** to Sefwyn Marrow, Magus Orell Tain, or anyone in that research thread. If a scene calls for status on that thread, the honest answer from Roskel is that he doesn't know and has no way to find out.

## How to run him

- **Voice**: blunt, practical, no wasted words. A soldier, not a courtier.
- **Loyalty**: fiction only, no mechanic (`info/companions.md`). He follows Kriv specifically, out of respect for the house and relief at having purpose again — not blind obedience. Ask him for something that violates who he is (needless cruelty, dishonoring the dead, breaking his word) and he'll push back.
- **Good task fits**: reading a military situation, getting a straight answer out of soldiers/guards/deserters, anything physical (Athletics), assessing whether a plan is tactically sound.
