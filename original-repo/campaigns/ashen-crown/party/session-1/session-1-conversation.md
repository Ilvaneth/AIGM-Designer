# Session 1 — Conversation Archive

**Date**: 2026-08-23 · **Location**: Gallowmere

---

## Mother Vey's offer

Party accepted a job from Mother Vey: break the seal on the Gallowmere ossuary before an Ashlord Concordat convoy arrives (3-4 days out). Payment: an advance pouch (20 gp total, split 10/10), plus anything found inside. Vey doesn't know what's down there — only that the Concordat's interest means it's valuable.

## Recon — Fennick Orle

Party visited Ilvaneth's contact Fennick Orle (barkeep/info broker, "The Last Coin") before the job.

- Ilvaneth, Persuasion: rolled 17 (+1) = 18 — Fennick shared: convoy timeline (3-4 days), that the Grey Tally is working the same lead for a Concordat-linked buyer, and an old local legend about "ash-kings" buried to prevent their return.
- Ilvaneth tipped Fennick 2 gp on the way out. He added a warning about the ossuary's location under the old graveyard, in a collapsed section that people avoid.

## The ossuary — entrance

Party found the sealed entrance under a collapsed mausoleum in Gallowmere's old graveyard. A circular ward/seal (bone-and-flame motif, clearly maintained/renewed) covered the door.

- Ilvaneth, Investigation: rolled 19 (+5) = 24 — recognized the seal as a containment ward (keeping something *in*, not intruders *out*), found the "release point" in the pattern.
- Kriv, History: rolled 11 (+2) = 13 — partial recognition; the motif matches old royal funerary rites, predating the current dynasty (consistent with Fennick's "ash-kings" legend, no new specifics).
- Ilvaneth, Arcana (attempt to unravel the seal): rolled 11 (+5) = 16 — succeeded; seal dispersed quietly, no alarm triggered.

## The ossuary — interior

Descended into a bone-lined corridor, then a circular chamber: a warded chest on a central pedestal, and a seated, fully armored figure in a wall niche — dormant but visibly breathing (confirmed later as a genuine animated/magical guardian, not a statue).

Kriv held the stairs on watch; Ilvaneth approached alone.

- Ilvaneth, Stealth: rolled 16 (+4) = 20 — crossed the room to the chest without waking the guardian.
- Ilvaneth, Investigation (checking the chest's trigger plate for traps): rolled 9 (+5) = 14 — sensed something was off about the plate (residue, faint magical hum) but couldn't determine the safe method.
- Ilvaneth cast Detect Magic (ritual): revealed the plate (moderate abjuration), the guardian (strong necromancy — confirmed genuinely active/animated), and a faint, diffuse necromancy aura from the bone shelves throughout the room (ambient, not a new threat).
- Ilvaneth, Arcana (reading the ward's nature): rolled 10 (+5) = 15 — sensed the ward respond to an *offering* rather than force, tied to "what fire leaves behind."
- Player inference: ash. Party confirmed grave-ash was available in nearby funerary urns.
- Ilvaneth poured ash over the trigger plate — correct solution. Seal dispersed, chest opened, guardian stayed dormant throughout.

**Found**: a broken, ash-grey fragment (unidentified in-fiction; DM-known to be a piece of the Ashen Crown), plus minor grave goods (tarnished silver bracelet, a few old coins, a bone bead necklace).

Ilvaneth retreated to Kriv with the fragment (no new roll — conditions unchanged from the earlier successful stealth pass).

## Search before leaving

Party searched the chamber before leaving (trusting the guardian would stay dormant).

- Ilvaneth, Investigation: rolled 19 (+5) = 24 — found the grave goods; partially read worn wall text near the guardian's niche referencing a "vow" and "return."
- Kriv, Perception: rolled 13 (+1) = 14 — spotted fresh footprints just outside the entrance, not the party's own. Never followed up in-session.

Party confirmed (DM clarification) the ossuary is small — just the entry corridor and this one chamber, no hidden passages found despite the high search result.

## Reporting to Vey

Party returned to Mother Vey. She pressed for details beyond "the seal broke." Party gave a cover story: claimed they found only a dormant armored guardian and left without opening anything.

- Deception roll: 13 (+2, Kriv) = 15 — Vey wasn't fully convinced, but let it go for now ("Gallowmere's small. If you're keeping something, I'll know eventually.").

Party kept the advance payment (10 gp each) and the fragment. Session ended with the party resting for the night in Gallowmere.
