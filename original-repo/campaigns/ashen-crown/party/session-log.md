## Session 1 — 2026-08-23

**Location**: Gallowmere · **XP awarded**: 100 each (100/300 total)

Mother Vey hired Ilvaneth and Kriv to break the seal on the old ossuary beneath Gallowmere before an Ashlord Concordat convoy arrived to claim it "officially" — 3-4 days out. Before going in, they consulted Ilvaneth's contact Fennick Orle at The Last Coin, who confirmed the convoy timeline, revealed a rival fixer (the Grey Tally) was working the same lead for a Concordat buyer, and passed along an old legend about "ash-kings" buried to prevent their return.

At the ossuary, Ilvaneth identified the entrance seal as a containment ward (not a lock) and unraveled it cleanly. Inside, a dormant but genuinely animated armored guardian watched over a warded chest. Detect Magic confirmed the guardian's necromancy aura and the chest's abjuration ward; a follow-up Arcana check suggested the ward wanted an offering, not force. Ilvaneth poured grave-ash from a nearby urn onto the trigger plate — correct guess, ward dispelled safely, guardian never woke.

Inside the chest: a broken, ash-grey fragment (unidentified in-fiction — a piece of the Ashen Crown) plus minor grave goods. A search of the chamber turned up worn text referencing a "vow" and "return," and fresh footprints just outside the entrance — someone got close but didn't go in, never investigated further.

Back at Vey's, the party gave a cover story (claimed they found only a dormant guardian and fled). She wasn't fully convinced but didn't push. Session ended with the party turning in for the night.

**Open going into session 2**: identify the fragment, the unfollowed footprints, Vey's quiet suspicion, the convoy clock, and the still-unnamed hooks (who betrayed Kriv's house; Ilvaneth's mother's fate).

The party remains exactly where session 10 left them. The entire session was DM-Claude collaborative design and tooling work, triggered by the post-session observation that Kesh Deeps “didn’t feel like a dungeon, felt like an invaded mine.” The root cause: `dungeon-design.md` had no vocabulary for a site’s structural type, and Kesh Deeps’ ecology/lore rooms went undelivered in play without anything flagging it.

**Phase 0** was added to `dungeon-design.md` — the DMG’s six-type dungeon taxonomy (Ruins/Lair/Maze/Mine/Planned Dungeon/Cave Complex) plus a **Payoff type** field (Treasure-heavy/Strategic-info/Territory). A standing rule now requires every dungeon’s Room-by-room table to carry a Category column and a DMG content-ratio check (`dungeon_content_ratio_check.py`) before any site is presented — verifying room mix falls within DMG stocking bands. Refined twice: Payoff-type-aware bands (Mine/Strategic-info sites get more Lore headroom) and a Maze-type combat override (40–50%). A **Location class** header (`Dungeon` / `Non-dungeon`) was added to all location files after a sweep-queue accidentally included The Gallow Tree; `location_class_check.py` now makes that label a one-command check.

Ten existing unplayed sites were upgraded to the new standard. Group A (five full-standard sites, new header tags + ratio check only): Riverwatch, The Weeping Wood, The Drowned Cathedral, The Unremembered Shrine, The Chalk Warrens. Group B (four small-dungeon candidates expanded to full room-by-room): The Drowned Mill, The Tithe Barn, The Gnoll Battlefield, The Feral Menagerie. Four new dungeons built from scratch: **The Maker’s Undoing** (Cindermoor, Planned Dungeon/Vault, 20 rooms), **The Debased Mint** (Karsgate undercity, 18 rooms — House Doskarn’s fragment site, a thematic mirror to Kriv’s ascension), **Ashvale Necropolis** (22 rooms, Chapter 1 climax, Sarelle survives by design), **The Unquiet Barrow** (Thornlands, 18 rooms, the campaign’s first Maze-type dungeon). The campaign now holds 14 fully-standard, Category-tagged, DMG-ratio-verified Dungeon-class sites.
