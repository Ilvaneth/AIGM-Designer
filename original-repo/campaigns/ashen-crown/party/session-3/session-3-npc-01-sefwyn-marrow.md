## Conversation End

**Final disposition**: Warming

**Information revealed**:
- Sefwyn's three original offers: buy the fragment outright, buy information about it, or a mutual-access partnership.
- **Magus Orell Tain** exists — a senior Concordat archivist who opposes Sarelle's Crown project on scholarly/moral grounds, not corruption. He believes the last ash-king's assembly of the Crown produced something other than the ash-king himself, and wants the fragments catalogued and kept apart permanently. He is losing the argument internally and is isolated — an opening for the party.
- Confirmation (already partly known): Sarelle began excavating months *before* the Emperor's death — proof exists in the Ashvale archives. If Tain learns this, he would move against Sarelle openly.
- A warning about Hollowmoor Barrows: the deepest barrow (the face-down king) is a real danger, not just a legend — Sefwyn has been there and says not to rush in.
- Via Insight (21): Sefwyn's stated motive ("I'm a record-keeper, not a collector") reads as true but incomplete — this feels personal, not purely professional, possibly building toward independence from whoever they currently answer to.

**Information withheld**:
- Sefwyn's actual full motive: a private, Concordat-hidden tally built to eventually sell to the highest bidder (Compact, Choir, or anyone else) and disappear wealthy and unowned. Not exposed even under a 21 Insight check — only the "this is personal" impression came through.
- That the footprints outside the Gallowmere ossuary (session 1) were Sefwyn's own.
- That Sefwyn has already been quietly tracking the party since the ossuary job.

**Commitments made**:
- Sefwyn will arrange contact with Magus Orell Tain within a few days and knows how to find the party again.
- In exchange, the party showed (did not surrender) **only the first (ossuary) fragment** — the one Sefwyn already knew about from tracking the party to the ossuary door. Sefwyn has no knowledge the party later acquired a second fragment (session 3, the convoy ambush) — it was never mentioned, and Sefwyn never asked. This side of the deal is already fulfilled.

**Next beat**:
Sefwyn will reach out again once the Tain introduction is arranged — expect this to surface as a future session's hook rather than something the party needs to chase down themselves.
