# Session 9 — Conversation Log

**Campaign**: Ashen Crown
**Date**: 2026-08-30
**Starting Location**: Ashgate Conservatory, Harrowgate — day 51

---

## Scene 1 — Departure and the road to the Hold

Sister Mave's directions in hand, the party left Harrowgate for Shestendeliath Hold, eastern Thornlands. A lone Specter rose from an unmarked wayside grave en route — destroyed round 1 (Scorching Ray, then Kriv's Barrow-King's Blade, including its vs-undead bonus). 100 XP each. Arrived at the Hold at dusk.

## Scene 2 — The garrison and the gate

Ilvaneth's Investigation confirmed the main gate was opened from inside, not forced — this was betrayal, not a siege. Inside, four bound retainer wights stood at the gate hall, led by **Ser Kethrax**, captain of the gate guard. Kethrax recognized Kriv via blood/oath resonance and knelt without hesitation — fifteen years of duty, finally answered.

Kethrax's account: on duty the night the house fell, fighting elsewhere when the gate actually opened, never saw who did it. Led the party to the muniment room.

## Scene 3 — The muniment room

**The gate ledger**: the missing name from the second watch shift is **Vharkoss** — Kriv's uncle. Ilvaneth's Investigation (24) found the watch-night page had been physically replaced afterward — different paper, re-stitched. Someone tampered with the record after the fall.

**On the parents, from Kethrax**: Lord Threxis died fighting, sword in hand. Lady Isvara died trying to reach the library, likely searching for her children.

**The sister**: Kriv had an older sister, **Sethra**, six or seven years his senior. Kethrax never saw her die or escape — "always had hope." Family records showed every other death closed with a uniform "died that night" entry, in the same hand. Sethra's was never closed.

**The Charge-Roll**, read by Kriv (Draconic): House Shestendeliath was charged with the Ember Ward fragment — the party's own first fragment, from the Gallowmere ossuary. A second legible entry named **House Doskarn**, charged with a fragment beneath Karsgate's old ash-mint, in the undercity. The rest of the Roll was water-damaged, illegible.

## Scene 4 — The oath-hall

Kriv read the Warden's Charge, two layers: an outer formal band (*"Let no Warden of this house permit two pieces of the Ash-throne to touch while blood of this line still walks..."*) and a deeper, archaic inner band (*"...what sleeps in the blood wakes only for true cause..."*) that resonated with his own recent symptoms — running warm, drawn to fire — without explanation. Took **Wardensteel** from the kneeling effigy beneath the carving.

## Scene 5 — The vault

Found the **Warden's Oathplate** (Dwarven Plate, AC 20, no attunement needed) — Kriv put it on immediately, replacing his Mithral Chain Mail. ~800 gp in unsold plate, silver, and ceremonial pieces. A cedar box held Lady Isvara's wedding ring and a hand-carved wooden dragon toy. Kriv gave the ring directly to Ilvaneth — a real bond moment, not loot.

## Scene 6 — The great hall (combat)

Kethrax stopped at the threshold — bound to the gate, not this room. Inside: three wraiths, unmodified SRD stats (67 HP each — the final version after several design iterations that session settled "more enemies, not more HP" as the standing default for this kind of fight). A real, several-round fight; both PCs spent real resources. Ilvaneth took no damage in the canon version. 2,700 XP each.

## Scene 7 — The lower cistern (combat)

A short rest, then descent to the flooded lower cistern: a gibbering mouther, base SRD stats (its HP checked against the party's actual round-1 burst for the first time via a new script, before the fight). Killed without incident. 225 XP each. A search of the cistern floor turned up 55 gp and a Potion of Healing on a long-dead looter's body.

## Scene 8 — The morning after: the restoration plan

Kriv gathered the garrison in the courtyard the next morning and formally declared House Shestendeliath would rise again. Kethrax, visibly moved beneath his soldier's formality, pledged the garrison's service. Together they worked out a concrete three-phase restoration plan: physical repairs (the burned great-hall roof), living staff and lands (a newly-named nearby hamlet, **Greyholt**, whose "the hold doesn't like visitors" reputation may or may not still hold old tenant families), and legal recognition (tied to Magus Orell Tain's ongoing attainder research). The garrison began clearing debris as the session paused.
