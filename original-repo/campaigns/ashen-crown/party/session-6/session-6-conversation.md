## Kargrim's Reach — finishing the delve

Session opened mid-dungeon at Room 13 (The Sealed Refuge), day 35, having just taken a short rest after clearing Groth's Hall. The party opened the Sealed Vault (room 11) using the motto found earlier ("Stone holds; iron answers") — no roll needed. Inside: 220 gp, ~100 gp worked silver, and a +1 Dagger with dwarven runes (folk-ward vs. goblinoids). A follow-up Investigation search (nat-adjacent 22) found a hidden plaque naming **Yüzbaşı Aldrek Kargrim** — confirming Kargrim was the dwarven garrison captain who deliberately left this vault's contents behind, tying back to the map note found at the Widow's Hollow.

Descended the Shaft to the flooded depths (room 20). A stealth approach (Ilvaneth 22, Kriv 19 vs. the basilisk's passive Perception 9) went undetected, allowing careful positioning before attacking. Combat: the basilisk never got a turn — Kriv's Goading Attack (advantage, hit) plus an Action Surge follow-up dropped it to 17 HP in round 1, and Ilvaneth's Scorching Ray (three hits, one a natural 20) finished it in round 2. Loot: 350 gp, raw silver ore, a Periapt of Wound Closure, and (after a successful Survival check) the basilisk's harvested eyes and fangs. A third short rest was taken at Room 13; Ilvaneth claimed the vault's +1 Dagger, Kriv attuned the Periapt of Wound Closure (3rd attunement slot).

Descended again toward the mine level. The Warren Entrance (room 15, 2 kobold sentries) was cleared with a fully successful stealth ambush — both killed before either could act or raise an alarm (one to a critical Fire Bolt). The party then chose to attack Skree's Warren (room 16, Skree + 4 kobolds) rather than pursue the negotiation the site was designed to offer — an upcast Burning Hands and a Thunderwave wiped the room in two rounds, no PC damage taken. Loot: minor coin, a citrine gem, and — found on Skree's person — a hand-drawn diagram of the kobolds' cistern sabotage (room 7), letting the party confirm that room's secret without a check. Cleared the Rigged Gallery (room 17, both dart traps spotted and avoided) and the Flooded Gallery (room 18, gas hazard identified and avoided, no further content). Chose not to touch the cistern's control lever (room 7) — both rival factions being dead made it moot for now. Exited via the Main Gate (room 1). **Kargrim's Reach is now fully cleared** — both resident factions (Groth's bugbear warband, session 5; Skree's kobold clan, this session) eliminated, the basilisk dead, the dungeon sitting empty.

## Harrowgate — business and a long-overdue reunion

Two nights' travel (Widow's Hollow route) brought the party to Harrowgate, day 37 — a second toll gang (unrelated to the Sorrel's Hollow group wiped out in session 2) was scattered by Kriv's Intimidation without a fight.

In town: sold Kargrim's Reach loot to **Corvin Thale** (Groth's morningstar + corroded dwarven weapons, 140 gp; he personally bought the basilisk eyes/fangs for his own collection, 180 gp) and to a new NPC, **Borgha Kresk** at Ironmonger's Row (worked silver, raw silver ore, bugbear gear, haggled to 145 gp). Ilvaneth visited **Vestry & Cole** (a new NPC, apothecary Cole) and copied the Identify ritual into her spellbook (50 gp/2 hrs); Cole also mentioned a standing want for fresh harpy feathers from the Harpy Crags. Ilvaneth then fully examined Harn's Spellbook for the first time, copying four more Necromancy spells (False Life, Ray of Enfeeblement, Blindness/Deafness, Animate Dead) and the previously-uncopied Mirror Image scroll into her own book (300 gp/12 hrs total).

**Sefwyn Marrow's long-overdue re-contact fired** at The Hanged Man — the promised Orell Tain introduction, twelve days overdue (see `bug-log.md`), was finally delivered: a meeting confirmed for ~day 42, sundown, at an old chapel on the Karsgate road called Millward Crossing.

## The road to Millward Crossing

Departed Harrowgate the same day. Over 5 nights of travel (day 37→42):
- **Night 1**: quiet.
- **Night 2**: a Concordat convoy (6 skeletons, 2 priests, a warded wagon) was ambushed and wiped out entirely via a stealthy alpha strike (upcast Burning Hands, then melee) — no survivors, no alarm. A **third Ashen Crown fragment** was recovered from the coffin (Arcana 24, no alarm triggered). Ilvaneth forged evidence framing the **Ironclad Compact** for the attack (Forgery 26), planting it among the bodies before they were burned — a deliberate attempt to open a wedge between the Concordat and the Compact. Both PCs reached **level 5** after the following long rest (Extra Attack for Kriv; 3rd-level slots, Fireball and Haste for Ilvaneth, both added free at level-up).
- **Night 3**: a Concordat investigation party (searching for the two missing shipments) was observed from hiding and avoided — no contact.
- **Night 4**: a second toll gang scattered by Intimidation, no fight.
- **Night 5** (the corrected fifth night — the original "4-5 day" travel estimate was initially resolved too short, then fixed once the discrepancy was caught): risen skeletons from an unmarked roadside grave, wiped out in a single round by Ilvaneth's first-ever cast of Fireball.

Arrived at Millward Crossing the same day as the meeting (day 42), cutting it close as the "4-5 day" estimate and the party's rushed departure had implied. Disguised for the meeting: Ilvaneth cast Disguise Self, appearing as a human in her 40s with a military bearing; Kriv stayed undisguised but planned to present himself as "one of Kriv's men" rather than confirm his own identity outright.

**The actual meeting with Orell Tain had not yet taken place when the session paused** — he and one aide were seen approaching the chapel at sundown, but no dialogue was exchanged. Any earlier version of this conversation played out at the table was explicitly retconned and did not happen in-fiction; the scene resets to the moment of his arrival, unresolved, for whenever play resumes.
