# Ashen Crown — Campaign Primer

## The World

The Hollow Kingdoms have no emperor. Ashkar Vaelthorn is dead — no heir, no clear succession — and the realm is quietly tearing itself apart while it pretends otherwise. Three powers circle the throne:

- **The Ashlord Concordat** — a magocracy of necromancers and archivists who claim only ritual and ash-lineage can grant legitimate rule.
- **The Ironclad Compact** — mercenary trade houses with no loyalty but coin, selling armies to whoever pays.
- **The Cinder Choir** — a death-cult who believe the true ruler must have died and come back.

None of them are heroes. None of them are asking your permission.

## Where You Start

**Gallowmere** — a border town living off refugees, smugglers, and everyone the capital doesn't want to see. It's the kind of place where a job is a job, and nobody asks where the coin came from.

## Tone

Dark fantasy. You are not saviors. You are ambitious, capable, and willing to do what it takes to get what you want — power, wealth, standing, survival. That doesn't mean cruelty for its own sake; it means the ends justify the means, and mercy is a tool, not a virtue. Expect moral compromise, betrayal, and hard choices with no clean answers.

## Tier Structure

- **Chapter 1 (levels 1–10)**: Carve out a foothold. Local rivals, local stakes, local power.
- **Chapter 2 (levels 11–20+)**: The stakes go empire-wide. What you built in Chapter 1 decides what you're fighting for in Chapter 2.

## Session Zero Notes

Before the first session, the table should agree on:
- **Content lines and veils** — what's off the table, what fades to black.
- **Character connections** — why are you two already known to each other? Partners, rivals-turned-allies, something owed?
- **Goals** — what does each character actually want, long-term? (Power, revenge, legacy, escape, something else.)

Everything else, you'll find out at the table.
